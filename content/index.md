*"When the Covenant shattered, creation did not break."*

## Welcome

  

The Shattered Covenant is a high-fantasy setting centered upon the aftermath of a cosmic fracture that forever changed the structure of creation.

  

Ancient powers contend for influence across existence.

  

Dragonkind remembers the First Age.

  

The Thrones maintain celestial order.

  

The Primordials stir beneath reality.

  

And mortals struggle to determine what the future of creation will become.

  

This archive serves as the official lore compendium of the setting.
