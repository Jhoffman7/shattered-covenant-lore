# The Hall of Calling

**Location Type:** Celestial Academy and Evaluation Center

**Maintained By:** [[The Path of Ascension]]

**Associated Throne:** [[Throne of Faith]]

---

## Overview

The Hall of Calling serves as the heart of the [[The Path of Ascension]].

It is here that prospective Saints are educated, evaluated, and guided toward their purpose.

Unlike many institutions within Heaven, the Hall was not built to judge worth.

It was built to discover potential.

Within its chambers, candidates from every corner of creation gather to learn, grow, and better understand themselves.

Many Saints later describe their time within the Hall as the most transformative period of their lives.

---

## Purpose

The Hall exists to answer a single question:

> Where can this soul best serve creation?

Every aspect of the institution is designed around that purpose.

Students receive instruction from:

- Saints
    
- Ascended Saints
    
- Exalted Saints
    
- Representatives of the Twelve Thrones
    
- Scholars and mentors
    

The goal is not specialization.

The goal is understanding.

---

## Architecture

The Hall is often described as simultaneously grand and welcoming.

Its structure combines celestial stone, living gardens, libraries, training grounds, meditation chambers, and countless classrooms.

Many visitors claim the Hall feels strangely familiar regardless of their origin.

Some believe the building subtly adapts itself to those who enter.

No official explanation exists.

---

## The Chamber of Aspirants

The largest gathering space within the Hall.

New candidates are welcomed here upon arrival.

The Chamber serves as a place of orientation, instruction, and community.

Many lifelong friendships begin within its walls.

---

## The Twelve Trial Wings

Radiating outward from the Hall's center are twelve wings.

Each is associated with one of the Twelve Thrones.

These wings host portions of the Aptitude Trials and expose candidates to the values represented by each Throne.

No wing exists to recruit candidates.

Their purpose is discovery.

---

## The Aptitude Trials

The Hall serves as the primary location where the Aptitude Trials occur.

The trials evaluate:

- Character
    
- Conviction
    
- Wisdom
    
- Service
    
- Leadership
    
- Sacrifice
    

Success is not measured by performance.

It is measured by growth.

The Hall seeks to reveal who a candidate is becoming.

---

## The Chamber of Alignment

The most important room within the Hall.

Upon completing the Path, candidates enter the Chamber of Alignment.

Here, representatives from the Twelve Thrones evaluate the candidate's journey and determine where they are most likely to flourish.

The process remains one of the Hall's oldest traditions.

---

## Relationship With The Hall of Exemplars

The Hall of Calling and [[The Hall of Exemplars]] maintain a close relationship.

Candidates frequently study the lives of the Exalted Saints as part of their education.

The Hall teaches that purpose is discovered through example as much as instruction.

---

## Reputation

Throughout Heaven, the Hall of Calling is regarded as a place of beginnings.

For many Saints, it marks the moment their lives truly began.

A common saying among graduates states:

> The Hall did not tell me who I was. It showed me who I could become.

---

## Philosophy

> Potential is a gift. Purpose is a choice.
