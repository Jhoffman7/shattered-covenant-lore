# Exalted Saints

**Classification:** Celestial Honor and Order

**Administered By:** [[Throne of Faith]]

**Recognized By:** [[The Celestial Court]]

---

## Overview

The Exalted Saints are the greatest mortal servants ever recognized by the Heavens.

Though all Exalted Saints were once ordinary Saints, not all Saints become Exalted.

The distinction is extraordinarily rare.

Exaltation is not awarded for power, status, influence, or achievement alone.

It is granted to those whose service fundamentally altered the course of creation.

Individuals whose lives became enduring examples of the virtues the Heavens seek to cultivate.

To be remembered is common.

To be revered is uncommon.

To be Exalted is extraordinary.

---

## Purpose

The Exalted Saints serve as exemplars for both mortals and celestials.

Their lives are studied throughout the Mortal Realm and the Heavens alike.

They represent the highest ideals of service, sacrifice, conviction, wisdom, stewardship, protection, and faith.

The purpose of Exaltation is not to reward greatness.

It is to preserve examples worthy of emulation.

The Celestial Court believes future generations learn best through those who walked the path before them.

---

## The Nature of Exaltation

Exaltation is not a rank.

It is not a title of authority.

It grants no automatic power over others.

Instead, Exaltation is recognition.

A declaration that an individual's life and service embody principles worthy of eternal remembrance.

Many Exalted Saints remain humble mentors.

Others become symbols associated with entire cultures, nations, or eras.

The title carries immense respect.

It carries surprisingly little authority.

---

## Eligibility

No formal application exists.

No Saint may seek Exaltation.

The process begins only after centuries of observation and reflection.

Potential candidates are typically evaluated according to:

- Service to creation
    
- Personal sacrifice
    
- Enduring legacy
    
- Influence upon future generations
    
- Fidelity to purpose
    
- Impact upon history
    
- Integrity of character
    

Many extraordinary Saints are never Exalted.

The Court intentionally reserves the honor for only the most exceptional examples.

---

## The Process of Recognition

Recommendations for Exaltation originate within the [[Throne of Faith]].

From there, a candidate undergoes extensive review by representatives of the Twelve Thrones.

Historical records are examined.

Testimonies are gathered.

Legacies are evaluated.

The candidate's life is considered in its entirety.

If consensus is reached, the matter is presented before the [[The Celestial Court]].

Only the Court may formally recognize an Exalted Saint.

Such ceremonies are exceedingly rare.

Entire centuries may pass without a new Exaltation.

---

## The Hall of Exemplars

At the heart of the Heavens stands a sacred institution known as the Hall of Exemplars.

The Hall preserves the stories, teachings, accomplishments, and sacrifices of every Exalted Saint.

Unlike ordinary archives, the Hall is intended not merely to preserve history.

It exists to inspire.

Pilgrims, Saints, scholars, and even members of the Thrones frequently visit the Hall seeking guidance.

Many describe it as one of the most revered locations within creation.

---

## Duties of the Exalted

Though Exaltation grants no formal authority, many Exalted Saints continue serving the Heavens.

Common responsibilities include:

- Mentoring Saints
    
- Advising the Thrones
    
- Preserving historical wisdom
    
- Teaching future generations
    
- Mediating disputes
    
- Guiding major initiatives
    

Their influence derives from respect rather than office.

Many institutions value their perspectives precisely because they are not bound by bureaucracy.

---

## Relationship With The Path of Ascension

The [[The Path of Ascension]] views the Exalted Saints as living proof of its purpose.

Prospective Saints frequently study the lives of the Exalted.

Not to imitate them.

But to understand what devotion to purpose can achieve.

The Path teaches that no one becomes Exalted by seeking Exaltation.

Every Exalted Saint achieved greatness while pursuing something greater than themselves.

---

## Relationship With The Thrones

Every Exalted Saint maintains ties to one or more Thrones.

However, most eventually transcend narrow institutional identities.

Their wisdom often proves valuable across multiple domains.

As a result, Exalted Saints frequently serve as bridges between institutions.

Many possess perspectives unavailable to those still bound by jurisdictional concerns.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

Among the Exalted Saints exist a small number of individuals who have quietly expressed concern regarding the growing inconsistencies appearing throughout creation.

Several have reported noticing unusual patterns centuries before the issue attracted broader attention.

Others have become increasingly interested in forgotten histories and unexplained gaps within the archives.

Though no official position exists, some observers suspect that the Exalted Saints may recognize aspects of the Covenant Crisis that remain invisible to most institutions.

Whether this is true remains uncertain.

---

## The Twelve Exemplars

Tradition holds that among all Exalted Saints, twelve individuals stand above the rest.

Known collectively as the Twelve Exemplars, these figures are regarded as the greatest Saints in recorded history.

Each is associated with one of the Twelve Thrones.

Their stories are taught throughout the Heavens.

Their legacies continue shaping generations long after their mortal lives ended.

The identities of the Twelve Exemplars are preserved within the Hall of Exemplars.

---

## Reputation

Throughout creation, Exalted Saints are viewed as symbols of what mortals can become when purpose outweighs self-interest.

To some they are heroes.

To others they are legends.

To many they are proof that greatness is measured not by power, but by service.

---

## Philosophy

> The measure of a life is not what it gains, but what it leaves behind.
