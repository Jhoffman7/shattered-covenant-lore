# The Crossroads

**Location Type:** Transitional Nexus

**Maintained By:** [[Throne of Passage]]

**Associated Primordial:** [[Nethrys]]

---

## Overview

The Crossroads is one of the most mysterious locations within the Heavens.

It exists at the symbolic and metaphysical intersection of countless journeys.

Every path eventually leads through it.

Though often associated with death, the Crossroads serves a far broader purpose.

It is a place of transition.

A place where endings and beginnings meet.

A place where travelers pause before continuing onward.

---

## Purpose

The Crossroads serves as the primary nexus through which the [[Throne of Passage]] oversees major transitions throughout creation.

Souls entering the Great Cycle often pass through its pathways.

Saints undertaking significant pilgrimages may visit its halls.

Even members of the Celestial Court occasionally seek its guidance during moments of profound change.

The Crossroads exists to remind all travelers of a simple truth:

> Every journey eventually reaches a threshold.

---

## Appearance

Descriptions of the Crossroads vary dramatically.

Some report endless pathways stretching toward distant horizons.

Others describe quiet lantern-lit roads winding through twilight.

Some claim to have seen forests.

Others cities.

Others oceans.

The location appears differently to different visitors.

Yet every account contains three consistent elements:

- Countless paths
    
- Gentle light
    
- An overwhelming sense of possibility
    

---

## The Lantern Roads

The most famous feature of the Crossroads.

Thousands of illuminated pathways extend outward in every direction.

No map of these roads has ever remained accurate for long.

The Throne of Passage teaches that the roads do not change.

The travelers do.

---

## The House of Lanterns

At the center of the Crossroads stands the House of Lanterns.

This structure serves as headquarters for the Lanterns, one of the principal traditions of the [[Throne of Passage]].

Travelers often seek refuge here when facing uncertainty, grief, fear, or significant change.

The House contains no throne.

No court.

No authority.

Only guidance.

---

## The Hall of Departures

A solemn but peaceful chamber where souls beginning their journey through the Great Cycle are welcomed.

The Hall is not a place of judgment.

It is a place of farewell.

Many Guides and Lanterns serve here, helping travelers prepare for the road ahead.

---

## The Hall of Arrivals

Opposite the Hall of Departures lies the Hall of Arrivals.

This chamber receives those entering new chapters of existence.

Saints beginning service.

Pilgrims completing journeys.

Souls returning from profound transitions.

The Hall serves as a reminder that every ending creates space for a beginning.

---

## Relationship With The Great Cycle

The Crossroads is inseparable from [[The Great Cycle]].

While the Cycle represents the process, the Crossroads represents the place where that process becomes visible.

Many scholars describe the location as the nearest thing creation possesses to a doorway between chapters.

---

## Relationship With Nethrys

The Crossroads is perhaps the location most strongly associated with [[Nethrys]].

Despite this, she is rarely depicted as ruling the location.

Instead, traditions portray her as a traveler among travelers.

A quiet figure carrying a lantern and offering guidance to those who need it.

Many stories claim she still walks the roads.

The Throne neither confirms nor denies such tales.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

Several members of the Throne of Passage have reported unusual changes within portions of the Crossroads.

Roads appearing where none existed before.

Paths behaving unexpectedly.

Travelers reporting unfamiliar destinations.

Though isolated, these incidents have attracted increasing attention.

Some believe the Crossroads may be responding to broader changes occurring throughout creation.

---

## Reputation

Among all celestial locations, few inspire greater contemplation than the Crossroads.

Many arrive seeking certainty.

Most leave with perspective instead.

A common saying among the Lanterns states:

> Every road begins with a step.
> 
> Every step changes the traveler.

---

## Philosophy

> The purpose of a road is not where it begins.
> 
> It is where it leads.
