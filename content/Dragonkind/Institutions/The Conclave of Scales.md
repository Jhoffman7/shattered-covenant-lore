# The Conclave of Scales

## Overview

The Conclave of Scales is the highest institution within Dragon society.

Known among Dragons simply as **The Scalebound**, the Conclave serves as the collective voice of Dragonkind and the guardian of its oldest traditions.

Unlike mortal kingdoms, the Conclave does not govern the daily lives of Dragons.

It does not levy laws.

It does not collect tribute.

It does not rule.

Instead, the Conclave exists to address matters that affect Dragonkind as a whole.

The preservation of the Broodlands.

The protection of the Cradle of Scales.

The succession of Regencies.

The preservation of Dragonkind's future.

These responsibilities belong to the Scalebound.

---

## Purpose

The Conclave exists to fulfill three sacred responsibilities:

### Preservation

To safeguard the Broodlands, the Cradle of Scales, and the future of Dragonkind.

### Succession

To oversee the passage of the Regencies and ensure that worthy successors inherit the ancient mantles.

### Continuity

To preserve the wisdom, traditions, and collective memory of Dragonkind across the ages.

The Conclave does not exist to command.

It exists to endure.

---
## The Twenty-Six Seats

The Scalebound is composed of twenty-six permanent seats.

### The Thirteen Regents

The bearers of the Thirteen Regencies.

Each Regent serves as a steward of one of Dragonkind's oldest responsibilities.

Through their mantles they preserve continuity, wisdom, and the duties entrusted to Dragonkind since the dawn of creation.

### The Thirteen First Scales

The eldest representatives of Dragonkind's oldest and most influential Broods.

Each First Scale speaks for their lineage and carries the responsibility of representing the interests of their Brood.

Together, the Regents and First Scales form the complete Conclave.

Neither group outranks the other.

Both are necessary.

---

## Regents and First Scales

Among Dragonkind, it is often said:

> "The Regents speak for what creation means.
>
> The First Scales speak for those who must live within it."

This balance lies at the heart of the Conclave.

Regents represent ideals.

First Scales represent experience.

Regents preserve philosophy.

First Scales preserve lineage.

Neither perspective is considered complete without the other.

For this reason, major decisions traditionally require support from both groups before action is taken.

---

## The First Scales

A First Scale is not a ruler.

The title carries neither ownership nor dominion over a Brood.

Instead, a First Scale is the most respected representative of a lineage.

The title may be held by any Dragon regardless of sex.

What matters is wisdom, reputation, and the trust of the Brood.

Many First Scales are ancient Dragons who have guided their Broods for centuries or even millennia.

---

## Selection of a First Scale

Unlike the Regencies, the title of First Scale is not a mantle.

Each Brood determines its own method of succession.

These traditions vary greatly across Dragonkind.

Some Broods elevate the eldest living Dragon.

Others choose through consensus.

Some require ritual trials.

Others seek guidance from the Echoes of Scale.

Because each Brood follows its own traditions, no two successions are exactly alike.

This diversity is viewed as a strength rather than a weakness.

The Conclave recognizes every legitimate First Scale regardless of how their Brood selected them.

---

## The Hall of Scales

The Conclave gathers within the Hall of Scales, an ancient meeting place located deep within the Broodlands.

The Hall predates many mortal civilizations and is regarded as one of the most sacred places in Dragon society.

Its walls bear the marks of countless ages.

Its chambers have witnessed the rise and fall of kingdoms, the succession of Regents, and the debates that shaped Dragon history.

Many Dragons believe the Hall itself remembers those who have spoken within it.

Whether this belief is symbolic or literal remains a matter of debate.

---

## Decision Making

The Scalebound rarely acts quickly.

Many matters are debated for years, decades, or even centuries.

To outsiders this appears inefficient.

To Dragons it is wisdom.

A decision that affects future generations deserves careful consideration.

The purpose of the Conclave is not speed.

It is permanence.

When consensus cannot be reached, discussion continues until a path forward becomes clear.

For this reason, the Conclave is often described as the slowest institution in existence.

Its defenders argue that it is also among the most enduring.

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

The Conclave does not control the Regencies.

Nor do the Regencies control the Conclave.

The two exist in partnership.

When a Regency becomes vacant, the Conclave gathers to determine which Dragon is most worthy to receive the mantle.

Ultimately, however, the decision belongs to the Regency itself.

The mantle must accept its bearer.

No Dragon may claim a Regency through force, inheritance, or political influence.

---
## Relationship to the Crucible of Creation

The Conclave exists to uphold the principles of the [[Crucible of Creation]].

Its purpose is not to impose order upon creation.

Its purpose is to ensure that Dragonkind remains a faithful steward of it.

Many Dragons view the Scalebound as the institutional expression of the Crucible itself.

Not because it commands Dragonkind.

Because it reminds Dragonkind of its responsibilities.

---

## Legacy

The Conclave of Scales has endured since the earliest ages of creation.

Empires have risen and fallen.

Gods have come and gone.

Civilizations have vanished into memory.

Yet the Scalebound remains.

As long as Dragons endure, the Conclave will continue its vigil.

Protecting the Broodlands.

Guiding the Regencies.

Preserving the future.

And ensuring that the voice of Dragonkind is never lost.

---
