# Dragonborn

## Overview

Dragonborn are the mortal descendants of Dragonkind.

Though they lack the immense power, longevity, and presence of true Dragons, they carry within them fragments of draconic heritage that stretch back to the dawn of creation itself.

Across the ages, Dragonborn have established kingdoms, cities, traditions, and cultures throughout the Mortal Realm.

While Dragons remain closely tied to the Elder Broods and the ancient responsibilities of Dragonkind, Dragonborn have become one of the most widespread peoples in creation.

To many Dragons, Dragonborn represent the continuation of draconic legacy beyond the Broodlands.

---

## Origins

The exact origins of Dragonborn remain the subject of debate among scholars.

According to Dragon traditions, Dragonborn emerged gradually as Dragonkind spread throughout creation and left an enduring mark upon the world.

Though not Dragons themselves, Dragonborn inherited fragments of draconic lineage that manifested in mortal form.

For this reason, many Dragons consider Dragonborn to be distant descendants of the Elder Broods.

Others describe them as creation's mortal reflection of Dragonkind.

Whatever their ultimate origin, Dragonborn have existed for nearly as long as recorded history.

---

## Relationship to Dragonkind

Dragonborn occupy a unique position within Dragon society.

They are not considered Dragons.

Yet neither are they viewed as entirely separate from Dragonkind.

Most Dragons regard Dragonborn as younger kin.

Though differences in lifespan, power, and perspective often create distance between the two peoples, many Dragons feel a sense of responsibility toward Dragonborn and take pride in their accomplishments.

For this reason, Dragonborn are often welcomed within Dragon communities in ways that few other peoples are.

---

## Differing Perspectives

Not all Dragons view Dragonborn in the same way.

Most regard them with affection, respect, or familial pride.

Others adopt a more paternal attitude, viewing Dragonborn as younger relatives who require guidance and protection.

A minority hold more dismissive views.

These Dragons believe Dragonborn are lesser reflections of Dragonkind and lack the qualities that make Dragons exceptional.

Such attitudes are often criticized by other Dragons, though they remain present within some lineages and traditions.

As with any civilization, Dragonkind is not entirely united in its opinions.

---

## Brood Heritage

Nearly all Dragonborn trace their ancestry to one of the Elder Broods.

This heritage often influences both appearance and culture.

An Ashwing Dragonborn may possess dark scales, ember-like eyes, and traditions centered on renewal and perseverance.

A Winterborn Dragonborn may display pale coloration, sturdy builds, and values emphasizing patience and endurance.

A Starbound Dragonborn may inherit traditions focused on exploration, curiosity, and discovery.

Though generations of distance often dilute direct lineage, brood ancestry remains an important part of Dragonborn identity.

Many Dragonborn take great pride in knowing which Elder Brood their family descends from.

---

## Place in the Mortal Realm

Unlike Dragons, who remain closely connected to the Broodlands and their ancient responsibilities, Dragonborn can be found throughout nearly every region of the Mortal Realm.

They serve as merchants, scholars, rulers, artisans, explorers, soldiers, and adventurers.

Many Dragonborn cultures preserve fragments of ancient draconic traditions while adapting them to mortal life.

Because of this, Dragonborn often serve as a bridge between Dragonkind and the wider world.

Many Dragonborn who leave the Broodlands do so in service to both Dragonkind and the wider world.

These individuals often act as delegates, advisors, diplomats, merchants, scholars, and explorers throughout the Mortal Realm.

While Dragons themselves rarely involve themselves directly in the day-to-day affairs of shorter-lived civilizations, Dragonborn possess a unique ability to understand both perspectives.

For this reason, Dragonborn have long served as bridges between Dragonkind and other peoples.

Many Dragons view this role with great respect, believing that Dragonborn help nurture the growth of creation in ways that Dragons themselves often cannot.

---

## Relationship with the Broods

The relationship between Dragonborn and the Elder Broods varies considerably.

Some Broods maintain strong ties with their descendants and actively support Dragonborn communities.

Others interact only rarely.

Many Dragonborn never meet a true Dragon during their lifetime.

Yet even among those separated by distance, a shared sense of ancestry often remains.

To many Dragonborn, the Elder Broods are both family and legend.

---

## Legacy

Dragonborn are among Dragonkind's greatest legacies.

Though mortal, they carry fragments of draconic heritage into every corner of creation.

They preserve traditions.

Build civilizations.

Forge new paths.

And ensure that the influence of Dragonkind extends far beyond the Broodlands.

Where Dragons embody the enduring foundations of creation, Dragonborn embody its capacity to grow, adapt, and flourish.

---

## Common Saying

> "A Dragon shapes the age.
>
> A Dragonborn shapes the world."
