# The Sundering Dawn

## Overview

The Sundering Dawn was the First Recorded Near-Cataclysm.

It occurred during the earliest age of creation, shortly after the emergence of the First Regents and before the formation of the Elder Broods, the Conclave of Scales, or the formal institutions of Dragonkind.

Unlike later Near-Cataclysms, the Sundering Dawn was not caused by a foreign force, a plague, or the weakening of a single Reality Anchor.

It was caused by the First Regents themselves.

Newly emerged into a newborn reality, the First Regents possessed immense power but little understanding of their purpose. Each embodied a fundamental manifestation of creation, yet none fully understood how deeply they were connected to the world around them.

Their confusion eventually became conflict.

Their conflict became war.

And their war nearly unmade creation before it had fully learned how to endure.

---

## The Age Before Wisdom

The First Regents emerged into a reality still young, unstable, and unfinished.

There were no ancient traditions to guide them.

No Conclave to mediate between them.

No Echoes to preserve inherited wisdom.

No Crucible of Creation to teach restraint, balance, or stewardship.

There were only thirteen beings of immense power, each newly awakened to existence and each bearing within themselves a force creation could not survive without.

The First Regents understood that they mattered.

They did not yet understand why.

---

## The First Conflict

Conflict among the First Regents did not begin from malice.

It began from misunderstanding.

Each Regent perceived creation through the lens of the force they embodied.

The Foundation sought stability.

The Tempest sought motion.

The Dawnbringer sought radiance.

The Veil sought hidden truth.

The Reforger sought transformation.

Each perspective contained wisdom.

None contained the whole.

Without shared understanding, disagreement escalated.

Disagreement became rivalry.

Rivalry became violence.

The first war in Dragon history began before Dragonkind itself fully existed.

---

## Reality Begins to Break

As the First Regents clashed, creation itself began to suffer.

Their powers were not separate from reality.

They were woven into it.

When the Regents fought, the systems they embodied strained beneath the force of their conflict.

Mountains fractured before their foundations had fully settled.

Storms moved without pattern.

Light burned where no dawn had risen.

Shadows swallowed places that had barely come into being.

Cycles faltered.

Paths vanished.

The newborn world trembled beneath the weight of beings who did not yet understand the consequences of their own strength.

Had the conflict continued, Dragon records suggest creation may have collapsed before its earliest age had truly begun.

---

## The Foundation's Realization

According to the oldest preserved traditions, the first to understand the danger was the one later remembered as the Foundation.

Where others saw conflict, the Foundation perceived collapse.

The world itself was not merely being damaged.

It was becoming unstable.

The First Regents were not simply fighting within creation.

They were injuring the systems that allowed creation to exist.

This realization changed the course of the conflict.

The Foundation recognized that victory was meaningless if the world could not survive the victor.

---

## The End of the Sundering

The Sundering Dawn did not end through conquest.

No Regent defeated the others.

No single force imposed order upon creation.

Instead, the conflict ended when the First Regents understood the truth of their existence:

They were not masters of creation.

They were bound to it.

If one of them sought dominion over the others, creation would suffer.

If one of them destroyed another, reality itself might lose something it could never replace.

The First Regents ceased their war not because they lacked the power to continue, but because they finally understood what continued conflict would cost.

---

## The Birth of Stewardship

The Sundering Dawn became the first great lesson of Dragonkind.

From it emerged the earliest principles that would eventually shape the Crucible of Creation, the Regencies, and the sacred responsibilities of Dragonkind.

The First Regents learned that power without understanding invites ruin.

They learned that creation could not be possessed.

It could only be stewarded.

This realization marked the beginning of Dragonkind's oldest philosophy:

Strength exists to preserve, not to dominate.

Wisdom must guide power.

Creation belongs to itself.

---

## Legacy

The Sundering Dawn is remembered by very few.

Most Dragons know nothing of it.

Even among those aware of the Near-Cataclysms, the details of the First Recorded Near-Cataclysm remain fragmentary and deeply guarded.

Yet its legacy touches every part of Dragon civilization.

The fear of dominion.

The reverence for stewardship.

The separation between authority and responsibility.

The belief that no single force should rule creation alone.

All of these trace their earliest roots to the Sundering Dawn.

The First Regents nearly destroyed creation before they understood their purpose.

Then they chose to preserve it.

In that choice, Dragonkind began.

---

>
