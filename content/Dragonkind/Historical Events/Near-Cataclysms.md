# Near-Cataclysms

## Overview

Throughout the history of creation, a small number of events have threatened the stability of reality itself.

Among Dragonkind, these crises are collectively known as Near-Cataclysms.

A Near-Cataclysm is not a specific event.

It is a classification applied to any incident that comes dangerously close to triggering a true Cataclysm.

Such events are exceedingly rare.

Even among Dragons, knowledge of their existence is restricted to a select few entrusted with safeguarding creation.

---

## Definition

A Near-Cataclysm occurs when circumstances threaten the stability of one or more Reality Anchors to such a degree that the risk of widespread systemic failure becomes possible.

Though the precise conditions vary from event to event, every Near-Cataclysm shares a common characteristic:

Reality itself was placed at risk.

In each recorded case, catastrophe was ultimately prevented.

Had these events not been contained, the consequences might have extended far beyond individual civilizations, regions, or worlds.

For this reason, Near-Cataclysms occupy a unique place within Dragon history.

They are remembered not for what occurred.

They are remembered for what almost occurred.

---

## The Secret Classification

The existence of Near-Cataclysms is among the most closely guarded categories of knowledge maintained by Dragonkind.

Most Dragons never learn of them.

Many Regents possess only partial information.

Complete records are preserved through the Conclave of Scales and select custodians entrusted with the preservation of creation.

This secrecy exists for a simple reason:

The knowledge itself can be dangerous.

Understanding how reality nearly failed often reveals truths concerning the Reality Anchors, the Cataclysm, and other matters whose misuse could threaten creation anew.

---
## Shared Characteristics

Though each Near-Cataclysm manifested differently, Dragon scholars have identified several recurring patterns.

Rather than threatening individual civilizations alone, Near-Cataclysms appear to endanger fundamental principles necessary for the continued stability of creation.

The Sundering Dawn threatened stewardship and stability.

The Scourge of Ash threatened renewal.

The Silence Below threatened memory and continuity.

Other Near-Cataclysms appear to follow similar patterns, though their details remain restricted.

For this reason, Near-Cataclysms are often viewed not as conventional disasters, but as failures or destabilizations of reality's underlying systems.

While the precise relationship between these crises and the Reality Anchors remains uncertain, many Dragons believe the two are deeply connected.

---
## Recorded Near-Cataclysms

Dragon records recognize five confirmed Near-Cataclysms.

Not all details remain available, and some records have been lost, restricted, or intentionally sealed.

The five known incidents are:

### The Sundering Dawn

The first confirmed Near-Cataclysm and the oldest reality-threatening event preserved within Dragon records.

Unlike later Near-Cataclysms, the Sundering Dawn was not caused by an external force or foreign intrusion.

It was caused by the First Regents themselves.

Shortly after their emergence, the First Regents entered into a period of escalating conflict driven by differing understandings of their purpose within creation.

As their struggles intensified, reality itself began destabilizing beneath the weight of their power.

The crisis ultimately ended when the First Regents recognized that they were not separate from creation, but intrinsically connected to it.

Many Dragons regard the Sundering Dawn as the event that gave rise to the stewardship philosophy that would later define Dragon civilization.

For more information, see [[The Sundering Dawn]].

### The Scourge of Ash

One of the most extensively documented Near-Cataclysms.

Though much of the event remains classified, surviving records describe a crisis whose consequences threatened far more than a single Brood or region.

The Scourge of Ash remains one of the most studied incidents in Dragon history.

For more information, see [[The Scourge of Ash]].

### The Third Recorded Near-Cataclysm

The details of this incident remain restricted.

Even among the Conclave, only limited records are available.

Its existence is acknowledged.

Its history remains obscured.

### The Silence Below

One of the most mysterious Near-Cataclysms ever recorded.

What began as an isolated anomaly eventually grew into a threat significant enough to warrant intervention from some of Dragonkind's greatest stewards.

Many questions surrounding the Silence Below remain unanswered.

For more information, see [[The Silence Below]].

### The Fifth Recorded Near-Cataclysm

The most recent confirmed Near-Cataclysm.

Records concerning this event remain heavily restricted.

Even many Regents possess only fragmentary knowledge of what occurred.

The reasons for this secrecy remain a matter of speculation.

---

## Why They Matter

Near-Cataclysms serve as reminders that creation is neither permanent nor invulnerable.

Even the systems established during the dawn of existence require vigilance, stewardship, and sacrifice to preserve.

For Dragonkind, these events reinforce one of the central teachings of the Crucible of Creation:

Creation endures only so long as its guardians remain willing to protect it.

Many Dragons view the prevention of future Near-Cataclysms as one of the highest responsibilities entrusted to the Conclave and the Regents.

---

## Relationship to the Cataclysm

Every recorded Near-Cataclysm is studied through the lens of a greater fear:

The Cataclysm.

Though no true Cataclysm has ever occurred, Near-Cataclysms provide the closest examples of what such a disaster might resemble.

Because of this, each incident continues to be analyzed, preserved, and monitored by those responsible for safeguarding creation.

The lessons learned from past Near-Cataclysms remain among the most valuable resources available to Dragonkind.

---

## Legacy

Most peoples of the Mortal Realm have never heard the term Near-Cataclysm.

Many Dragons remain unaware of their existence.

Yet hidden beneath the visible history of creation lies another history entirely.

A history of crises narrowly averted.

Of sacrifices forgotten.

Of guardians who prevented disasters that most would never know had been possible.

The continued existence of creation may owe more to these unseen victories than anyone realizes.
