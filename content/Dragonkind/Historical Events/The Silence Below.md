# The Silence Below

## Overview

The Silence Below was the Fourth Recorded Near-Cataclysm and remains one of the most unsettling crises ever documented by Dragonkind.

Unlike most threats to creation, the Silence Below did not destroy cities, civilizations, or peoples.

It erased them.

What began as isolated historical inconsistencies eventually revealed the presence of a foreign entity capable of consuming memory, meaning, and continuity itself.

Had the crisis continued unchecked, Dragon scholars believe creation may have gradually forgotten portions of its own existence.

Though the entity responsible was ultimately destroyed, the true extent of what was lost can never be known.

---

## The First Signs

The earliest indications appeared as seemingly harmless anomalies.

Ancient maps no longer matched historical records.

Scholars discovered references to places whose names could no longer be recalled.

Libraries contained citations to texts that did not exist.

Travelers described ruins whose histories had vanished entirely.

At first these inconsistencies were dismissed as ordinary errors.

Only later did investigators recognize a disturbing pattern.

Something was missing.

And no one could determine what.

---

## The Forgotten Things

As the phenomenon spread, the losses became increasingly severe.

Entire settlements disappeared from historical records.

Languages became fragmented.

Events remembered by some communities could not be verified by others.

Individuals occasionally reported vague memories of people, places, or ideas that no longer seemed to exist.

The most alarming aspect of the crisis was that the victims rarely appeared destroyed.

Instead, they appeared forgotten.

Even when evidence of their existence remained, the meaning attached to that evidence slowly vanished.

What remained was an absence.

A silence where memory should have been.

---

## Discovery of the Intrusion

The Deepscales were among the first to suspect that the phenomenon was not natural.

What others viewed as historical inconsistencies, they recognized as signs of a deeper threat.

Their investigations eventually revealed evidence of a foreign presence operating beneath the surface of reality.

Records describe the entity only in vague terms.

Some accounts refer to it as a consuming intelligence.

Others describe it as a living absence.

No surviving record preserves its true nature.

Only its effects.

The entity appeared capable of feeding upon memory, continuity, and meaning itself.

Where it passed, history unraveled.

---

## The Silence Below

The crisis became known as the Silence Below after an early Deepscale report.

According to surviving fragments, investigators concluded that the threat did not originate within the visible world.

Instead, it seemed to exist beneath memory itself.

Beneath history.

Beneath understanding.

A place where forgotten things gathered and eventually disappeared.

The report warned:

> "There is something beneath memory.
>
> And it is hungry."

The phrase spread among those investigating the phenomenon and eventually became the accepted name for the crisis.

---

## Escalation

As the entity continued feeding, the effects became increasingly severe.

Entire regions appeared to lose portions of their recorded history.

Ancient treaties vanished from archives.

Important historical events became inconsistent across different sources.

Even the Echoes of Scale began displaying discrepancies.

For Dragonkind, this represented a terrifying development.

The Echoes had long been regarded as one of the most reliable methods of preserving memory across generations.

If even the Echoes could be affected, then no repository of knowledge could be considered entirely safe.

Many historians later identified this moment as the point at which the crisis became a true Near-Cataclysm.

---

## The Response

The Deepscales first recognized the threat, but they did not confront it alone.

As evidence mounted, other Elder Broods became involved.

The Emberveils recognized the danger posed to memory and historical continuity.

The Moonscales identified disruptions to long-established cycles and patterns.

The Starbounds reported regions and landmarks disappearing from charts and navigational records.

Eventually nearly every Elder Brood contributed to the effort.

Though the Conclave of Scales already existed by this period, the exact details of the response remain heavily restricted.

What is known is that Dragonkind committed enormous resources toward locating and eliminating the source of the phenomenon.

---

## The Hunt Beneath

Unlike the Scourge of Ash, the Silence Below could not simply be contained.

The entity responsible had to be found.

Historical records describe a prolonged pursuit through locations that no longer appear on modern maps and across regions whose histories remain incomplete.

Fragments suggest that several Dragons willingly risked the loss of their own memories to track the entity's movements.

The full details of the hunt remain sealed within Conclave archives.

What is known is that Dragonkind eventually cornered the intruding presence and destroyed it before the phenomenon could spread further.

---

## Victory and Uncertainty

The destruction of the entity halted the crisis.

Historical records stabilized.

Memories ceased disappearing.

The spread of the phenomenon ended.

Yet Dragonkind's victory was incomplete.

No one knows how much was lost before the Silence Below was stopped.

Entire civilizations may have vanished from memory.

Languages may have disappeared forever.

Important events may have been erased so thoroughly that no evidence of their existence remains.

The surviving records simply begin after the loss.

For this reason, the Silence Below remains one of the most troubling events in Dragon history.

Dragonkind won.

But no one knows what the price of victory truly was.

---

## Legacy

The Silence Below reinforced one of Dragonkind's oldest beliefs:

Memory is not merely a record of creation.

It is part of creation itself.

The event continues to influence the practices of the Emberveils, the preservation efforts of the Echoes of Scale, and the secrecy surrounding many Conclave archives.

Even today, Dragons occasionally encounter gaps in history that cannot be fully explained.

Most dismiss them as ordinary losses to time.

Others quietly wonder whether some scars left by the Silence Below remain unhealed.

---

> "What is forgotten can be lost forever."
