# The Cataclysm

## Overview

Among the deepest fears preserved within Dragonkind is the possibility known as The Cataclysm.

The Cataclysm is not a prophecy.

It is not a singular event.

It is not a prediction of how the world will end.

The Cataclysm is the name given to the collapse of a Reality Anchor.

Since the dawn of creation, the Original Thirteen Broods have served as the living anchors through which reality maintains its most fundamental physical systems.

As long as the Anchors endure, creation remains stable.

Should one fail, reality itself would begin to unravel.

For this reason, the Cataclysm is regarded as one of the greatest existential threats known to Dragonkind.

Most Dragons know only fragments of this truth.

The full understanding of the Cataclysm is preserved through the Echoes of Scale and safeguarded by the Conclave of Scales.

---

## The Reality Anchors

The Original Thirteen Broods are more than ancient bloodlines.

They are Reality Anchors.

Each Brood embodies and stabilizes a fundamental physical system necessary for creation's continued existence.

The extinction of a Brood would not merely diminish Dragonkind.

It would damage the system that Brood was created to maintain.

Because of this, the preservation of the Thirteen Broods has been considered a sacred responsibility since the age of the First Regents.

To protect a Brood is to protect creation itself.

---

## Collapse and Extinction

Contrary to popular belief, a Cataclysm does not begin when a Brood vanishes.

It begins long before that.

The weakening of a Reality Anchor often manifests gradually.

As a Brood declines, the system it anchors may begin exhibiting instability.

At first these signs are subtle.

Over time they become increasingly severe.

Only when an Anchor reaches a critical state does the possibility of a true Cataclysm emerge.

Because of this, Dragonkind monitors the health of the Broods with extraordinary care.

The loss of even a single lineage may be treated as a matter of grave concern.

---

## Signs of Instability

The symptoms of an Anchor's decline vary depending upon the Brood involved.

Stoneheart instability may manifest through increased seismic activity, collapsing caverns, and fractures within ancient mountain ranges.

Tideborn instability may produce erratic currents, rising seas, and disruptions to long-established weather patterns.

Stormclaw instability may create unnatural storms, chaotic wind systems, and unpredictable atmospheric phenomena.

Winterborn instability may disrupt seasonal cycles, causing winters to linger or fail entirely.

Wildroot instability may weaken ecosystems, disrupt reproduction, and reduce nature's ability to adapt.

Ashwing instability may interfere with geological renewal, volcanic activity, and transformative processes.

Dawnfire instability may affect illumination, radiance, and the flow of life-giving energy throughout creation.

Emberveil instability may result in the gradual loss of records, memories, and continuity across generations.

Ironcrest instability may weaken structures, foundations, and systems built upon order and stability.

Moonscale instability may disrupt natural cycles and recurring celestial rhythms.

Starbound instability may diminish exploration, discovery, and creation's drive toward expansion.

Skyfang instability may affect gravity, altitude, and the delicate balance between ascent and descent.

Deepscale instability may cause shadows to behave unnaturally, hidden places to fail, and boundaries between the known and unknown to weaken.

No Dragon knows with certainty how severe such manifestations could become.

That uncertainty is part of what makes the Cataclysm so feared.

---

## The War of Dominion

The first known Near-Cataclysm occurred during the War of Dominion.

In those early ages, relations between Heaven and Dragonkind were far more hostile than they are today.

Neither civilization fully understood the role of the other.

As the conflict escalated, entire Dragon territories were devastated.

Several Broods suffered catastrophic losses.

In the aftermath, strange anomalies began appearing throughout the Mortal Realm.

Mountain ranges shifted unexpectedly.

Storm systems behaved erratically.

Seasonal patterns drifted from their established rhythms.

Though no Brood was lost, the disturbances were severe enough to alarm both Heaven and Dragonkind.

Many scholars believe these events provided the first evidence that the health of the Broods was directly tied to the stability of creation itself.

The lessons learned during the war would eventually contribute to the agreements that became the Divine Boundary.

---

## The Scourge of Ash

The second known Near-Cataclysm is remembered as the Scourge of Ash.

A mysterious sickness emerged among the Ashwing Brood.

The disease spread rapidly and resisted every known remedy.

Those afflicted did not simply weaken.

Their bodies became trapped between states of transformation.

Molten systems crystallized.

Scales fused together.

Growth ceased.

Entire generations perished.

For the first time in recorded history, members of the Conclave openly discussed the possibility that one of the Original Thirteen Broods might cease to exist.

Though the plague was eventually overcome, Ashwing numbers required centuries to recover.

The scars left by the Scourge remain visible within the Brood's collective memory to this day.

---

## The Silence Below

The third known Near-Cataclysm remains one of Dragonkind's greatest mysteries.

Known as the Silence Below, the event centered upon the Deepscale Brood.

Over the course of several centuries, entire lineages vanished.

No bodies were discovered.

No battles were recorded.

No explanation was ever found.

More disturbing still, portions of the affected Echo lineages disappeared alongside them.

The event produced the first confirmed instances of true Silence.

The Conclave launched investigations that lasted generations.

No definitive answer was ever discovered.

The unanswered questions surrounding the Silence Below continue to trouble Dragonkind even now.

Many believe the event revealed the existence of dangers older and stranger than any Dragon has yet understood.

---

## The Duty of Dragonkind

The existence of the Cataclysm defines one of Dragonkind's most sacred responsibilities.

Dragons do not preserve the Broods merely out of tradition.

They preserve them because creation depends upon them.

This responsibility transcends politics, rivalries, and personal ambition.

Every Regent.

Every First Scale.

Every member of the Conclave ultimately serves the same purpose:

To ensure that the Reality Anchors endure.

For as long as the Anchors remain stable, creation remains whole.

---

## The Hidden Truth

Knowledge of the Cataclysm is not widely shared.

Most Dragons understand only fragments of the truth.

Among mortals, the concept is almost entirely unknown.

The deepest understanding of the Cataclysm is preserved through the Echoes of Scale and safeguarded by the Conclave.

New Regents are gradually introduced to this knowledge as they grow into their responsibilities.

The First Regents believed that fear alone was a poor teacher.

Wisdom must come first.

For this reason, the full truth is entrusted only to those whose duties require it.

---

## The Unanswered Question

No true Cataclysm has ever occurred.

At least none that Dragonkind remembers.

Yet a question preserved within the oldest Echoes continues to haunt the Conclave.

If the Original Thirteen were created to stabilize reality...

What would happen if one truly vanished?

No Dragon has ever wished to discover the answer.
