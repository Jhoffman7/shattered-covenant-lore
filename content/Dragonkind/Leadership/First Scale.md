# First Scale

## Overview

A First Scale is the recognized representative of a Brood's living Dragons.

While Regents serve as stewards of the Regencies and carry the wisdom of the Echoes of Scale, First Scales represent the concerns, traditions, and collective voice of the Broods as they exist today.

Every Elder Brood maintains a First Scale.

Together, the Thirteen First Scales serve alongside the Thirteen Regents within the [[The Conclave of Scales]], forming one of the most important institutions in Dragon society.

Among Dragons, it is often said that Regents speak for the responsibilities of the past, while First Scales speak for the needs of the present.

---

## The Voice of the Living

The primary responsibility of a First Scale is to represent their Brood.

They serve as advocates, diplomats, mediators, and leaders whose duty is to ensure that the perspectives of their fellow Dragons are heard.

A First Scale does not rule their Brood.

Nor do they command unquestioning obedience.

Instead, they serve because their Brood trusts them to speak on its behalf.

For this reason, First Scales are often viewed as the living voice of their people.

Where a Regent embodies a sacred mantle inherited across generations, a First Scale embodies the current spirit of their Brood.

---

## Relationship to the Regents

Though both positions are highly respected, Regents and First Scales fulfill very different roles.

A Regent represents a Regency, its mantle, and the responsibilities entrusted to it since the dawn of creation.

A First Scale represents the living Dragons of their Brood.

This distinction creates an important balance within Dragon society.

The Regents preserve continuity, stewardship, and ancient responsibility.

The First Scales ensure that the concerns of living Dragons remain represented alongside those traditions.

Together they form the foundation of the [[The Conclave of Scales]].

---

## Selection of a First Scale

The selection of a First Scale occurs through a process that combines personal merit, Brood support, and recognition by Dragonkind as a whole.

### Recognition

Before a Dragon may be considered for the position, they must first earn widespread recognition within their Brood.

The path to such recognition varies among the Broods.

Some Dragons earn it through leadership.

Others through wisdom, scholarship, diplomacy, exploration, service, or great accomplishments.

Regardless of the path, a potential First Scale must already possess the respect of their fellow Dragons.

### Selection by the Brood

Once suitable candidates emerge, the Brood itself participates in the selection process.

Through discussion, consensus-building, and formal votes, the Brood identifies the candidates it believes best represent its interests.

The three most widely supported candidates advance to the final stage.

### Confirmation by the Conclave

The final selection is made by a vote of the sitting Regents and First Scales.

This confirmation process ensures that the chosen Dragon possesses not only the support of their own Brood, but also the confidence of Dragonkind's highest institutions.

The candidate who receives the support of the Conclave becomes the next First Scale.

---

## Seat Within the Conclave

Each First Scale occupies one of the twenty-six seats within the [[The Conclave of Scales]].

Together, the Thirteen Regents and Thirteen First Scales form the complete Conclave.

This structure reflects one of Dragonkind's most enduring principles:

Ancient wisdom and living experience must exist in balance.

The Regents provide continuity.

The First Scales provide perspective.

Neither is intended to govern without the other.

---

## Authority and Responsibility

A First Scale possesses significant influence within Dragon society.

Their responsibilities often include:

- Representing their Brood within the Conclave
- Mediating disputes among members of their Brood
- Preserving cultural traditions
- Guiding the long-term direction of their lineage
- Coordinating responses to major challenges facing Dragonkind
- Serving as ambassadors to other Broods and civilizations

Despite this influence, First Scales are expected to lead through respect rather than authority.

A Dragon who loses the trust of their Brood rarely remains effective for long.

---

## Separation of Offices

A Dragon may never simultaneously serve as both a Regent and a First Scale.

The offices are intentionally kept separate to preserve the balance between Regency responsibilities and Brood representation.

If a First Scale is chosen to inherit a Regency, their position as First Scale immediately becomes vacant.

A new selection process is then conducted to choose their successor.

Likewise, a Dragon who once served as a First Scale does not automatically regain that office after leaving a Regency.

Each role must be earned independently.

---

## Legacy

Throughout Dragon history, many First Scales have become legendary figures in their own right.

Some guided their Broods through wars.

Others preserved ancient traditions during periods of upheaval.

Many helped shape the future of Dragonkind through wisdom, diplomacy, and service.

Though Regents often stand at the center of Dragon history, the influence of the First Scales can be found throughout nearly every age.

They are the voices through which Dragonkind speaks to itself.
