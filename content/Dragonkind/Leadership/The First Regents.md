# The First Regents

## Overview

The First Regents were the first Dragons to emerge into creation.

According to the oldest traditions preserved within Dragonkind, they appeared shortly after the birth of reality itself, emerging from the Cradle of Scales as manifestations of creation's response to the newborn cosmos.

Each First Regent embodied a fundamental manifestation of reality and became the original steward of one of the Reality Anchors.

Together they established the Thirteen Elder Broods, becoming both their founders and first ancestors.

Though countless ages have passed since their time, their influence continues to shape Dragonkind through the Regencies, the Elder Broods, the Echoes of Scale, and the traditions they left behind.

---

## The First Emergence

Among the deepest traditions preserved by Dragonkind is the belief that the First Regents emerged directly from the Cradle of Scales during the earliest moments of creation.

Not as hatchlings.

Not as ordinary Dragons.

But as fully formed beings uniquely attuned to the newborn reality around them.

According to these traditions, the Primordials brought creation into existence.

Reality then answered in kind.

The Cradle formed.

The First Regents emerged.

And through them, Dragonkind first entered the world.

Whether this account represents literal history, symbolic truth, or some combination of both remains a matter of debate even among Dragons.

Yet few dispute the profound connection between the First Regents and the origins of Dragon civilization.

---

## The First Thirteen

Dragonkind teaches that thirteen First Regents emerged during the dawn of creation.

Each embodied a fundamental manifestation of reality and became the original steward of a Reality Anchor.

Though later generations would come to understand these responsibilities through the Regencies and the Elder Broods, the First Regents carried them long before such institutions formally existed.

The thirteen are remembered by titles rather than personal names.

Over time, their true names faded into myth, while their epithets endured.

The First Thirteen are remembered as:

- The Reforger
- The Foundation
- The Tempest
- The Dawnbringer
- The Worldroot
- The Confluence
- The Bastion
- The Witness
- The Zenith
- The Preserver
- The Veil
- The Reverie
- The Voyager

Among Dragons, these titles are spoken with profound reverence.

---

## Founders of the Elder Broods

Upon their emergence, each First Regent established one of the Elder Broods.

The Broods therefore trace their origins directly to the First Regents themselves.

Every Ashwing ultimately descends from the lineage established by the Reforger.

Every Stoneheart traces its ancestry to the Foundation.

And so on through all thirteen lineages.

For this reason, the histories of the Elder Broods and the histories of the First Regents are inseparable.

To study one is to study the other.

---
## The Sundering Dawn

The earliest years of the First Regents were not marked by wisdom or unity.

According to the oldest secret traditions preserved by Dragonkind, the First Regents initially struggled to understand both their purpose and their relationship to one another.

Each embodied a fundamental manifestation of creation and perceived reality through a different lens.

What began as disagreement eventually became conflict.

The resulting crisis, later remembered as the Sundering Dawn, became the First Recorded Near-Cataclysm.

As the First Regents fought, creation itself began to fracture beneath the strain of their power.

Only when they recognized that they were not separate from creation, but deeply connected to it, did the conflict finally end.

Many Dragons believe the lessons learned during the Sundering Dawn became the foundation of the stewardship philosophy that would later shape Dragon civilization.

---
## The Original Stewards

Among the secret traditions preserved by Dragonkind, the First Regents are remembered as the original stewards of creation.

They understood truths that would later become known only to Regents, select members of the Conclave, and those entrusted with knowledge of the Reality Anchors.

According to these traditions, the First Regents came to recognize that creation required stewardship rather than dominion.

This understanding was not immediate.

Many Dragon traditions trace the realization to the lessons learned during the Sundering Dawn, when the First Regents witnessed firsthand the consequences of wielding power without wisdom.

Strength alone could not preserve reality.

Wisdom, restraint, balance, and responsibility were equally necessary.

Many of the principles later expressed through the Crucible of Creation are believed to originate from lessons first taught by the First Regents themselves.

---

## Before the Conclave

The First Regents never established a unified government for Dragonkind.

Though they cooperated when necessary and maintained relationships with one another, each primarily guided their own Brood and responsibilities.

The institutions known today did not yet exist.

There was no Conclave.

There were no First Scales.

There was only the stewardship of the First Regents and the lineages they founded.

The Conclave of Scales would not be established until much later during the War of Dominion, when the Elder Broods united in response to challenges that threatened Dragonkind as a whole.

For this reason, the First Regents are remembered not as rulers of Dragonkind, but as the founders of its oldest lineages.

---

## The Echoes of the First Regents

Though the First Regents themselves have long since passed from the world, their influence remains.

The Echoes of Scale preserve fragments of their wisdom, perspectives, and experiences.

These Echoes continue to guide future generations through the Regencies and through carefully maintained chains of memory.

Many Dragons believe that no modern Dragon can truly understand Dragonkind without understanding the legacy left behind by the First Regents.

---

## The Mystery of the Thirteenth

Dragonkind remembers that there were thirteen First Regents.

This belief has persisted across every age and every Brood.

Unlike many traditions concerning the Primordials, no Dragon disputes the existence of the thirteenth Regent.

Every Elder Brood traces its lineage to one of the First Thirteen.

Every Regency traces its heritage to one of their responsibilities.

For this reason, the First Regents serve as one of the strongest pieces of evidence supporting Dragonkind's belief that creation began with thirteen foundational manifestations rather than twelve.

---

## Legacy

The First Regents occupy a unique place within Dragon history.

They were the first Dragons.

The founders of the Elder Broods.

The first stewards of the Reality Anchors.

They were also the participants in the Sundering Dawn, the first great crisis of creation and the event that transformed them from powerful beings into willing stewards.

The ancestors of Dragon civilization itself.

Though ages beyond counting have passed since their emergence, their influence can still be found in every Brood, every Regency, and every Echo that endures into the present day.
