# Hearth

## Overview

Hearth is the Regency of belonging, shelter, kinship, and community.

Where Foundation teaches what allows something to stand, Hearth teaches why it is worth standing for.

Among Dragonkind, Hearth is regarded as the guardian of bonds—the force that transforms a collection of individuals into a family, a Brood, or a people.

The warmth of a hearth does not come from the fire alone.

It comes from those gathered around it.

For this reason, Hearth is often viewed as the emotional heart of Dragon society.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Hearth embodies:

- Belonging
- Family
- Shelter
- Community
- Hospitality
- Unity
- Kinship

Its influence can be found wherever individuals gather together in trust and mutual care.

A Dragon returning to their Brood.

A hatchling protected by its elders.

A community rebuilding after hardship.

All are reflections of Hearth.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Hearth emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient draconic traditions describe Hearth as the dragon who first taught the scattered Dragons of the early world that survival alone was not enough.

Dragons needed one another.

Some legends claim the first gathering of Broods occurred beneath Hearth's guidance, while others say the first communal hatcheries were established at their urging.

Whatever the truth, nearly every tradition agrees on one thing:

Without Hearth, Dragonkind may never have become a civilization.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Hearth seeks Dragons who naturally bring others together.

Strength alone is rarely enough.

Many who inherit Hearth are known for wisdom, compassion, patience, and the ability to unite those who disagree.

They are often mediators, caretakers, teachers, and protectors of their people.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "A fire may warm a cave.
>
> A hearth warms a people."

---

## Philosophy

Hearth teaches that no life is truly lived alone.

Every creature depends upon others, whether they acknowledge it or not.

Strength grows when shared.

Knowledge survives when taught.

Communities endure when their members care for one another.

Followers of Hearth believe that belonging is one of creation's greatest gifts.

Not because it removes hardship.

Because it provides the support needed to endure it.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Hearth have often acted as mediators within the Conclave of Scales.

When disputes arise between Broods or Regencies, Hearth frequently seeks common ground before division can take root.

While other Regencies may focus upon change, exploration, or challenge, Hearth concerns itself with what binds Dragonkind together.

For many Dragons, Hearth serves as a reminder that the strength of a people is measured not only by its greatest individuals, but by the bonds they share.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Hearth views creation as a living network of relationships.

Nothing exists entirely alone.

Mountains shape rivers.

Forests shelter life.

Parents guide hatchlings.

Broods preserve memory.

Every part of creation influences every other.

For this reason, Hearth teaches that isolation weakens while connection strengthens.

Creation flourishes most when its many parts support one another.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Hearth include:

- Firelight
- Nests
- Circles
- Shared Meals
- Interwoven Scales
- Hatcheries

Among Dragons, a communal nesting circle is often considered the purest symbol of Hearth.

Not because it provides shelter.

Because it represents a place where every member belongs.
