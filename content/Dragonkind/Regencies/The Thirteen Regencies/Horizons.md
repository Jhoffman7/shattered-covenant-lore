# Horizons

## Overview

Horizons is the Regency of discovery, exploration, aspiration, and the endless pursuit of what lies beyond the known.

Where Crossroads teaches that every future begins with a choice, Horizons teaches that every choice reveals a new horizon waiting to be reached.

The horizon can never truly be touched.

No matter how far one travels, another waits beyond it.

To many, this might seem frustrating.

To Horizons, it is beautiful.

Among Dragonkind, Horizons is regarded as the embodiment of curiosity itself—the desire to seek what has not yet been seen, understood, or imagined.

The unknown is not something to fear.

It is an invitation.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Horizons embodies:

- Discovery
- Exploration
- Curiosity
- Aspiration
- Wonder
- Opportunity
- The Unknown Future

Its influence can be found wherever individuals look beyond what is familiar.

A Dragon flying beyond charted lands.

A scholar pursuing an unanswered question.

A civilization reaching toward a brighter future.

A hatchling dreaming of what they might one day become.

All are reflections of Horizons.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Horizons emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Horizons as endlessly curious and impossible to satisfy.

While other Regents sought to understand the world they had inherited, Horizons constantly searched for what lay beyond it.

Legends claim the First Regent was responsible for some of the earliest explorations of the world, charting unknown regions and venturing into places no Dragon had ever seen.

Some stories suggest Horizons spent so much time exploring that many Dragons believed they had vanished entirely.

Only for them to return centuries later with stories that expanded Dragonkind's understanding of creation itself.

To Horizons, every answer revealed another question.

Every destination revealed another journey.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Horizons seeks Dragons who possess boundless curiosity and a willingness to venture beyond the familiar.

Those chosen are often explorers, adventurers, visionaries, inventors, and seekers of distant possibilities.

Many Regents of Horizons become renowned for discoveries that reshape Dragonkind's understanding of the world.

The mantle values wonder as much as wisdom.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "The horizon exists to be followed."

---

## Philosophy

Horizons teaches that creation is larger than any individual can fully comprehend.

There will always be another mystery.

Another frontier.

Another possibility.

Followers of Horizons believe that curiosity is one of creation's greatest gifts.

Without curiosity, discovery ceases.

Without discovery, growth slows.

Without growth, possibility fades.

To Horizons, the pursuit of understanding is often more important than understanding itself.

The purpose of exploration is not simply to find answers.

It is to expand what can be asked.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Horizons have often served as explorers, innovators, and champions of possibility.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Horizons frequently advocates for bold ideas and the pursuit of opportunities others may overlook.

Its Regents often challenge Dragonkind to think beyond present limitations and imagine what could be achieved in the future.

Many Dragons view Horizons as the Regency most responsible for preventing stagnation and encouraging continual exploration.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Horizons views creation as infinite in possibility.

No matter how much is discovered, more remains.

No matter how far one travels, further distances await.

No matter how much is achieved, greater accomplishments remain possible.

For this reason, Horizons teaches humility alongside ambition.

The purpose of exploration is not conquest.

It is understanding.

Horizons shares strong philosophical ties with Journey, Depths, Growth, and Dawn.

Together, they represent creation's endless capacity for discovery and renewal.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Horizons include:

- Distant Mountains
- Open Skies
- Uncharted Maps
- Rising Suns
- Wings in Flight
- Stars Beyond Sight

Among Dragons, the meeting point between sky and earth is often considered the purest symbol of Horizons.

Not because it can be reached.

Because it always encourages one to continue.

---

## Common Saying

> "Beyond every horizon waits another."
