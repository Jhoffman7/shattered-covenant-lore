# Depths

## Overview

Depths is the Regency of mystery, hidden truths, exploration, and the unknown.

Where Storms teaches that hardship reveals strength, Depths teaches that mystery reveals understanding.

Not all truths dwell beneath the light.

Some rest within forgotten caverns.

Some sleep beneath ancient oceans.

Some remain hidden within the depths of memory itself.

Among Dragonkind, Depths is regarded as the keeper of unanswered questions and the patron of those willing to seek them.

It reminds creation that what is unknown is not something to fear.

It is something to explore.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Depths embodies:

- Mystery
- Discovery
- Hidden Truths
- Exploration
- Curiosity
- Forgotten Knowledge
- The Unknown

Its influence can be found wherever understanding waits beyond certainty.

An unexplored cavern.

A forgotten ruin.

A question without an answer.

A truth buried beneath centuries of assumptions.

All are reflections of Depths.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Depths emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Depths as endlessly curious.

While many of the First Regents sought to shape, protect, or cultivate the newborn world, Depths sought to understand it.

Legends claim the First Regent journeyed into regions of reality untouched by any other Dragon, exploring depths so ancient that even creation itself seemed uncertain there.

Some tales suggest Depths was among the first to notice that certain truths had begun disappearing from memory.

Others claim it was the first to question why Dragons remembered Thirteen while many within Heaven remembered Twelve.

Whether these stories are true remains unknown.

Depths would likely approve of that.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Depths seeks Dragons driven by curiosity rather than certainty.

Those chosen often possess patient minds and an insatiable desire to understand what others overlook.

Many Regents of Depths become explorers, historians, archivists, scholars, and seekers of forgotten truths.

The mantle values questions as highly as answers.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "The deepest truth is often hidden beneath the deepest question."

---

## Philosophy

Depths teaches that certainty is often the enemy of discovery.

Many creatures stop searching once they believe they possess the answer.

Depths teaches that wisdom begins when one is willing to admit there may be more to learn.

Followers of Depths believe that ignorance is not shameful.

Refusing to learn is.

Every mystery contains an opportunity.

Every unanswered question opens a door.

Every forgotten truth waits patiently to be rediscovered.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Depths have served as keepers of memory, seekers of knowledge, and explorers of forgotten corners of existence.

Within the Conclave of Scales, they often advocate caution before accepting assumptions as truth.

Many Dragons regard Depths as the Regency most likely to uncover hidden dangers, forgotten opportunities, or ancient secrets.

Its Regents are often consulted when mysteries arise that challenge conventional understanding.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Depths views creation as something far larger than any individual can fully comprehend.

Reality contains depths beyond what is visible.

History contains truths beyond what is remembered.

The future contains possibilities beyond what is imagined.

For this reason, Depths teaches humility.

No matter how much one learns, there will always be another mystery waiting beyond the edge of understanding.

Depths often shares philosophical ties with Horizons, Journey, and Crossroads.

Together, they represent creation's endless invitation to explore.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Depths include:

- Caverns
- Abyssal Waters
- Hidden Doorways
- Ancient Ruins
- Starless Depths
- Forgotten Paths

Among Dragons, a cave whose end has never been found is often considered the purest symbol of Depths.

Not because it is dark.

Because it still holds secrets.
