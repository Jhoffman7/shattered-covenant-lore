# Wilds

## Overview

Wilds is the Regency of freedom, untamed creation, natural order, and the living world beyond ownership.

Where Horizons teaches that there is always something beyond the known, Wilds teaches that not everything beyond the known is meant to be conquered.

The forest does not belong to the kingdom.

The river does not belong to the city.

The mountain does not belong to the traveler.

They belong to themselves.

Among Dragonkind, Wilds is regarded as one of the oldest and most revered Regencies, for it embodies a truth that predates civilizations, empires, and even the first mortal settlements.

Creation existed before anyone sought to claim it.

It will continue long after they are gone.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Wilds embodies:

- Freedom
- Natural Order
- Independence
- Untamed Creation
- Instinct
- Balance Through Nature
- Self-Determination

Its influence can be found wherever creation exists free from domination.

Ancient forests untouched by civilization.

Predators and prey maintaining the balance of an ecosystem.

A river choosing its own course.

A Dragon refusing to bow to unjust authority.

All are reflections of Wilds.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Wilds emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Wilds as fiercely independent and deeply protective of the natural order of the newborn world.

While many of the First Regents sought to understand, cultivate, or shape creation, Wilds sought to preserve its freedom to become what it was meant to be.

Legends claim the First Regent wandered farther than any of the others, spending ages among untouched lands where creation remained closest to its original form.

Some stories even suggest that Wilds was the loudest voice opposing any attempt to claim ownership over creation itself.

To Wilds, creation was not a possession.

It was a living thing.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Wilds seeks Dragons who value freedom, authenticity, and the integrity of the natural world.

Those chosen are often wanderers, guardians of remote places, defenders of ancient ecosystems, and protectors of those who cannot defend themselves.

Many Regents of Wilds spend long periods away from the centers of Dragon civilization, preferring to remain close to creation in its purest forms.

The mantle values independence above conformity.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "What is truly alive cannot be caged forever."

---

## Philosophy

Wilds teaches that creation possesses its own wisdom.

Not every problem requires intervention.

Not every challenge requires control.

Not every path requires guidance.

Followers of Wilds believe that life flourishes when given the freedom to become what it was meant to be.

To Wilds, excessive control is often more dangerous than chaos.

A forest grows because it is alive.

Not because someone commands it to grow.

Wilds teaches that stewardship and domination are not the same thing.

Creation should be protected.

It should not be owned.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Wilds have served as defenders of Dragonkind's oldest principles.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Wilds frequently reminds the other Regencies that creation is not theirs to command.

Its Regents often oppose efforts that seek to impose excessive control upon either Dragonkind or the Mortal Realm.

Many Dragons view Wilds as the spiritual guardian of the Crucible of Creation itself.

When debates arise regarding the balance between intervention and restraint, the voice of Wilds carries immense weight.

---

## Relationship to Creation

More than any other Regency, Wilds views creation as a living entity.

Not a resource.

Not a machine.

Not a possession.

A living process.

For this reason, Wilds serves as one of the strongest philosophical foundations of Dragonkind.

The belief that creation belongs to itself lies at the heart of both the Regency and the Crucible of Creation.

This conviction would eventually become one of the primary causes of the War of Dominion.

When others sought greater authority over creation, Wilds stood firmly opposed.

Wilds teaches that creation flourishes best when allowed the freedom to follow its own course.

---

## Relationship to the Crucible of Creation

Of all the Regencies, Wilds is most closely associated with the [[Crucible of Creation]].

Many Dragons view Wilds as the purest embodiment of the philosophy itself.

Where other Regencies represent specific expressions of creation, Wilds represents creation's right to exist free from domination.

For this reason, Regents of Wilds are often regarded as spiritual exemplars of Dragonkind's deepest beliefs.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Wilds include:

- Ancient Forests
- Untamed Rivers
- Great Beasts
- Open Skies
- Clawed Tracks
- Unbroken Landscapes

Among Dragons, an untouched wilderness stretching beyond sight is often considered the purest symbol of Wilds.

Not because it is beautiful.

Because it belongs only to itself.
