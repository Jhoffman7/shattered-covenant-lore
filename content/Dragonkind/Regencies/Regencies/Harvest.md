# Harvest

## Overview

Harvest is the Regency of cultivation, stewardship, abundance, and reward.

Where Hearth teaches the value of belonging, Harvest teaches the responsibility that follows.

Nothing flourishes without care.

Nothing grows without effort.

Nothing endures without stewardship.

Among Dragonkind, Harvest is regarded as the embodiment of the truth that creation yields according to what is invested within it.

To reap abundance, one must first cultivate.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Harvest embodies:

- Stewardship
- Cultivation
- Abundance
- Responsibility
- Patience
- Prosperity
- Sustained Growth

Its influence can be found wherever effort is transformed into lasting reward.

A forest nurtured over centuries.

A Brood preserving its traditions.

A kingdom built through generations of labor.

All are reflections of Harvest.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Harvest emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient draconic accounts describe Harvest as the Regent who first understood that creation could not simply survive—it needed to flourish.

While other Regents explored, protected, or endured, Harvest sought ways to ensure that future generations would inherit a world richer than the one before.

Many ancient stories credit Harvest with establishing some of the earliest traditions of stewardship that still endure among Dragonkind.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Harvest seeks Dragons who understand the value of patience and responsibility.

Those chosen are rarely impulsive.

They are planners, caretakers, mentors, and builders of lasting legacies.

Many Regents of Harvest become known not for what they accomplish during their own lifetime, but for what continues to prosper long after they are gone.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "The greatest harvest is not what you gather.
>
> It is what remains when you are gone."

---

## Philosophy

Harvest teaches that abundance is never accidental.

Every reward carries a cost.

Every blessing requires effort.

Every flourishing thing depends upon countless unseen acts of care.

Followers of Harvest believe that true stewardship means leaving creation better than it was found.

They reject wastefulness and short-sighted ambition, viewing both as betrayals of future generations.

To Harvest, prosperity is not measured by what is possessed.

It is measured by what is preserved and passed forward.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Harvest have served as long-term thinkers within the Conclave of Scales.

Where other Regencies may focus upon immediate concerns, Harvest often considers the consequences that may unfold centuries or even millennia later.

Their guidance frequently influences decisions regarding the Broodlands, hatcheries, and the preservation of Dragonkind's future.

Many Dragons consider Harvest one of the most trusted voices when discussing matters that affect future generations.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Harvest views creation as a garden rather than a resource.

Creation must be nurtured.

Protected.

Cultivated.

Its gifts should be respected rather than exploited.

For this reason, Harvest often finds common ground with Foundation, Hearth, and Growth.

Together, these Regencies represent the cycle through which life is sustained, nurtured, and allowed to flourish.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Harvest include:

- Golden Fields
- Fruit-Bearing Trees
- Ripened Grain
- Overflowing Baskets
- Ancient Orchards
- The Changing Seasons

Among Dragons, a flourishing grove planted by previous generations is often considered the purest symbol of Harvest.

Not because it bears fruit.

Because it represents a gift given to the future.

---

## Common Saying

> "What you inherit is a blessing.
>
> What you leave behind is your legacy."
