# Foundation

## Overview

Foundation is the Regency of stability, permanence, and enduring strength.

Among the Thirteen Regencies, Foundation is often regarded as the bedrock upon which all others stand.

Before a home may shelter its inhabitants, it requires a foundation.

Before a kingdom may flourish, it requires a foundation.

Before a mountain may rise, it must first possess a foundation deep beneath the earth.

Foundation teaches that all lasting things are built upon something greater than themselves.

To Dragons, Foundation is not merely stone.

It is the principle of enduring support.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Foundation embodies:

- Stability
- Permanence
- Structure
- Enduring Strength
- Bedrock
- Patience
- Reliability

Its influence can be seen wherever something withstands the passage of time.

Mountain ranges.

Ancient fortresses.

The roots of great trees.

The traditions that survive generations.

All are reflections of Foundation.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Foundation emerged during the earliest moments of creation.

Among the First Thirteen Regents, Foundation was known for patience beyond measure and a steadfast nature that rivaled mountains themselves.

Many ancient Dragon tales claim that when the newborn world was still unstable, Foundation helped anchor portions of reality until creation could fully settle into its present form.

Whether these stories are literal truth or symbolic memory remains a matter of debate among Dragons.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Foundation seeks Dragons who embody unwavering resolve.

Those chosen rarely pursue glory or recognition.

Instead, they are often guardians, protectors, builders, and keepers of ancient traditions.

Many Regents of Foundation have been remembered not for dramatic deeds, but for their refusal to yield when others would have broken.

Among Dragonkind, it is often said:

> "The strongest mountain is not the one that stands tallest.
>
> It is the one still standing when all others have fallen."

---

## Philosophy

Foundation teaches that strength without stability is fleeting.

Ambition without structure collapses.

Growth without support withers.

Even the greatest achievements require something capable of bearing their weight.

For this reason, followers of Foundation often value patience over haste and consistency over brilliance.

They believe that what endures matters more than what dazzles.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Foundation have frequently served as stabilizing voices within the Conclave of Scales.

When Dragonkind faces uncertainty, many look to Foundation for guidance.

Not because Foundation commands authority over the other Regencies, but because its perspective is rooted in continuity rather than reaction.

Foundation often reminds Dragonkind that lasting solutions are rarely the fastest ones.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Foundation views creation as something that must be supported before it can flourish.

Where Growth encourages change and Horizons seeks discovery, Foundation asks whether those things possess the strength to endure.

For this reason, Foundation is often viewed as a balancing counterpart to several other Regencies.

Without Foundation, creation risks becoming unstable.

Without the others, Foundation risks becoming stagnant.

Together, they form part of the balance that sustains reality.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Foundation include:

- Mountains
- Standing Stones
- Pillars
- Bedrock
- Ancient Roots
- Stone Circles

Among Dragons, a mountain is often considered the purest symbol of Foundation.

Not because it is immovable.

But because it remains despite the passing of ages.
