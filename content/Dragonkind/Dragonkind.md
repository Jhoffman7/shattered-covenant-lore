## At a Glance

Dragonkind is one of the oldest civilizations in creation.

Born from reality itself during the dawn of existence, Dragons view themselves not as servants of Heaven, but as children of Creation.

Their society is organized through ancient Broods, guided by sacred Regencies, and united by the principles of the Crucible of Creation.

Though they often stand alongside the Celestial Court, Dragonkind ultimately serves a different purpose: the preservation of Creation itself.

---
## Overview

Dragonkind is one of the oldest civilizations in existence.

When the First Thirteen Primordials brought reality into being, creation answered in kind. From the newborn foundations of existence emerged thirteen great dragons, each embodying a fundamental manifestation of reality itself.

Unlike the Primordials, who embody the Cosmic Forces that define existence, Dragons embody the Physical Systems through which those forces are expressed and stabilized.

The Primordials created reality.

Reality gave rise to Dragonkind.

For this reason, Dragons do not consider themselves children of Heaven. They consider themselves children of Creation.

This distinction has shaped Dragonkind from the first age of the world to the present day.

---

## The First Thirteen Regents

At the dawn of creation, thirteen great dragons emerged alongside reality itself.

These beings became known as the First Regents.

Each embodied a fundamental manifestation of reality and carried a sacred mantle known as a Regency.

The First Regents became the founders of Dragon civilization, the ancestors of the great Broods, and the first guardians of the Mortal Realm.

Unlike Heaven, Dragonkind remembers that there were Thirteen.

While many within the Heavens speak of the First Twelve, Dragons have never forgotten the original number.

Among Dragonkind, the disappearance of the Thirteenth Primordial remains one of the oldest unresolved mysteries in existence.

The First Regents were also entrusted with truths concerning the nature of creation itself. These truths would later be preserved through the Echoes of Scale and passed only to those whose responsibilities required such knowledge.

---

## The Reality Anchors

Among the oldest and most carefully guarded teachings of Dragonkind is the belief that the Original Thirteen Regents served a greater purpose than merely founding the great Broods.

Many ancient traditions suggest that the First Regents were entrusted with responsibilities tied directly to the continued stability of creation itself.

The full truth surrounding these teachings is restricted knowledge, preserved through the Echoes of Scale and revealed only to those whose duties require such understanding.

For more information, see [[The Reality Anchors]].

---

## The Broods

Dragon society is organized through ancient bloodlines known as Broods.

A Brood is neither a political faction nor a governing institution.

A Brood is a lineage.

Every Dragon traces their ancestry through a Brood and carries the traditions, stories, and memories of that lineage.

The Original Thirteen Broods descend directly from the First Regents and remain the foundational lineages of Dragonkind.

Over countless ages, numerous lesser lineages emerged from these ancestral Broods. Some flourished into respected regional bloodlines. Others faded into obscurity, were absorbed into larger lineages, or vanished entirely.

While lineage is respected among Dragons, it does not determine one's destiny.

A Dragon's worth is measured by who they become, not solely by where they came from.

---

## The Regencies

The Regencies are among the most sacred institutions within Dragonkind.

Originating with the First Regents during the dawn of creation, each Regency serves as a living mantle entrusted with a specific responsibility carried forward through the ages.

While individual Regents rise and fall, the Regencies themselves endure, preserving continuity, stewardship, and memory across generations.

The bearers of these mantles stand among the most respected figures in Dragon society and play a central role in preserving Dragonkind's oldest traditions.

For more information, see [[The Thirteen Regencies]].

---

## The Echoes of Scale

The Echoes of Scale are the living memory of Dragonkind.

Through the Echoes, Dragons preserve wisdom, tradition, and experience across countless generations. The Echoes play a central role in Dragon culture, Regency succession, and the preservation of ancient knowledge.

To Dragons, death is not viewed as an ending.

It is a continuation.

For more information, see the [[The Echoes of Scales]].

---

## The Broodlands

The ancestral homeland of Dragonkind is known as the Broodlands.

Protected by ancient creation magic older than recorded history, the Broodlands serve as the spiritual and cultural heart of Dragon civilization.

Though Dragons establish territories throughout the Mortal Realm, all Dragons ultimately trace their origins back to the Broodlands.

The region is considered sacred by every Brood.

Even Regents tread carefully within its borders.

---

## The Cradle of Scales

At the heart of the Broodlands lies the Cradle of Scales, the most sacred site in all of Dragonkind.

The Cradle is believed to have existed since the dawn of creation. It serves as the birthplace of new generations, the resting place of vacant Regencies, and one of the greatest mysteries preserved within Dragon civilization.

To Dragons, the Cradle represents continuity, renewal, and the enduring future of their people.

For more information, see [[The Cradle of Scales]].

---

## The Conclave of Scales

The Conclave of Scales, often called the Scalebound, serves as the highest institution within Dragon society.

Composed of the current Regents and the First Scales of the major Broods, the Conclave exists to preserve Dragonkind's oldest traditions, oversee Regency succession, protect the Broodlands, and safeguard the future of Dragon civilization.

Unlike mortal governments, the Conclave does not rule through laws or authority. Its purpose is continuity rather than governance.

When the Scalebound speaks, it does so not as a ruler of Dragons, but as the collective voice of Dragonkind itself.

For more information, see [[The Conclave of Scales]].

---

## The Cataclysm

Among the oldest fears preserved within Dragonkind is the possibility known as The Cataclysm.

The Cataclysm refers to the theoretical extinction of one of the Original Thirteen Broods and the collapse of the Reality Anchor it maintains.

Most Dragons know only fragments of this belief.

The full truth remains restricted knowledge.

Those entrusted with understanding the Reality Anchors recognize The Cataclysm as one of the greatest existential threats creation could face.

The extinction of a Brood would not simply diminish Dragonkind.

It would destabilize one of the fundamental systems that allows reality to function.

For this reason, the continued existence of all Thirteen Broods is considered essential to the continued stability of creation itself.

For more information, see [[The Cataclysm]].

---

## The Crucible of Creation

The guiding philosophy of Dragonkind is known as the Crucible of Creation.

Dragons believe that creation is neither property nor machine.

Creation is a living process.

Everything is tested.

Everything changes.

Everything grows.

Everything becomes.

To Dragons, the purpose of life is not to dominate creation, but to endure its trials, learn from them, and help preserve its ability to continue.

Three principles form the foundation of this philosophy:
### Endure
Strength is found through perseverance.
### Become
Growth is one of creation's fundamental truths.
### Preserve
Creation must be protected from forces that would halt its natural course.

For more information, see [[Crucible of Creation]].

## The War of Dominion

One of the defining events in Dragon history was the War of Dominion, a conflict between Dragonkind and powerful factions within Heaven concerning the stewardship of the Mortal Realm.

The war profoundly shaped the relationship between Dragons, the Celestial Court, and the wider world. Its consequences would eventually contribute to the establishment of the Divine Boundary and many of the political realities that persist into the present age.

For more information, see [[The War of Dominion]].

## Dragonborn

Though descended from Dragonkind's legacy, Dragonborn occupy a unique place within creation.

Across history they have served as diplomats, scholars, warriors, artisans, and leaders, carrying draconic traditions far beyond the Broodlands.

While they do not possess the immense power or longevity of true Dragons, Dragonborn remain deeply connected to the heritage of the Broods and are widely regarded as one of Dragonkind's greatest contributions to the wider world.

For more information, see [[Dragonborn]].

---
## Relationship with Heaven

Dragonkind neither serves Heaven nor opposes it.

The two civilizations share a relationship built upon mutual respect, ancient disagreements, and an understanding of one another's power.

Where Heaven seeks to preserve the Covenant, Dragonkind seeks to preserve Creation itself.

Often these goals align.

History has proven they are not always the same.

For this reason, Dragons remain among the few powers in existence willing to openly challenge Heaven when they believe creation itself is at risk.

To Heaven, Dragonkind is a necessary counterpart.

To Dragonkind, Heaven is a trusted ally that occasionally forgets its place.
