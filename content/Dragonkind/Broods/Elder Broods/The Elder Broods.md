# The Elder Broods

## Overview

The Elder Broods are the oldest and most revered lineages within Dragonkind.

Their histories stretch back to the earliest ages of creation, and many of Dragon civilization's most enduring traditions originated among them.

Though countless Broods have emerged throughout history, the Elder Broods remain unique in both age and influence.

To Dragons, they are more than ancient bloodlines.

They are living connections to the foundations of Dragon civilization itself.

---

## The First Lineages

The Elder Broods trace their origins to the earliest generations of Dragonkind.

Their histories extend beyond recorded memory and into the mythic ages that followed creation itself.

Throughout the centuries, these lineages produced Regents, First Scales, explorers, scholars, warriors, and visionaries whose actions helped define the course of Dragon history.

Many younger Broods ultimately trace portions of their ancestry back to one or more of the Elder Broods.

For this reason, the Elder Broods are often viewed as the ancestral roots from which much of Dragonkind grew.

---

## Legacy and Influence

The influence of the Elder Broods extends far beyond lineage alone.

Many of Dragonkind's oldest customs, traditions, philosophies, and institutions originated within these ancient lineages.

The Elder Broods played central roles in the formation of [[The Conclave of Scales]], the development of [[Wyrmreach]], and the preservation of the traditions that continue to unite Dragonkind today.

While membership within an Elder Brood is respected, Dragons generally believe that lineage alone does not determine worth.

A Dragon's actions, character, and contributions remain far more important than ancestry.

Even so, the Elder Broods continue to command immense respect throughout Dragon society.

---

## The Thirteen Elder Broods

### [[Ashwing]]

**The Brood of Transformation**

Associated with destruction, renewal, and rebirth. Ashwings teach that endings are not failures of creation, but necessary beginnings. They embody the belief that growth often emerges from what has been broken, burned away, or left behind.

### [[Stoneheart]]

**The Brood of Foundation**

Associated with endurance, resilience, and permanence. Stonehearts believe that true strength is measured not by force, but by the ability to withstand the passage of time and remain unbroken by adversity.

### [[Stormclaw]]

**The Brood of Atmosphere**

Associated with freedom, momentum, and decisive action. Stormclaws embrace movement over stagnation and often view hesitation as the greatest obstacle to progress.

### [[Dawnfire]]

**The Brood of Radiance**

Associated with hope, vitality, and inspiration. Dawnfires seek to illuminate paths forward for themselves and others, believing that even the darkest age eventually yields to a new dawn.

### [[Wildroot]]

**The Brood of Adaptation**

Associated with growth, interconnection, and life's endless capacity to change. Wildroots view survival not as dominance, but as the ability to evolve alongside creation itself.

### [[Tideborn]]

**The Brood of Flow**

Associated with unity, connection, and cooperation. Tideborn believe that strength emerges through relationships and that no individual truly thrives in isolation from the greater whole.

### [[Ironcrest]]

**The Brood of Structure**

Associated with duty, stewardship, and unwavering responsibility. Ironcrests view themselves as guardians of continuity, believing that civilization endures only when its foundations are diligently maintained.

### [[Emberveil]]

**The Brood of Continuity**

Associated with memory, remembrance, and the preservation of wisdom. Emberveils devote themselves to ensuring that knowledge, stories, and lessons survive beyond those who first carried them.

### [[Skyfang]]

**The Brood of Ascent**

Associated with ascent, exploration, and the pursuit of greater heights. Skyfangs believe creation rewards those willing to rise above limitations and seek what lies beyond the horizon.

### [[Winterborn]]

**The Brood of Preservation**

Associated with patience, endurance, and the wisdom of restraint. Winterborn understand that not every challenge is overcome through action and that survival often depends upon knowing when to endure.

### [[Deepscale]]

**The Brood of Shadow**

Associated with mystery, hidden truths, and the unseen depths of existence. Deepscales believe that understanding often lies beyond what is immediately visible and that some truths reveal themselves only to the patient.

### [[Moonscale]]

**The Brood of Cycles**

Associated with reflection, balance, and recurring patterns. Moonscales teach that creation moves through rhythms greater than any individual and that wisdom comes from understanding one's place within those cycles.

### [[Starbound]]

**The Brood of Horizons**

Associated with discovery, wonder, and the pursuit of possibility. Starbounds are driven by curiosity and the belief that every horizon exists to be crossed.

---

## Relationship to Dragonkind

The Elder Broods are not rulers of Dragonkind.

Nor do they possess authority over younger lineages.

Instead, they serve as respected ancestors within Dragon society.

Their histories provide perspective.

Their traditions provide continuity.

Their examples provide inspiration.

Throughout the ages, Dragons have looked to the Elder Broods not because they command obedience, but because they embody the accumulated wisdom of countless generations.

---
