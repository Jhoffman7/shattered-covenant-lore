# Ashwing

## The Brood of the Caldera

### Common Name

**The Brimscale**

---

## Overview

The Ashwing Brood is among the oldest and most respected lineages of [[Dragonkind/Dragonkind]].

Masters of the volcanic territories known as the [[Brimstone Veil]], the Ashwings are renowned for their resilience, intensity, and transformative nature.

To outsiders, Ashwings are often viewed as living embodiments of volcanic power.

Beneath their calm exteriors burns an immense inner fire.

Like the mountains they call home, Ashwings are often patient and composed.

Yet when pushed beyond their limits, they are capable of sudden and overwhelming fury.

Throughout history, Ashwings have produced warriors, leaders, Regents, explorers, and heroes whose actions forever changed the course of Dragonkind.

---

## Homeland

### The Brimstone Veil

The ancestral territory of the Ashwing Brood lies within a vast volcanic region of the [[Wyrmreach]] known as the Brimstone Veil.

The region is characterized by:

- Active calderas
- Volcanic mountain chains
- Lava rivers
- Obsidian fields
- Sulfur springs
- Ash deserts
- Smoke-shrouded valleys

A perpetual haze of ash and volcanic smoke hangs over much of the region, giving rise to its name.

The Ashwings view the Brimstone Veil not as a harsh land, but as a place of strength, renewal, and transformation.

---
## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Ashwings are known as catalysts.

Where others tolerate stagnation, Ashwings often push for action.

Where others hesitate, Ashwings act.

This reputation has earned them both admiration and frustration throughout history.

Many Dragons respect their willingness to confront difficult truths.

Others consider them reckless.

A common saying among other Broods states:

> "When the mountain stirs, something is about to change."

---

## Culture

Ashwing culture values:

- Conviction
- Courage
- Honesty
- Resolve
- Action

Ashwings generally prefer directness over subtlety.

Though capable of patience, they dislike unnecessary delays and indecision.

Unlike many younger Dragons, Ashwings do not fear conflict.

They view disagreement as a natural part of growth and believe that difficult conversations are often necessary for meaningful change.

To an Ashwing, avoiding a problem rarely solves it.

Confronting it does.

---

## Temperament

Ashwings are often misunderstood by outsiders.

Contrary to popular belief, they are not quick to anger.

In truth, most Ashwings possess tremendous patience.

However, they tend to internalize frustration rather than express it immediately.

Like pressure building beneath a mountain, their emotions accumulate over time.

When they finally reach their breaking point, the result can be sudden, intense, and unforgettable.

Among Dragonkind it is often said:

> "The mountain only appears calm."

---

## First Scale Selection

### The Rite of the Caldera

When an Ashwing First Scale passes, candidates undergo an ancient succession ritual known as the Rite of the Caldera.

The trial takes place deep within the volcanic heartlands of the Brimstone Veil.

Participants descend into sacred calderas where they are tested physically, mentally, and spiritually.

The purpose of the Rite is not survival.

It is transformation.

Candidates are expected to emerge changed by the experience.

Those who complete the Rite are judged by the Ashwing elders and the [[The Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon deemed most worthy becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Relationship with Other Broods

### Closest Allies

#### [[Stormclaw]]

Both Broods value decisive action and possess little patience for stagnation.

Though their methods often differ, they share a mutual respect.

---

#### [[Ironcrest]]

Ashwings admire the steadfast nature of the Ironcrests, while Ironcrests respect the Ashwings' conviction.

---

### Traditional Rivals

#### [[Stoneheart]]

One of the oldest rivalries among the Original Thirteen.

Stonehearts favor patience and permanence.

Ashwings favor action and transformation.

Though disagreements are common, mutual respect remains strong.

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Ashwings have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Forgefire]]
- [[Storms]]
- [[Wilds]]
- [[Horizons]]

However, Ashwings hold no special claim to any Regency.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may one day inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Ashwings have remained one of the defining lineages of Dragonkind.

Their influence can be found in ancient victories, historic reforms, and pivotal moments that reshaped draconic civilization.

Whether admired or criticized, few Dragons deny the impact Ashwings have had upon history.

Like the volcanoes from which they draw inspiration, they are forces of transformation.

And like the mountains beneath those volcanoes, they endure.

---

## Common Saying

> "From ash we rise. Through fire we endure."
