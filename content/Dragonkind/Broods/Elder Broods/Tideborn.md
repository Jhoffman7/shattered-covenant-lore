# Tideborn

## The Brood of Living Currents

### Common Name

**The Waveborn**

---

## Overview

The Tideborn Brood is among the most traveled and widely connected lineages within [[Dragonkind/Dragonkind]].

Masters of the vast oceanic region known as the [[Endless Tides]], the Tideborn embody the currents, tides, and connections that bind distant places together.

Where other Broods often focus inward upon their territories and traditions, the Tideborn naturally look outward.

To them, no shore exists in isolation.

No island stands alone.

No life remains untouched by the greater currents of creation.

Throughout history, Tideborn have served as explorers, diplomats, messengers, navigators, storytellers, and Regents whose efforts have strengthened the bonds between peoples, kingdoms, and Broods.

Many Dragons believe the Tideborn know more about the world than anyone else.

Not because they study it.

Because they travel through it.

---

## Homeland

### The Endless Tides

The ancestral territory of the Tideborn Brood is a vast oceanic region known as the Endless Tides.

Unlike many Broods whose territories are defined by singular landscapes, the Endless Tides are defined by the waters that connect countless islands, coastlines, reefs, and archipelagos together.

The region is characterized by:

- Vast open oceans
- Tropical island chains
- Ancient coral archipelagos
- Deep sea channels
- Hidden sea caves
- Powerful ocean currents
- Coastal sanctuaries

The waters of the Endless Tides are rarely still.

Movement defines the region.

Currents shift.

Tides rise and fall.

Storms pass.

Life migrates.

To the Tideborn, this constant motion is not chaos.

It is harmony.

---

## Sacred Landmark

### The Worldreef

At the heart of the Endless Tides lies the Worldreef.

No place is more sacred to the Tideborn.

Stretching across hundreds of miles, the Worldreef is one of the largest living structures within the [[Wyrmreach]].

Ancient coral forests, thriving ecosystems, and countless species call the reef home.

To the Tideborn, the Worldreef serves as the ultimate expression of connection.

No single coral built the reef.

No single current sustains it.

Its existence depends upon countless living things working together across generations.

For this reason, the Worldreef is viewed as both a sacred site and a living lesson.

It is here that the Great Current begins.

It is here that First Scales are chosen.

And it is here that Tideborn from across the Endless Tides gather during times of celebration and crisis alike.

---

## Appearance

Tideborn Dragons possess some of the most graceful and elegant forms within Dragonkind.

They do not resemble creatures that inhabit the ocean.

They resemble the ocean's currents given physical form.

Fluid, radiant, and endlessly adaptable, Tideborn embody connection, movement, and unity.

Where many Dragons dominate their surroundings through power or presence, Tideborn transform them through flow.

Every movement appears effortless.

Every motion appears intentional.

Common traits include:

- Hydrodynamic scale structures
- Coral horn formations
- Bioluminescent current markings
- Ribbon-like fins
- Oceanic coloration
- Sensory barbels
- Seafoam wakes
- Living reef ecosystems

Many observers describe Tideborn as appearing less like Dragons and more like living rivers flowing through the sea.

### Caustic Mirror Scales

The scales of a Tideborn form an exceptionally smooth and seamless surface.

Unlike traditional armor plates, these scales interlock perfectly to create a sleek hydrodynamic carapace.

Their polished surfaces display shifting colors of pearl, turquoise, seafoam, and sapphire.

When illuminated, the scales project shimmering networks of reflected light across the body.

These patterns resemble sunlight dancing upon the ocean floor beneath shallow waters.

The effect is known among the Brood as the Caustic Mirror.

### Living Coral Crowns

The horns of a Tideborn are formed from extraordinarily dense calcified coral structures.

Rather than growing as simple spikes, they branch outward into elegant fans, spirals, and reef-like formations.

Each Dragon's crown develops unique patterns throughout its life.

The oldest Tideborn often host colonies of tiny glowing marine organisms, anemones, and reef polyps upon these structures.

Among the Brood, coral crowns are viewed as living records of a Dragon's journey through the world.

### Ribbon-Fin Shrouds

The wings, tails, and secondary fins of a Tideborn resemble the flowing appendages of exotic reef creatures.

Long translucent ribbons drift behind them during movement.

In water, these structures allow impossible agility.

In the air, they catch currents and thermals with graceful precision.

Observers often compare their movements to flowing silk or underwater kelp drifting upon the tides.

### The Great Current

Beneath the scales of every Tideborn runs a luminous vascular system known as the Great Current.

These glowing pathways trace the contours of the Dragon's body in elegant flowing patterns.

Unlike the violent electrical channels of Stormclaws, the Great Current pulses slowly and rhythmically.

The colors shift depending upon the Dragon's environment and emotional state.

Surface currents often produce bright aqua and turquoise light.

Deeper waters generate darker sapphire and indigo hues.

The effect resembles entire oceans flowing beneath the skin.

### Seafoam Wake

Tideborn naturally generate a phenomenon known as the Seafoam Wake.

As they move, specialized glands and scale seams aerate the surrounding air or water.

The result is a trailing veil of tiny bubbles, mist, and ocean spray.

The air around them carries the scent of saltwater, sea breeze, and distant storms.

Many describe their presence as feeling like standing upon a coastline moments before sunrise.

### Eyes of Deep Sapphire

Tideborn eyes are large, expressive, and unusually warm.

Their irises resemble clear tropical lagoons or deep ocean waters viewed beneath sunlight.

Unlike the intimidating gazes of many Dragons, Tideborn eyes naturally invite trust and communication.

Many diplomats throughout history have remarked that it feels almost impossible to remain hostile while meeting a Tideborn's gaze.

### Sensory Barbels

Flowing from the lower jaw are elegant whisker-like barbels.

These structures drift gently through air and water alike.

Extremely sensitive to pressure changes, magical disturbances, temperature shifts, and emotional tension, the barbels allow Tideborn to perceive subtle changes in their environment long before others notice them.

Among the Brood, these organs are often compared to the currents themselves.

Always feeling.

Always listening.

Always connected.

### The Living Reef

Older Tideborn often develop symbiotic ecosystems upon their bodies.

Barnacles.

Coral growths.

Sea grasses.

Tiny shellfish.

Bioluminescent organisms.

Entire miniature reef communities may flourish upon elder Dragons.

Rather than removing these growths, Tideborn encourage them.

Each new organism represents another connection formed with the world.

### The Colors of the Reef

Tideborn coloration reflects the incredible diversity of the oceans.

Primary coloration includes:

- Mother-of-pearl white
- Turquoise
- Seafoam blue
- Sapphire
- Deep ocean teal

These are accented by vibrant reef colors such as:

- Coral pink
- Amber gold
- Peacock teal
- Reef violet
- Tropical azure

The resulting appearance shifts constantly depending upon lighting and surrounding water conditions.

### The Living Scrapbook

As Tideborn age, they intentionally collect memories and relationships in physical form.

Many braid charms, sea-glass tokens, woven cords, and gifts from distant cultures into their fins and coral crowns.

Others bear carved ocean-runes gifted by foreign civilizations.

Among the Tideborn, these collections are considered living records of friendships formed and journeys shared.

A Tideborn's body becomes a history of connections.

### The Current-Drift Form

The oldest Tideborn eventually begin to lose the appearance of solid matter.

Their scales become increasingly translucent.

Their bodies appear composed of flowing water held together by will alone.

Light passes through portions of their forms.

The Great Current becomes visible beneath the surface at all times.

Some ancient Tideborn appear almost spectral.

As though they are no longer Dragons traveling the currents.

But currents that have become Dragons.

### Living Passengers

Many elder Tideborn carry entire communities of marine life within the translucent structures of their fins and wings.

Schools of tiny luminous fish drift through their bodies.

Symbiotic creatures follow them for centuries.

Some ocean cultures believe these animals are the preserved memories of companions encountered throughout the Dragon's life.

Whether this is truth or legend remains unknown.

### Visual Identity

A Tideborn should appear less like a Dragon that lives within the ocean and more like a living current connecting distant shores.

Graceful.

Welcoming.

Endlessly moving.

Every aspect of their existence reflects the belief that life gains meaning through the connections we form with one another.

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Tideborn are known as travelers, diplomats, and bridge-builders.

They are often the first to establish relationships between distant peoples and cultures.

Many Dragons seek their stories, knowledge of distant lands, and understanding of foreign customs.

A common saying among Dragonkind states:

> "If you wish to know what lies beyond the horizon, ask a Tideborn."

Their willingness to travel and connect others has earned them widespread respect throughout the Wyrmreach.

---

## Culture

Tideborn culture values:

- Community
- Cooperation
- Exploration
- Relationships
- Adaptability
- Communication

Unlike many lineages, Tideborn rarely view themselves as isolated individuals.

They naturally build networks of friendships, alliances, and partnerships wherever they travel.

Stories, traditions, and knowledge are often shared freely among their people.

To a Tideborn, every relationship creates another current connecting the world.

---

## Temperament

Tideborn are generally sociable, adaptable, and open-minded.

When confronted with obstacles, they rarely seek direct confrontation.

Instead, they look for ways to move around, through, or beyond the problem.

Among Dragonkind, they are often considered excellent mediators because of their ability to see multiple perspectives and find common ground.

Their approach to adversity is often summarized as:

### Pressure → Flow

Where others meet resistance head-on, Tideborn seek the path forward.

Among Dragonkind it is often said:

> "Water reaches places stone never will."

## First Scale Selection

### The Great Current

When a Tideborn First Scale passes, candidates undertake an ancient succession ritual known as the Great Current.

The journey begins at the Worldreef.

Each candidate follows a different route throughout the Endless Tides.

They travel alone.

They meet new peoples.

Face new challenges.

Build new relationships.

The purpose of the Great Current is not speed.

Nor strength.

It is connection.

Upon returning to the Worldreef, candidates are judged by Tideborn elders and the [[Dragonkind/Traditions/The Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon who demonstrates the greatest ability to build understanding, cooperation, and meaningful relationships becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Tideborn have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Journey]]
- [[Horizons]]
- [[Crossroads]]
- [[Harvest]]

However, Tideborn possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Tideborn have served as the connective tissue of Dragonkind.

Their voyages have united distant regions.

Their diplomacy has resolved conflicts.

Their stories have preserved histories that might otherwise have been lost.

Though many Broods possess great strength, wisdom, or influence, few have done more to connect the world than the Tideborn.

Like the currents they embody, their influence can be found far beyond the places where they began.
