# Wildroot

## The Brood of Living Roots

### Common Name

**The Untamed**

---

## Overview

The Wildroot Brood is among the oldest and most vibrant lineages within [[Dragonkind/Dragonkind]].

Masters of the vast wilderness known as the [[Verdant Expanse]], the Wildroots embody the untamed forces of life itself.

Growth.

Adaptation.

Renewal.

Survival.

To the Wildroots, creation is not defined by permanence or stability.

It is defined by change through growth.

Every living thing must adapt.

Every living thing must evolve.

Every living thing must find its place within the greater cycle of existence.

Wildroots do not view nature as something separate from civilization.

Nor do they believe life can be reduced to simple concepts of good or evil.

A blooming flower.

A hunting predator.

A towering forest.

A devastating wildfire.

All are natural expressions of life.

For countless ages, the Wildroots have served as guardians of living places, stewards of ancient ecosystems, and champions of creation's endless capacity to endure through transformation.

---

## Homeland

### The Verdant Expanse

The ancestral territory of the Wildroot Brood is a vast primordial wilderness known as the Verdant Expanse.

Unlike many regions of the [[Wyrmreach]], the Verdant Expanse has never been fully shaped by Dragonkind.

Life grows freely here.

The landscape is characterized by:

- Colossal ancient forests
- Endless grasslands
- Massive root networks
- Towering flowering canopies
- Living valleys
- Ancient wetlands
- Primeval ecosystems

Many plants and creatures found within the Expanse exist nowhere else in the world.

Entire ecosystems have survived unchanged since the earliest days of creation.

To outsiders, the Verdant Expanse appears wild.

To the Wildroots, it is simply alive.

---

## Sacred Landmark

### The Worldroot Grove

At the heart of the Verdant Expanse lies a sacred region known as the Worldroot Grove.

Ancient trees of impossible size dominate the landscape.

Their roots stretch across vast portions of the Expanse.

Many Wildroots believe the Grove preserves echoes of creation's earliest growth.

The Worldroot Grove serves as a place of gathering, reflection, and ceremony.

It is here that the Trial of Seasons begins.

It is here that First Scales are chosen.

And it is here that many of the Brood's oldest traditions continue to endure.

---

## Appearance

Wildroot Dragons possess some of the most diverse and biologically complex forms within Dragonkind.

They do not resemble creatures that live within nature.

They resemble nature itself given draconic form.

Every Wildroot is a living ecosystem.

Their bodies serve as homes for plants, fungi, insects, and countless symbiotic organisms.

Where many Dragons appear separate from the world around them, Wildroots blur the distinction between creature and landscape.

Common traits include:

- Bark-like armor
- Living root musculature
- Antler-like horn formations
- Symbiotic ecosystems
- Amber or jade eyes
- Botanical growths
- Spore-based breath
- Seasonal transformations

Many observers describe Wildroots as appearing less like Dragons and more like ancient forests that learned how to move.

### Ironwood Scutes

The scales of a Wildroot resemble ancient tree bark rather than traditional draconic armor.

Deep furrows, knots, and grain patterns run across every plate.

Their hides often resemble ironwood, sequoia, oak, petrified wood, or other ancient species.

Unlike conventional scales, Wildroot armor continuously adds new layers over time.

Many elder Dragons possess visible growth rings across portions of their bodies.

Moss, lichen, and flowering plants frequently grow within the crevices between these scales.

### Living Root Endoskeleton

Beneath their bark-like armor lies a network of powerful root structures.

The underbelly, neck joints, wing joints, and major muscle groups consist of densely braided living roots rather than conventional musculature.

These root systems visibly contract and shift during movement.

When exerting themselves, faint emerald sap can often be seen pulsing through the exposed structures.

The result is a body that appears simultaneously plant-like and immensely powerful.

### Crown Antlers

The horns of a Wildroot resemble magnificent branching antlers formed from petrified wood, calcified bramble, and living root structures.

Unlike the fixed horns of most Dragons, Wildroot antlers subtly change throughout the year.

Spring often brings mosses and flowering growths.

Summer produces vibrant foliage.

Autumn introduces brilliant seasonal colors.

Winter leaves the structures bare and skeletal.

Among the Brood, a Dragon's antlers are viewed as visible expressions of their connection to the living world.

### The Three Forest Forms

Nature is not singular.

Neither are the Wildroots.

Over countless generations, the Brood developed three primary body archetypes.

### Iron-Burl Colossus

The Iron-Burl Colossi represent endurance, protection, and survival.

These Dragons possess immense low-slung bodies resembling ancient oak trees given life.

Their backs often support entire ecosystems of grasses, flowers, and shrubs.

Massive root-hammer tails allow them to break stone and reshape terrain.

Many serve as guardians of the oldest regions within the [[Verdant Expanse]].

### Canopy Skimmer

The Canopy Skimmers embody adaptability, exploration, and growth.

These Dragons possess long serpentine bodies and exceptional agility.

Their wing membranes resemble enormous living leaves reinforced by thick vascular structures.

Prehensile vine-like tails allow them to anchor themselves within forest canopies and cliffside groves.

Among the Wildroots, they are often explorers, scouts, and storytellers.

### Bramble-Fiend

The Bramble-Fiends represent instinct, competition, and predation.

Their bodies are lean, angular, and covered in dense clusters of ironwood thorns.

Though physically smaller than many Wildroots, they possess explosive speed and remarkable aggression when threatened.

Many outsiders mistakenly assume they are hostile.

In reality, they simply embody the harsher realities of nature.

### Symbiotic Ecosystems

Every Wildroot carries life upon their body.

Mosses.

Lichens.

Fungi.

Vines.

Flowers.

Entire miniature ecosystems develop across elder Dragons.

Some host glowing mushrooms.

Others cultivate medicinal plants.

Many support species found nowhere else within the Wyrmreach.

The oldest Wildroots are often regarded as moving habitats rather than individual creatures.

### The Photosynthetic Gaze

Wildroot eyes possess no visible pupils.

Instead they appear as flawless gemstones of polished amber or deep jade.

Beneath the crystalline surface, subtle swirling structures resemble plant cells suspended within liquid sunlight.

When a Wildroot focuses intently, ambient light appears to gather around their eyes and refract into emerald shafts.

Many creatures find their gaze deeply calming.

Others find it impossible to look away.

### The Spore Wake

Rather than breathing fire, Wildroots release vast clouds of pollen, spores, and botanical particles.

The composition varies dramatically between individuals.

Some clouds stimulate growth and healing.

Others accelerate plant development.

Some induce calm.

Others provoke territorial aggression or fear.

The most ancient Wildroots can transform entire landscapes through a single breath.

Among Dragonkind, this phenomenon is known as the Spore Wake.

### The Living Greenhouse

The presence of a Wildroot naturally accelerates biological activity.

Flowers bloom more readily.

Seeds germinate more quickly.

Fungal networks spread farther.

Animals often gather around resting Wildroots without fear.

The oldest members of the Brood carry entire microclimates with them wherever they travel.

Many regions of the Verdant Expanse owe their existence to the prolonged presence of ancient Wildroots.

### The Colors of Nature

Wildroot coloration reflects the diversity of the natural world.

Common base tones include:

- Lichen gray
- Burnt umber
- Peat black
- Deep bark brown
- Petrified wood

These earthy foundations are contrasted by vibrant living elements:

- Moss emerald
- Fungal yellow
- Amber gold
- Orchid crimson
- Jungle violet

The resulting appearance often changes with seasons and environmental conditions.

### Taking Root

As Wildroots enter their final centuries, many begin a gradual transformation known simply as Taking Root.

Movement becomes slower.

Periods of rest become longer.

Their bodies increasingly merge with the surrounding environment.

Roots emerge from their limbs.

Plants spread across their backs.

Entire ecosystems begin depending upon their presence.

Many eventually spend decades or centuries in a single location.

### The Living Monuments

The oldest Wildroots become nearly indistinguishable from the landscape itself.

Birds nest within their antlers.

Streams flow across their backs.

Forests grow around their resting forms.

Some become so integrated with their surroundings that travelers unknowingly walk across sleeping Dragons without realizing it.

These ancient beings are known among the Brood as Living Monuments.

They are revered as embodiments of the endless cycle of growth, decay, and renewal.

### Visual Identity

A Wildroot should appear less like a Dragon living within a forest and more like a forest given consciousness.

Ancient.

Wise.

Untamed.

Every aspect of their existence reflects the truth that nature is not merely life.

It is the endless cycle through which all life grows, adapts, competes, and endures.

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Wildroots are known for their wisdom, adaptability, and connection to living things.

Unlike the scholarly wisdom of the [[Moonscale]] Brood, Wildroot wisdom is rooted in experience.

Observation.

Survival.

Growth.

Many Dragons seek their guidance when facing difficult transitions or uncertain futures.

A common saying among Dragonkind states:

> "Ask a Wildroot how life survives."

---

## Culture

Wildroot culture values:

- Growth
- Adaptation
- Balance
- Resilience
- Harmony
- Instinct

Wildroots believe stagnation is one of the greatest threats to life.

Everything must grow.

Everything must adapt.

Everything must change.

To remain unchanged indefinitely is considered unnatural.

This belief shapes nearly every aspect of Wildroot society.

Young Dragons are encouraged to explore.

To learn.

To experience the world directly.

Wisdom gained through living is considered far more valuable than wisdom gained through observation alone.

---

## Temperament

Wildroots are generally calm, patient, and observant.

They rarely seek conflict unnecessarily.

However, they are also highly adaptable.

When confronted with hardship, they do not resist change.

They embrace it.

Among Dragonkind, Wildroots are often described as remarkably resilient.

Difficult circumstances rarely break them.

Instead, they learn from adversity and emerge stronger.

Among Dragonkind it is often said:

> "A Wildroot does not survive the storm. It grows through it."

## First Scale Selection

### The Trial of Seasons

When a Wildroot First Scale passes, candidates undergo an ancient succession ritual known as the Trial of Seasons.

The trial requires candidates to journey throughout the Verdant Expanse and endure every aspect of its living cycle.

Growth.

Scarcity.

Predation.

Renewal.

Each candidate must demonstrate adaptability, wisdom, and resilience in the face of constantly changing circumstances.

The trial does not reward strength alone.

It rewards growth.

Upon completing the Trial, candidates are judged by Wildroot elders and the [[Dragonkind/Traditions/The Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon deemed most worthy becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Wildroots have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Growth]]
- [[Wilds]]
- [[Harvest]]
- [[Dawn]]

However, Wildroots possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Wildroots have remained among the most enduring lineages within Dragonkind.

Their stewardship has preserved ancient ecosystems.

Their wisdom has guided countless Dragons.

Their resilience has allowed them to thrive through every age of history.

Though kingdoms rise and fall, life endures.

And as long as life endures, the Wildroots believe creation itself remains alive.
