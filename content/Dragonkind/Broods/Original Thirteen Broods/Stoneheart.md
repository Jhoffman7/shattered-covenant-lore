# Stoneheart

## The Brood of the Worldspine

### Common Name

**The Worldspine Wardens**

---

## Overview

The Stoneheart Brood is one of the oldest and most respected lineages within [[Dragonkind/Dragonkind]].

Masters of the towering mountain realm known as the [[Great Lithic Spine]], the Stonehearts are renowned for their unwavering resolve, immense strength, and steadfast commitment to protecting what they deem worthy of preservation.

To Dragons, the Stonehearts are often regarded as the Shield of Dragonkind.

When uncertainty spreads, when conflict threatens stability, or when ancient places require guardians, many look to the Stonehearts.

Not because they seek authority.

Because they can be relied upon to endure.

Across countless ages, Stonehearts have served as protectors, stewards, and guardians of both Dragonkind and the oldest places of the [[Wyrmreach]].

---

## Homeland

### The Great Lithic Spine

The ancestral territory of the Stoneheart Brood lies within a colossal mountain range known as the Great Lithic Spine.

Among Dragons, the range is considered one of the oldest surviving landscapes in existence.

The region is characterized by:

- Sky-piercing mountain peaks
- Vast cliffside plateaus
- Crystal caverns
- Ancient stone valleys
- Snow-covered summits
- Hidden mountain sanctuaries
- Deep subterranean halls

Many locations within the Great Lithic Spine predate recorded draconic history.

Some peaks are considered sacred and remain untouched even by the Stonehearts themselves.

To the Stoneheart Brood, the mountains are not merely territory.

They are living monuments to endurance.

## Appearance

Stoneheart Dragons possess some of the most imposing and immovable forms within Dragonkind.

They do not resemble creatures covered in stone.

They resemble mountains that learned to walk.

Massive, broad, and seemingly eternal, Stonehearts embody permanence in physical form. Their presence evokes the feeling of standing before an ancient cliff face that has endured storms, wars, and ages beyond counting.

Common traits include:

- Massive fortress-like frames
- Tectonic plate armor
- Neon-blue crystalline eyes
- Geode-filled fault lines
- Branching stalagmite horns
- Hyper-dense anchor talons
- Resonant cavernous voices
- Alpine flora growing across elder Dragons

Many observers describe Stonehearts as appearing less like living creatures and more like pieces of the world itself.

### Tectonic Anatomy

The bodies of Stonehearts are defined by enormous overlapping plates resembling tectonic slabs, basalt columns, and ancient bedrock formations.

Rather than possessing conventional scales, their hides are composed of immense geological structures layered atop one another.

Deep fault lines separate these plates and create natural seams throughout their bodies.

When a Stoneheart moves, the plates shift and grind against one another with the deep rumbling sound of distant earthquakes.

The effect is both unsettling and awe-inspiring.

Many describe the sound as the voice of a mountain awakening.

### The Living Shield-Wall

Stonehearts possess exceptionally powerful forelimbs, shoulders, and chest structures.

When adopting a defensive stance, their massive plates interlock to form a nearly seamless wall of stone.

Their sternums resemble sheer cliff faces carved from ancient mountainsides.

In battle, this posture transforms the Dragon into a living fortress capable of withstanding tremendous force.

Among Dragonkind, few sights are more intimidating than a Stoneheart preparing to stand its ground.

### Anchor Talons

Unlike the hooked claws common among many Dragons, Stoneheart talons are broad, blunt, and incredibly dense.

Resembling pillars of slate, obsidian, or black granite, these talons are designed not for slashing but for anchoring.

A Stoneheart can drive its claws deep into mountainsides, glaciers, or bedrock and remain immovable even against tremendous force.

Many legends claim that entire landslides have broken around a determined Stoneheart rather than move it.

### Geode Fractures

The fault lines running throughout a Stoneheart's body are not empty spaces.

Within these seams grow vast crystalline formations hidden beneath their stone armor.

When calm, these crystals remain mostly concealed.

When a Stoneheart enters a state of absolute conviction, brilliant neon-blue light begins to shine from deep within the fractures.

The glow illuminates every crack and seam across their bodies.

In darkness, a resolute Stoneheart resembles a mountain split apart to reveal a blazing crystal heart.

### Eyes of Living Stone

Stoneheart eyes possess no visible pupils or irises.

Instead, they glow with the steady radiance of rare cobalt crystals.

Their neon-blue gaze never flickers.

Never wavers.

Never rushes.

Many who meet a Stoneheart's eyes describe the experience as strangely humbling.

Their gaze carries the weight of geological time itself.

As though the Dragon has already witnessed centuries pass and remains entirely untroubled by the next.

### Breath of Mineral Dust

When a Stoneheart exhales, they do not release smoke or flame.

Instead, they vent immense clouds of pulverized gemstone dust, crystalline fragments, and pressurized mineral particles.

In darkness, the glowing blue crystals suspended within the cloud create the appearance of a luminous subterranean nebula.

Though beautiful, the phenomenon can be devastating.

The microscopic crystal particles possess razor-sharp edges capable of grinding away stone and steel alike.

### The Cathedral Crest

The horns of a Stoneheart grow upward in branching formations resembling stalagmites, mountain peaks, and the vaulted arches of ancient cathedrals.

These structures often curve inward slightly, creating the appearance of a crown carved from living stone.

Among Stonehearts, the size and complexity of a Dragon's crest is considered a reflection of experience and perseverance rather than status.

The oldest Dragons possess horn formations so intricate they resemble entire mountain ranges.

### The Sacred Canopy

Stonehearts spend extended periods in stillness and contemplation, particularly during the [[Summit Vigil]].

As a result, elder Dragons often become hosts to rare alpine ecosystems.

White lichens, mountain mosses, crystalline fungi, and resilient high-altitude flowers take root within the crevices of their armor.

Some ancient Stonehearts carry entire living gardens across their backs.

Among the Brood, these growths are viewed as symbols of harmony between endurance and life.

### The Echoing Throat

The vocal structures of Stonehearts contain naturally resonant crystalline chambers.

When they speak, their voices possess a deep echoing quality similar to sound reverberating through vast caverns.

Even a quiet statement from a Stoneheart can seem to linger in the air long after the words have been spoken.

Many listeners describe their voices as carrying the weight of mountains.

### Geological Lineages

Stoneheart coloration varies dramatically between bloodlines.

Some possess pristine white marble armor crossed with elegant gray veins.

Others resemble rugged black basalt, dark volcanic slate, green serpentine stone, or polished granite.

These geological distinctions often serve as visible reminders of ancient ancestral lineages.

No two Stoneheart families appear exactly alike.

### The Patina of Time

Young Stonehearts possess rough, jagged armor resembling freshly broken bedrock.

As they age and endure centuries of weather, hardship, and experience, their plates gradually become smoother and more refined.

The process resembles stone shaped by wind, water, and time.

Ancient Stonehearts often possess armor polished to a marble-like sheen despite retaining their immense durability.

Among the Brood, this transformation is viewed as a physical manifestation of wisdom earned through perseverance.

### Visual Identity

A Stoneheart should appear less like a Dragon wearing stone armor and more like a living mountain shaped by the passage of ages.

Their bodies embody permanence, conviction, and endurance.

They do not resist time.

They outlast it.

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Stonehearts are known as protectors.

Where others seek change, Stonehearts seek preservation.

Where others move forward, Stonehearts ensure that nothing worth keeping is left behind.

This reputation has earned them widespread respect throughout the Wyrmreach.

Many Dragons view Stonehearts as dependable guardians capable of withstanding hardships that would break lesser beings.

A common saying among other Broods states:

> "If a Stoneheart stands before you, something worth protecting lies behind them."

---

## Culture

Stoneheart culture values:

- Stewardship
- Responsibility
- Patience
- Reliability
- Protection
- Duty

Stonehearts believe that strength carries responsibility.

The powerful are expected to protect the vulnerable.

The wise are expected to guide the inexperienced.

The enduring are expected to preserve what matters.

Though often viewed as conservative by other Broods, Stonehearts do not oppose change outright.

Rather, they believe change should occur carefully and with consideration for what may be lost.

---

## Temperament

Stonehearts are known for their calm and deliberate nature.

They rarely make decisions hastily.

Instead, they observe, evaluate, and reflect before acting.

Unlike the [[Ashwing]] Brood, whose frustrations often build toward eruption, Stonehearts respond to pressure with increasing resolve.

The greater the resistance they encounter, the firmer their convictions become.

Once a Stoneheart has truly committed to a course of action, convincing them otherwise can prove extraordinarily difficult.

Among Dragonkind it is often said:

> "Stonehearts do not resist pressure. They become it."

---

Throughout Dragonkind, Stonehearts are renowned as:

### Guardians

Protectors of sacred places.

Caretakers of ancient traditions and responsibilities.

The Dragons most willing to stand between danger and those unable to defend themselves.

---

## First Scale Selection

### The Summit Vigil

When a Stoneheart First Scale passes, candidates undergo an ancient succession ritual known as the Summit Vigil.

The trial requires each candidate to ascend one of the sacred peaks of the Great Lithic Spine alone.

There they remain in solitude.

Days.

Months.

Sometimes longer.

During the Vigil, candidates are expected to reflect upon duty, stewardship, and leadership.

The trial tests patience, judgment, and conviction rather than physical strength.

Upon descending from the summit, the candidates are evaluated by Stoneheart elders and the [[Dragonkind/Traditions/The Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon deemed most worthy becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Stonehearts have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Foundation]]
- [[Endurance]]
- [[Harvest]]
- [[Depths]]

However, Stonehearts possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Stonehearts have remained among the most dependable lineages within Dragonkind.

Their guardianship has preserved sacred places.

Their stewardship has preserved ancient traditions.

Their resolve has preserved countless lives.

Though they rarely seek recognition, the influence of the Stoneheart Brood can be found throughout the history of the Wyrmreach.

Like the mountains they call home, they endure.
