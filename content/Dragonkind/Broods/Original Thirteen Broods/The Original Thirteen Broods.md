# The Original Thirteen Broods

## Overview

The Original Thirteen Broods are the oldest and most revered lineages within Dragonkind.

According to draconic tradition, these thirteen bloodlines emerged during the earliest days of creation and became the foundation upon which all later Broods would develop.

More importantly, the Original Thirteen are believed to serve as the living Reality Anchors through which creation stabilizes many of its foundational physical systems.

Though many younger Broods have arisen throughout history, no additional Reality Anchors have ever emerged.

For this reason, the Original Thirteen occupy a unique place within Dragon society.

Their significance extends beyond ancestry, age, or influence.

They are regarded as essential components of reality itself.

---

## The First Lineages

Dragon tradition teaches that the Original Thirteen emerged alongside the earliest generations of Dragons.

While scholars debate the precise nature of their origins, all agree that the Original Thirteen predate every modern Brood.

Many younger lineages can trace portions of their ancestry back to one or more of these ancient bloodlines.

As a result, the Original Thirteen are often viewed as the roots from which the greater family of Dragonkind grew.

According to the oldest traditions preserved by the Echoes of Scale, the First Thirteen were created when reality itself responded to the emergence of the Primordials.

Where the Primordials embodied the cosmic truths that define existence, the First Thirteen became the physical stabilizers through which reality learned to endure.

---

## Legacy

The influence of the Original Thirteen extends far beyond their individual territories.

Across countless ages they have produced:

- Regents
    
- First Scales
    
- Heroes
    
- Scholars
    
- Explorers
    
- Guardians
    

Some have played pivotal roles in events such as the War of Dominion.

Others are remembered through traditions preserved by the Echoes of Scale.

Though they differ greatly in culture and temperament, all thirteen lineages helped shape the identity of Dragonkind itself.

Unlike younger lineages, the Original Thirteen are believed to possess a direct connection to the earliest moments of creation itself.

Each serves as a Reality Anchor, stabilizing a fundamental physical system necessary for reality's continued existence.

Because of this responsibility, the health of the Original Thirteen has long been monitored by the Conclave of Scales and safeguarded through traditions older than recorded history.

The weakening of an Original Brood is considered a matter of concern not only for Dragonkind, but for creation itself.

## The Original Thirteen

### [[Stoneheart]]

**Title:** The Brood of Foundation

**Reality Anchor:** Foundation

**Homeland:** The Great Lithic Spine

Stonehearts serve as the Reality Anchor of geological stability, tectonic integrity, and the enduring foundations upon which creation is built. They are widely respected throughout Dragonkind for their resilience, patience, and unwavering sense of duty.

---

### [[Tideborn]]

**Title:** The Brood of Flow

**Reality Anchor:** Flow

**Homeland:** The Worldreef Expanse

The Tideborn embody movement, circulation, and connection. Their Anchor governs the great currents through which systems, peoples, and forces travel throughout creation.

---

### [[Stormclaw]]

**Title:** The Brood of Atmosphere

**Reality Anchor:** Atmosphere

**Homeland:** The Arc-Light Rift

Stormclaws embody weather, pressure, lightning, and the restless energies of the sky. Among Dragonkind they are respected for their decisiveness and feared for the destructive power they command.

---

### [[Winterborn]]

**Title:** The Brood of Preservation

**Reality Anchor:** Preservation

**Homeland:** The Everfrost Expanse

Winterborn safeguard dormancy, equilibrium, and the natural pauses that allow creation to endure. Their philosophy teaches that survival often requires patience rather than action.

---

### [[Wildroot]]

**Title:** The Brood of Adaptation

**Reality Anchor:** Adaptation

**Homeland:** The Verdant Expanse

Wildroots embody growth through change. They serve as creation's reminder that life survives not through strength alone, but through its ability to adapt.

---

### [[Ashwing]]

**Title:** The Brood of Transformation

**Reality Anchor:** Transformation

**Homeland:** The Brimstone Veil

Ashwings represent destruction, renewal, and rebirth. They understand that some things must end so that new things may begin.

---

### [[Dawnfire]]

**Title:** The Brood of Radiance

**Reality Anchor:** Radiance

**Homeland:** The Dawnspire Expanse

Dawnfires embody illumination, vitality, and the life-giving energies that sustain creation. Throughout Dragonkind they are regarded as symbols of hope and renewal.

---

### [[Emberveil]]

**Title:** The Brood of Continuity

**Reality Anchor:** Continuity

**Homeland:** The Cinder Veil

Emberveils preserve memory, history, and knowledge. They believe that wisdom must survive beyond those who first discover it.

---

### [[Ironcrest]]

**Title:** The Brood of Structure

**Reality Anchor:** Structure

**Homeland:** The Everwatch Bastions

Ironcrests embody stability, reinforcement, and endurance under pressure. They are among the most trusted and respected lineages within Dragonkind.

---

### [[Moonscale]]

**Title:** The Brood of Cycles

**Reality Anchor:** Cycles

**Homeland:** The Astral Glades

Moonscales embody recurring patterns, natural rhythms, and celestial cycles. Their wisdom often centers upon balance, timing, and perspective.

---

### [[Skyfang]]

**Title:** The Brood of Gravity

**Reality Anchor:** Gravity

**Homeland:** The Skyreach

Skyfangs embody ascent, descent, momentum, and gravitational balance. They are known for their relentless pursuit of greater heights and deeper understanding.

---

### [[Deepscale]]

**Title:** The Brood of Shadow

**Reality Anchor:** Shadow

**Homeland:** The Sunless Reach

Deepscales embody darkness, concealment, boundaries, and the unseen. Often misunderstood by outsiders, they safeguard many of creation's oldest secrets.

---

### [[Starbound]]

**Title:** The Brood of Horizons

**Reality Anchor:** Horizons

**Homeland:** The Infinite Expanse

Starbounds embody discovery, expansion, and the drive to seek what lies beyond the known. They are among Dragonkind's greatest explorers and visionaries.

---

## Relationship to Dragonkind

The Original Thirteen are often regarded as the oldest living expressions of draconic culture.

Yet they do not stand apart from Dragonkind.

They are Dragonkind.

Their histories, rivalries, achievements, and traditions are inseparable from the story of the Dragons as a whole.

Even today, many of the customs practiced throughout the Wyrmreach originated within one or more of the Original Thirteen.

Though their responsibilities are immense, they are not rulers.

They are stewards.

Their purpose is not dominion over creation.

Their purpose is to ensure creation continues to endure.
