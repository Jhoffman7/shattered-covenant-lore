# Deepscale

## The Brood of the Uncanny

### Common Name

**The Hollow Eyes**

---

## Overview

The Deepscale Brood is among the most enigmatic lineages within [[Dragonkind/Dragonkind]].

Masters of the abyssal territory known as the [[Sunless Reach]], the Deepscales are renowned for their silence, restraint, and unsettling connection to Darkness and Concealment

The Deepscales anchor the hidden places of reality.

They govern shadow, concealment, obscurity, and the veiled depths of existence.

Where Dawnfire brings illumination, Deepscale preserves mystery.

Not all things are meant to stand beneath the light.

Some truths must remain buried.

Some places must remain hidden.

The Deepscales ensure reality retains both.

Unlike the other Broods, the Deepscales do not seek understanding of every mystery.

They understand that some truths are dangerous.

Some secrets are best forgotten.

And some discoveries carry consequences beyond comprehension.

For countless generations, the Deepscales have served as guardians of forgotten places, hidden knowledge, and ancient mysteries that few Dragons dare approach.

Many Dragons respect them.

Many distrust them.

Nearly all find them unsettling.

---

## Homeland

### The Sunless Reach

The ancestral territory of the Deepscale Brood lies deep beneath the surface of the [[Wyrmreach]].

Known as the Sunless Reach, it is an immense subterranean realm of endless caverns, abyssal chasms, forgotten tunnels, and ancient darkness.

No sunlight reaches these depths.

The region is characterized by:

- Vast underground oceans
- Endless cavern networks
- Abyssal trenches
- Bioluminescent flora
- Ancient crystal formations
- Forgotten ruins
- Bottomless chasms

Many portions of the Sunless Reach remain unexplored even by the Deepscales themselves.

The deeper one travels, the older and stranger the world becomes.

Among Dragonkind, few places inspire more unease.

---

## Sacred Landmark

### The Black Trench

At the heart of the Sunless Reach lies a colossal abyss known only as the Black Trench.

No site is more sacred to the Deepscales.

No site is more feared.

The Black Trench descends beyond known depths.

No Dragon has ever successfully mapped its full extent.

Ancient legends claim the Trench predates Dragonkind itself.

Some believe it conceals a wound left behind during creation.

Others claim something ancient sleeps beneath its depths.

The Deepscales neither confirm nor deny such stories.

What is known is that they guard the Trench with unwavering vigilance.

---

## Appearance

Deepscale Dragons possess some of the most unsettling and unnatural forms within Dragonkind.

They do not resemble creatures adapted to darkness.

They resemble creatures born from it.

Long, silent, and impossibly graceful, Deepscales move through the world with an eerie absence of sound and presence.

Where many Dragons command attention through power, beauty, or majesty, Deepscales evoke discomfort.

Not because they are threatening.

Because something about them feels fundamentally wrong.

Common traits include:

- Velvet-black scales
- Oversized white eyes
- Elongated, abyssal anatomy
- Living shadow anomalies
- Bioluminescent photophores
- Sensory barbels
- Silent movement
- Atmospheric pressure distortions

Many observers describe Deepscales as appearing less like Dragons and more like things that should not exist beneath open skies.

### Velvet-Black Absorbency

The scales of a Deepscale possess an extraordinary light-devouring quality.

Their hides resemble the abyssal skin of deep-sea predators or the darkest depths of the Black Trench itself.

Rather than reflecting illumination, their scales absorb it.

In dim environments, the boundaries of their bodies become difficult to distinguish.

Their exact size, posture, and shape often appear uncertain.

Even experienced Dragons occasionally struggle to determine where a Deepscale ends and the surrounding darkness begins.

### The Non-Euclidean Silhouette

Deepscales possess unusually elongated bodies and slender proportions.

Their limbs bend at slightly unnatural angles.

Their necks appear too long.

Their wings fold too tightly.

Their movements seem too smooth.

When walking, they glide forward with a fluid, almost liquid grace.

The absence of sound only heightens the discomfort.

Many witnesses report that their minds struggle to fully process a Deepscale's movements.

### Living Shadow Anomalies

A Deepscale's shadow is not entirely governed by ordinary light.

The shadow often behaves independently from the Dragon itself.

It may stretch in directions inconsistent with surrounding illumination.

It may appear larger than the Dragon casting it.

It may lag slightly behind their movements.

Some observers claim their shadows occasionally continue moving after the Dragon has stopped.

Whether these phenomena are natural, magical, or psychological remains unknown.

Most Deepscales refuse to discuss the matter.

### Eyes of the Abyss

The eyes of a Deepscale are among the most recognizable features within Dragonkind.

Massive, perfectly round, and entirely white, they contain no visible pupils, irises, or blood vessels.

Their gaze appears fixed.

Unblinking.

Empty.

Because there is no visible pupil to track, it is impossible to determine precisely where a Deepscale is looking.

Many individuals report the unsettling sensation that the Dragon is simultaneously staring directly at them and through them.

The oldest Deepscales often possess eyes resembling polished moonstone or pearl.

Cold.

Featureless.

Endlessly watchful.

### Phantom Bioluminescence

Rows of recessed photophores run along the neck, underbelly, wings, and tail of every Deepscale.

These organs emit pale cyan, ghostly blue, or faint violet light.

Unlike the comforting radiance of a Moonscale, the glow of a Deepscale feels predatory.

The lights pulse rhythmically.

Slowly.

Hypnotically.

Many creatures find themselves unconsciously staring at the patterns.

Among the Brood, these markings are often referred to as Phantom Lights.

### The Crushing Atmosphere

A Deepscale's presence subtly alters the environment around them.

Ambient light seems dimmer.

The air feels colder.

Breathing becomes slightly more difficult.

Sounds appear muted.

Many Dragons describe the sensation as standing beneath immense depths of water despite remaining on dry land.

The effect is strongest among elder Deepscales and those who have completed the [[Abyssal Descent]].

### Sensory Barbels

Rather than conventional horns, Deepscales possess long segmented sensory tendrils known as barbels.

These structures constantly twitch and shift in response to vibrations, heat, magical currents, and subtle environmental changes.

The barbels allow Deepscales to navigate the deepest regions of the [[Black Trench]] without relying upon sight.

Many Dragons compare them to the sensory organs of abyssal sea creatures.

### The Gaping Maw

Though rarely displayed, a Deepscale's jaws possess an unnerving degree of flexibility.

Their mouths open far wider than those of most Dragons.

Rows of fine needle-like teeth line the interior.

The appearance is reminiscent of abyssal predators dwelling within the deepest oceans.

Fortunately, Deepscale culture places tremendous value upon restraint.

Most keep their mouths closed almost constantly.

This self-control only adds to their unsettling presence.

### The Sunless Spectrum

Deepscale coloration reflects the darkness of the deepest abyssal environments.

Common shades include:

- Velvet black
- Deep indigo
- Bruised plum
- Petroleum violet
- Midnight blue

Their bioluminescent markings provide the only significant contrast against these dark tones.

When illuminated directly, portions of their scales reveal subtle oily sheens reminiscent of deep ocean waters.

### The Changed Ones

The effects of centuries spent guarding the deepest mysteries of creation become visible upon elder Deepscales.

Patches of scales gradually lose their dark coloration and become chalk-white.

These formations resemble fossilized coral, weathered bone, or calcified stone.

The phenomenon is especially common among Dragons who have completed the [[Abyssal Descent]] or served long vigil near the Great Vigil.

Many believe these pale markings represent physical manifestations of prolonged exposure to abyssal forces.

### The Blind Veil

The oldest Deepscales eventually develop a thin pearlescent film across their eyes.

To outside observers, they appear blind.

Yet these Dragons move with flawless certainty.

Never stumbling.

Never hesitating.

Never becoming lost.

Among the Brood, this condition is known as the Blind Veil.

Many consider it a sign that the Dragon has begun perceiving truths beyond ordinary sight.

### Visual Identity

A Deepscale should appear less like a Dragon dwelling within darkness and more like darkness itself given form.

Silent.

Ancient.

Uncanny.

Their very existence serves as a reminder that creation still contains mysteries best left unexplored.

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Deepscales are known as keepers of uncomfortable truths.

They are often consulted when ancient mysteries, forgotten places, or dangerous discoveries are involved.

Many Dragons believe that if a Deepscale advises caution, there is usually a reason.

Common sayings include:

> "If a Deepscale tells you to stop digging, stop digging."

> "Some things are buried for a reason."

Though respected, they are rarely trusted completely.

Their silence often leaves others wondering how much they truly know.

---

## Culture

Deepscale culture values:

- Restraint
- Observation
- Responsibility
- Patience
- Secrecy
- Vigilance

Unlike many lineages, Deepscales do not view knowledge as inherently good.

To them, knowledge is a responsibility.

Some truths should be preserved.

Others should remain hidden.

The ability to determine the difference is considered one of the highest virtues within their society.

Many Deepscales spend decades safeguarding ancient locations, forgotten records, and mysteries they may never fully understand themselves.

---

## Temperament

Deepscales are quiet by nature.

They observe more than they speak.

Listen more than they explain.

Watch more than they intervene.

As situations grow more dangerous, they often become increasingly silent.

Among Dragonkind, it is often said:

> "The quieter the Deepscale becomes, the more worried you should be."

Unlike many Broods, Deepscales rarely display strong emotions openly.

This can make them appear cold or detached.

In truth, they simply prefer caution over reaction.

## First Scale Selection

### The Abyssal Descent

When a Deepscale First Scale passes, candidates undertake an ancient succession ritual known as the Abyssal Descent.

Each candidate descends alone into the deepest regions of the Sunless Reach.

No companions accompany them.

No guidance is provided.

No records are kept.

What occurs during the Descent is never discussed afterward.

Those who return are forever changed by the experience.

Upon their return, the candidates are judged by Deepscale elders and the [[Dragonkind/Traditions/The Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon deemed most worthy becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## The Great Vigil

Among all the responsibilities entrusted to the Deepscales, none is greater than the Great Vigil.

Deep within the Black Trench lies something ancient.

Something forgotten.

Something the Deepscales have guarded for longer than recorded history.

Even the Brood's own records offer few answers.

Many younger Deepscales spend centuries preparing for service within the Vigil.

Those chosen are expected to watch, record, and report.

Never interfere.

Never descend too far.

Never attempt to uncover what has been deliberately forgotten.

The purpose of the Vigil is not understanding.

It is containment.

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Deepscales have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Depths]]
- [[Crossroads]]
- [[Journey]]
- [[Endurance]]

However, Deepscales possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Deepscales have remained among the most mysterious lineages within Dragonkind.

Their vigilance has prevented disasters.

Their caution has preserved knowledge.

Their watchfulness has protected secrets that may never be fully understood.

Though few understand them, their role within Dragonkind remains indispensable.

For some truths are too dangerous to ignore.

And too dangerous to reveal.
