# Skyfang

## The Brood of Endless Horizons**

### Common Name

**The Skyward**

---

## Overview

The Skyfang Brood is among the most ambitious and visionary lineages within [[Dragonkind/Dragonkind]].

Masters of the soaring realm known as the [[Skyreach]], the Skyfangs embody aspiration, leadership, vision, excellence, and the relentless pursuit of greater heights.

Unlike many Dragons who focus upon preserving what exists, enduring hardship, uncovering forgotten truths, or inspiring hope, the Skyfangs concern themselves with potential.

What can be achieved.

What can be built.

What can be discovered beyond the next horizon.

To a Skyfang, no accomplishment represents an ending.

Every summit reveals another peak.

Every horizon reveals another beyond it.

Every achievement creates an opportunity for something greater.

For countless generations, Skyfangs have served as pioneers, leaders, explorers, innovators, and Regents whose actions push Dragonkind toward ever greater possibilities.

Where others ask whether something can be done, Skyfangs ask how much farther it can go.

---

## Homeland

### The Skyreach

The ancestral territory of the Skyfang Brood is a breathtaking realm known as the Skyreach.

A land of impossible altitude and endless horizons, the Skyreach exists high above much of the [[Wyrmreach]], where clouds become seas and mountains float among the heavens.

The region is characterized by:

- Floating mountain chains
- Endless cloud oceans
- Suspended plateaus
- Vertical cliff kingdoms
- Sky bridges
- Permanent jet streams
- Peaks rising above the weather itself

Many locations within the Skyreach can only be reached through flight.

Some remain inaccessible even to Dragons.

To outsiders, these unreachable places represent barriers.

To the Skyfangs, they represent invitations.

The Skyreach is not merely a homeland.

It is a challenge.

---

## Sacred Landmark

### The Summit Crown

No place is more sacred to the Skyfang Brood than the Summit Crown.

A colossal floating mountain suspended impossibly high above the Skyreach.

According to Skyfang tradition, no Dragon has ever reached its true summit.

Every expedition discovers another ascent beyond.

Every achievement reveals another challenge.

Whether this phenomenon is magical, symbolic, or literal remains unknown.

Most Skyfangs consider the answer irrelevant.

The lesson is what matters.

There is always another horizon.

There is always another summit.

There is always farther to climb.

Many Skyfangs spend their entire lives attempting to reach heights that no Dragon has yet attained.

To them, the pursuit itself is sacred.

---
## Appearance

Skyfang Dragons possess some of the most elegant and distinctive forms within Dragonkind.

Everything about their bodies is built for ascent.

Where many Dragons appear ancient, imposing, or indomitable, Skyfangs appear purposeful.

Every feature seems designed to climb higher, fly farther, and reach places others cannot.

Common traits include:

- Pristine white, sky-blue, silver, and gold coloration
- Sleek aerodynamic frames
- Massive high-aspect-ratio wings
- Diamond-weave scales
- Forward-sweeping crest horns
- Sun-bright eyes
- Atmospheric vapor trails
- Cloud-like manes in old age

Many observers describe Skyfangs as resembling living arrows pointed toward the heavens.

### The Arrowhead Silhouette

The most recognizable feature of a Skyfang is its unmistakable silhouette.

Their bodies possess a naturally aerodynamic profile designed to minimize resistance during flight.

Most notably, their horns sweep forward and upward rather than backward.

These formations create a sharp crest resembling the prow of a ship cutting through the sky.

To other Dragons, this gives the impression that every Skyfang is perpetually moving toward something beyond the horizon.

### Diamond-Weave Scales

Unlike the armored plates of Stonehearts or the stratified glacial scales of Winterborn, Skyfang scales form tightly interlocking diamond-shaped structures.

The result is a remarkably smooth and streamlined surface.

Many possess a metallic or ceramic appearance despite remaining fully biological.

These scales allow Skyfangs to endure extreme altitudes and incredible flight speeds.

### Quill-Vane Wings

The wings of a Skyfang are among the most sophisticated flight structures found within Dragonkind.

Rather than terminating in traditional membranes, the trailing edges of their wings are lined with rigid ivory-colored quills.

These structures can flare outward to capture thermal currents or lock tightly together during high-speed dives.

This adaptation allows Skyfangs to perform aerial maneuvers impossible for most other Dragons.

### Dawn-Catcher Scales

Skyfang scales possess a unique iridescent quality.

Although their primary coloration varies, the edges of many scales permanently retain the golden-orange hues of sunrise and sunset.

As though they have captured fragments of distant horizons within themselves.

When viewed from the proper angle, a Skyfang appears perpetually illuminated by dawn.

### Prismatic Corona

When flying at extreme altitudes or channeling significant power, Skyfangs generate a phenomenon known as the Prismatic Corona.

Ice crystals form within the thin air surrounding them, creating shimmering rings of refracted light around their horns, wings, and bodies.

The resulting halos often resemble miniature rainbows suspended in the sky.

Among Dragonkind, the appearance of a Prismatic Corona is considered a sign of exceptional skill and mastery.

### Eyes of the Upper Sky

Skyfang eyes possess no visible pupils.

Instead they appear as molten gold, radiant silver, or pure white light.

Many who meet a Skyfang's gaze describe the sensation as unsettling.

Not because the Dragon is looking at them.

Because it feels as though the Dragon is focused upon something far beyond them.

Some distant horizon only they can see.

### Compression Vents

Elegant vent-like structures run along a Skyfang's neck, shoulders, and chest.

These biological adaptations allow them to regulate the tremendous pressures encountered during extreme flight.

At rest, faint wisps of condensation drift from these vents.

During rapid ascents or dives, they release dramatic vapor trails that streak behind the Dragon like banners.

### Windswept Scars

The scars carried by elder Skyfangs rarely resemble wounds from battle.

Instead, many appear as perfectly smooth grooves carved into their scales.

These marks are created by centuries of exposure to violent winds, turbulent currents, and the unforgiving conditions of the upper atmosphere.

Among the Skyward, such scars are regarded as marks of experience and achievement.

### Cloud-Manes

As Skyfangs age, the scales running along their necks and spines begin to separate into countless fine filaments.

These structures create the appearance of flowing white manes composed of cloud and mist.

The mane constantly shifts in response to surrounding air currents, often giving elder Skyfangs an ethereal and majestic presence.

### Shattered Crowns

Young Skyfangs possess pristine crest structures.

The oldest members of the Brood often bear crowns that appear fractured, weathered, and partially crystallized.

These formations are not signs of injury.

They are the result of repeated journeys toward the Summit Crown and the highest reaches of the atmosphere.

Among the Skyward, a shattered crown is considered one of the greatest marks of distinction a Dragon can possess.

### Visual Identity

A Skyfang should appear less like a Dragon adapted for flight and more like the living embodiment of ascent itself.

Every aspect of their form reflects their belief that there is always another horizon waiting beyond the one currently visible.

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Skyfangs are renowned for their ambition, leadership, and vision.

They are often viewed as pioneers and trailblazers who constantly push boundaries others accept as fixed.

When confronted with limitations, Skyfangs rarely ask how to endure them.

They ask how to overcome them.

Common sayings include:

> "A Skyfang sees farther."

> "Every horizon belongs to the Skyward eventually."

---

## Culture

Skyfang culture values:

- Aspiration
- Excellence
- Leadership
- Vision
- Achievement
- Self-improvement

Failure is accepted.

Complacency is not.

Skyfangs believe growth is among the highest callings an individual can pursue.

Many dedicate centuries to mastering a single skill.

Others spend their lives pursuing impossible goals.

Among the Skyward, achievement is respected.

The pursuit of achievement is revered.

---

## Temperament

Skyfangs are confident, driven, and future-oriented.

They rarely dwell upon what has already been accomplished.

Instead, they focus upon what remains possible.

Their response to adversity is often summarized as:

### Pressure → Aspiration

The greater the challenge becomes, the higher they aim.

Many Skyfangs view obstacles not as barriers but as opportunities to grow.

Among Dragonkind it is often said:

> "The mountain exists to be climbed."

---

## What They Are Known For

Throughout Dragonkind, Skyfangs are renowned as:

### Leaders

Those who guide others toward greater futures.

### Trailblazers

Those who travel where others have not.

### Visionaries

Those who see possibilities others overlook.

### The Keepers of Vision

The Dragons who remind the world that there is always another horizon.

---

## First Scale Selection

### The Highest Flight

When a Skyfang First Scale passes, candidates undertake an ancient succession ritual known as the Highest Flight.

Each candidate chooses a goal considered impossible.

Not merely difficult.

Impossible.

The objective is not necessarily to succeed.

It is to demonstrate the courage to pursue greatness despite uncertainty.

Candidates are judged not solely by their results, but by their vision, determination, and willingness to strive beyond accepted limits.

Upon completion, candidates are evaluated by Skyfang elders and the [[Dragonkind/Traditions/The Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon who best embodies aspiration and vision becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Skyfangs have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Horizons]]
- [[Journey]]
- [[Storms]]
- [[Crossroads]]

However, Skyfangs possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Skyfangs have challenged assumptions, explored the unknown, and pursued heights others considered unreachable.

They have led expeditions.

Founded kingdoms.

Discovered new paths.

And inspired others to pursue ambitions greater than themselves.

Their legacy is not measured by where they stood.

It is measured by how far they climbed.

As long as the Skyward endure, Dragonkind believes there will always be another horizon waiting to be reached.
