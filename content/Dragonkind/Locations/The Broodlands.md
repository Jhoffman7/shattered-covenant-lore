# The Broodlands

## Overview

The Broodlands are the ancestral homeland of Dragonkind and the cultural heart of draconic civilization.

Protected by ancient creation magic older than recorded history, the Broodlands have served as the home of the Elder Broods since the earliest ages of creation.

Though the Broodlands are often spoken of as a single region, they are composed of thirteen ancestral domains known as Hearths, each associated with one of the Elder Broods.

Together, these Hearths form one of the oldest continuously inhabited civilizations in existence.

To Dragons, the Broodlands are more than a homeland.

They are a living connection to their origins, their traditions, and the legacy of the First Regents.

---

## Origins

According to Dragon traditions, the Broodlands were established during the age of the First Regents.

Following their emergence from the [[The Cradle of Scales]], the First Regents founded the Thirteen Elder Broods and established the ancestral domains that would eventually become known as the Hearths.

Over countless ages, these domains grew into centers of culture, tradition, and draconic identity.

Though Dragonkind would later spread throughout creation, the Broodlands remained the spiritual center of their civilization.

Every Dragon ultimately traces their lineage back to one of the Hearths.

---

## The Thirteen Hearths

The Broodlands are composed of thirteen ancestral Hearths.

Each Hearth serves as the traditional homeland of one of the Elder Broods.

The Hearths are not nations in the mortal sense.

They are ancestral domains shaped by lineage, tradition, and stewardship rather than political borders.

## The Convergence

At the heart of the Broodlands lies a region known as the Convergence.

Unlike the Hearths, the Convergence belongs to no single Brood.

Instead, it serves as neutral ground shared by all of Dragonkind.

The Convergence exists as a place of unity, diplomacy, pilgrimage, and cooperation.

Many Dragons regard it as the symbolic center of Dragon civilization.

---

## Wyrmreach

Within the Convergence stands [[Wyrmreach]], the greatest city of Dragonkind.

Wyrmreach serves as the primary gathering place of the Elder Broods and acts as a center of diplomacy, trade, scholarship, and cultural exchange.

Representatives from every Hearth can be found within the city.

The [[The Conclave of Scales]] convenes within Wyrmreach whenever matters affecting Dragonkind as a whole require deliberation.

---

## The Cradle of Scales

Also located within the Convergence is the [[The Cradle of Scales]], the most sacred site in all of Dragonkind.

According to ancient tradition, the Cradle existed before the Elder Broods themselves and served as the place from which the First Regents emerged into creation.

To Dragons, the Cradle represents origin, continuity, and the enduring future of their people.

Its significance transcends individual Broods and unites all Dragons through a shared connection to their beginnings.

---

## Dragonborn in the Broodlands

Dragonborn communities exist throughout the Hearths and have done so for much of recorded history.

Many Dragonborn maintain strong ties to the Elder Broods from which their ancestry descends, often preserving traditions, customs, and cultural practices unique to their lineage.

While some Dragonborn choose to remain within the Broodlands, others venture into the wider Mortal Realm.

These individuals often serve as diplomats, advisors, scholars, merchants, and emissaries, helping maintain connections between Dragonkind and the civilizations beyond the Broodlands.

Many Dragons view such service as an important contribution to creation, allowing Dragonborn to act as bridges between ancient draconic traditions and the rapidly changing mortal world.

---

## Legacy

The Broodlands have endured through ages of triumph, upheaval, and transformation.

Empires have risen and fallen.

Kingdoms have appeared and vanished.

Yet the Hearths remain.

For Dragons, the Broodlands represent permanence in a changing world.

They are the birthplace of the Elder Broods, the home of the Cradle of Scales, and the foundation upon which all of Dragonkind was built.

Regardless of where a Dragon may travel, the Broodlands remain home.
