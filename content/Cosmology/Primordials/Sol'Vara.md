# Sol'Vara

**Title:** The First Light

**Domains:** Creation, Hope, Inspiration, Renewal

**Alignment:** [[The Balance]]

**Symbols:**

- A rising sun with twelve rays
    
- A radiant star emerging from darkness
    
- A golden flame surrounded by a circular halo
    

---

## Overview

Sol'Vara is the Primordial of Creation and the eldest of the First Twelve.

She embodies beginnings, inspiration, invention, hope, and possibility.

Among the Primordials, Sol'Vara is often regarded as the most compassionate and emotionally invested in the lives of both gods and mortals.

Where others see limitations, she sees opportunities.

Where others see obstacles, she sees potential.

Her influence can be found in every act of creation.

Every invention.

Every masterpiece.

Every civilization that dares to imagine a future greater than its present.

Many cultures portray Sol'Vara as the mother of civilization.

Others depict her as the first being to bring light into existence.

Though countless myths disagree on the details, nearly all agree on one thing:

Reality became possible because Sol'Vara believed it could.

---

## Personality

Unlike many of the First Twelve, Sol'Vara remains deeply connected to individuals.

She is curious.

Compassionate.

Optimistic.

At times impulsive.

She often becomes emotionally invested in people, causes, and ideas.

Among her siblings she is known for seeing the best in others, sometimes to a fault.

Her greatest strength is her belief that people can become more than they currently are.

Her greatest weakness is her tendency to trust that others share her hopes.

---

## Before Creation

Ancient myths claim that before reality existed there were only two beings.

[[Sol'Vara]]

And [[Umbros]].

Light and Shadow.

Possibility and Consequence.

Creation and Void.

Whether these stories are literally true remains unknown.

However, nearly every surviving creation myth places Sol'Vara and Umbros at the center of existence's earliest moments.

Many theologians believe their relationship predates reality itself.

---

## Umbros

No relationship has shaped Sol'Vara more than her bond with Umbros.

Though countless stories attempt to describe their connection, none agree entirely on its nature.

Some traditions describe them as lovers.

Others describe them as eternal companions.

Others claim they represent two halves of a greater whole.

Regardless of interpretation, all surviving traditions agree that their bond was unique among the First Twelve.

Where Sol'Vara imagined possibilities, Umbros considered consequences.

Where Sol'Vara expanded, Umbros refined.

Each completed what the other lacked.

---

## The Original Covenant

When debate concerning [[Xyros, The Final Turning]] threatened to divide the First Twelve, Sol'Vara ultimately agreed to the creation of the [[The Original Covenant|Original Covenant]].

She believed reality required stability.

She believed civilization needed time to grow.

Yet she feared the cost.

More specifically:

She feared what the burden would demand of Umbros.

Among all the First Twelve, Sol'Vara objected most strongly when Umbros volunteered to become the Covenant's anchor.

In the end, she accepted his decision.

Not because she agreed with it.

Because she trusted him.

---

## The Forgotten Question

As millennia passed, much of the Covenant's original purpose faded from memory.

Records disappeared.

Traditions changed.

Even divine recollections became fragmented.

Yet one thing remained.

A question.

Again and again, Sol'Vara asked:

> "Has it been long enough?"

Often she could not explain why.

Often she no longer remembered what inspired the question.

Yet some part of her refused to let it die.

A wound without a scar.

A memory without a name.

A promise whose shape had been forgotten.

---

## The Modern Age

In the current age, Sol'Vara finds herself increasingly troubled by the state of Heaven.

She sees uncertainty where others see stability.

Stagnation where others see peace.

Questions where others see answers.

Though she does not fully remember the truth behind her concerns, she increasingly senses that something has gone terribly wrong.

Many within [[The Balance]] quietly look to her for guidance.

Others fear where her questions may eventually lead.

---

## Worship

Sol'Vara is among the most widely revered of the First Twelve.

Her followers commonly include:

- Artists
    
- Inventors
    
- Teachers
    
- Leaders
    
- Craftsmen
    
- Explorers
    
- Founders
    

Temples dedicated to Sol'Vara often emphasize learning, creativity, and personal growth.

Her faith teaches that every individual possesses the ability to create something meaningful.

---

## Common Teachings

- Every beginning matters.
    
- Hope is a form of courage.
    
- Creation requires responsibility.
    
- The future belongs to those willing to build it.
    
- Possibility is a gift meant to be shared.
    

---

## Philosophy
