# Morwyn

**Title:** Lady of Dreams

**Domains:** Dreams, Imagination, Prophecy, Inspiration

**Alignment:** [[The Ascendant]]

**Symbols:**

- A silver moon reflected upon still water
    
- A closed eye surrounded by stars
    
- A feather drifting through a dreamscape
    
- A doorway opening onto an endless horizon
    

---

## Overview

Morwyn is the Primordial of Dreams, Imagination, Prophecy, and Possibility.

She governs the realm where reality and potential meet.

The space between what is and what could be.

Among the First Twelve, Morwyn is often regarded as the most enigmatic.

Even her fellow Primordials sometimes struggle to determine whether she speaks of the present, the future, or a possibility that may never come to pass.

Artists, visionaries, prophets, and dreamers often invoke her name.

Many believe every great creation first exists within Morwyn's domain before entering the waking world.

To Morwyn, imagination is not an escape from reality.

It is one of the forces that shapes it.

---

## Personality

Morwyn is thoughtful, gentle, and difficult to predict.

She rarely speaks directly.

Instead, she often communicates through stories, symbols, dreams, and questions.

Many who seek her guidance leave with more uncertainty than they arrived with.

Yet over time, they frequently discover the answers they needed were hidden within her words.

Unlike Aion, who observes events as they unfold, Morwyn is fascinated by possibility.

She delights in uncertainty.

Not because she enjoys confusion.

Because uncertainty allows creation to become something unexpected.

Among the First Twelve, she is one of the least concerned with control.

---

## The Nature Of Dreams

Most mortals believe dreams are illusions.

Morwyn disagrees.

She teaches that dreams reveal aspects of reality often overlooked by waking minds.

Some dreams are memories.

Some are fears.

Some are hopes.

Some are possibilities that may never come to pass.

The challenge lies in determining which is which.

For this reason, many prophets and seers consider dreams sacred.

They offer glimpses beyond the boundaries of ordinary perception.

---

## Aion

Morwyn shares a unique relationship with [[Aion]].

Both concern themselves with the future.

Yet they approach it from entirely different directions.

Aion observes timelines as they unfold.

Morwyn experiences countless possibilities before they ever become reality.

Some scholars describe their relationship simply:

> Aion remembers what was.
> 
> Morwyn dreams what could be.

Neither description is entirely accurate.

Yet both contain elements of truth.

---

## Velora

Of all the First Twelve, Morwyn is perhaps closest philosophically to [[Velora]].

Both value possibility.

Both believe the future should remain open.

Both distrust excessive certainty.

Many of the visions and prophecies preserved by Velora first emerged through Morwyn's domain.

Though they often work independently, their influence frequently overlaps.

Some theologians refer to them as the Twin Guardians of Possibility.

---

## The Original Covenant

Morwyn supported the creation of the [[The Original Covenant|Original Covenant]].

Reality required stability.

Civilization required time.

The Covenant offered both.

Yet Morwyn never viewed it as permanent.

Dreams cannot be frozen.

Possibilities cannot remain suspended forever.

To Morwyn, the purpose of stability was growth.

Not preservation.

A future that never changes eventually ceases to be a future.

---

## The Dreams Of The Forgotten

Throughout history, Morwyn has witnessed strange dreams that even she struggles to explain.

A shadow standing beside the dawn.

A forgotten throne.

A name no one remembers.

A promise spoken beneath unfamiliar stars.

For countless ages these visions appeared disconnected.

Meaningless fragments drifting through the realm of dreams.

Only recently has Morwyn begun suspecting they share a common source.

Something forgotten.

Someone forgotten.

A truth attempting to remember itself.

---

## The Modern Age

Among the First Twelve, Morwyn may possess the earliest evidence that something is wrong.

Not because she uncovered hidden records.

Not because she solved a mystery.

Because she keeps seeing the same dream.

Again.

And again.

And again.

A sealed door.

A patient figure waiting beyond it.

And a voice asking a question she cannot forget:

> "Has it been long enough?"

Morwyn does not yet understand the dream.

She fears she soon will.

## Worship

Morwyn is commonly revered by:

- Artists
    
- Poets
    
- Storytellers
    
- Visionaries
    
- Prophets
    
- Musicians
    
- Inventors
    

Her temples often function as places of artistic expression, meditation, and reflection.

Many followers keep dream journals as part of their spiritual practice.

---

## Common Teachings

- Every reality begins as an idea.
    
- Possibility is a gift.
    
- Dreams reveal truths hidden from waking eyes.
    
- Imagination shapes the future.
    
- Not every vision is meant to be understood immediately.
