# Thaloris

**Title:** The Ever-Tide

**Domains:** Change, Transformation, Evolution, Adaptation

**Alignment:** [[The Ascendant]]

**Symbols:**

- A river flowing into the sea
    
- A serpent shedding its skin
    
- A leaf changing through the seasons
    
- A spiral emerging from still water
    

---

## Overview

Thaloris is the Primordial of Change, Transformation, Evolution, and Adaptation.

She governs the endless process through which all things become something new.

Where others focus upon what exists, Thaloris concerns herself with what is becoming.

To Thaloris, change is not a disruption of reality.

It is one of reality's most fundamental truths.

Mountains erode.

Empires rise and fall.

People grow older.

Civilizations evolve.

Even the stars themselves transform over time.

Nothing remains unchanged forever.

Among the First Twelve, Thaloris is often viewed as one of the most patient.

She understands that transformation rarely occurs all at once.

Great change is often measured in generations.

---

## Personality

Thaloris is calm, reflective, and remarkably adaptable.

Unlike Kael'Thur, she does not seek conflict.

Unlike Tharos, she does not seek permanence.

She accepts that all things eventually become something different.

Many who speak with Thaloris describe her as serene.

Others find her unsettling.

She rarely clings to certainty.

She rarely fears the unknown.

Instead, she embraces transition as a natural part of existence.

Among the First Twelve, she is perhaps the least threatened by uncertainty.

---

## The Nature Of Change

Many mortals fear change.

Thaloris understands why.

Change often brings uncertainty.

Loss.

Risk.

Yet she teaches that refusing change carries dangers of its own.

A river that ceases to flow becomes stagnant.

A civilization that refuses adaptation eventually declines.

A person who never grows remains trapped by their former self.

For Thaloris, transformation is neither good nor evil.

It is simply inevitable.

The question is not whether change will come.

The question is whether one will grow with it.

---

## Kael'Thur

Among the First Twelve, Thaloris shares one of her closest philosophical relationships with [[Kael'Thur]].

Conflict creates pressure.

Transformation creates growth.

Kael'Thur initiates struggle.

Thaloris guides what emerges from it.

Though their domains often overlap, they approach change from different directions.

Kael'Thur believes adversity creates strength.

Thaloris believes adaptation creates survival.

Together they represent two essential forces of growth.

---

## The Original Covenant

Thaloris supported the creation of the [[The Original Covenant|Original Covenant]].

Reality required stability.

Civilization required time.

The Covenant provided both.

Yet Thaloris never believed stability should become permanence.

The purpose of stability was preparation.

Not preservation.

A seed remains protected only until it is ready to grow.

A child remains guided only until they can walk alone.

To Thaloris, the Covenant was intended to help reality mature.

Not prevent it from evolving.

---

## The Modern Age

Among the First Twelve, Thaloris increasingly views the Covenant as a cocoon that has remained sealed long after its purpose was fulfilled.

She does not believe the world beyond the Covenant will be easy.

She does not believe change guarantees improvement.

Transformation carries risks.

Mistakes.

Failures.

Losses.

Yet she also understands that refusing transformation carries risks of its own.

A reality that refuses to evolve eventually becomes trapped by its own success.

For this reason, Thaloris has become one of the strongest philosophical voices within [[The Ascendant]].

---

## The Anchor Of Change

The fragment of Umbros entrusted to Thaloris became known as the [[The Anchors of Umbros|Anchor of Change]].

Many scholars find this pairing surprising.

Yet Thaloris understands something many others overlook.

Umbros never feared change.

He accepted it.

When he chose to bear the burden of the Covenant, he believed reality would eventually move beyond it.

He believed the future would continue unfolding.

The Anchor preserves this aspect of him.

The willingness to accept that even sacred things must someday transform.

---

## Sol'Vara

Though they rarely agree on methods, Thaloris shares a subtle connection with [[Sol'Vara]].

Creation and transformation are deeply intertwined.

Sol'Vara creates possibilities.

Thaloris guides what those possibilities become.

Many ancient myths portray them working together during the earliest shaping of reality.

One imagined the future.

The other helped it grow.

---

## Worship

Thaloris is commonly revered by:

- Travelers
    
- Explorers
    
- Reformers
    
- Natural Philosophers
    
- Healers
    
- Gardeners
    
- Innovators
    

Her temples are often built near rivers, coastlines, forests, and other places where change is readily observed.

Followers are encouraged to embrace growth and adaptation rather than cling to what has already passed.

---

## Common Teachings

- Change is inevitable.
    
- Growth requires adaptation.
    
- Transformation is not failure.
    
- Stagnation is a hidden danger.
    
- The future belongs to those willing to evolve.
    

---

## Philosophy
