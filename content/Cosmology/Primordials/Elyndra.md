# Elyndra

**Title:** Keeper of Truths

**Domains:** Truth, Knowledge, Revelation

**Alignment:** [[The Concordant]]

**Symbols:**

- An unbroken crystal mirror
    
- A silver eye surrounded by stars
    
- A lantern illuminating a hidden path
    
- A book whose pages cannot be altered
    

---

## Overview

Elyndra is the Primordial of Truth, Knowledge, Revelation, and Understanding.

She embodies the pursuit of reality as it truly exists.

Not as it is remembered.

Not as it is taught.

Not as it is believed.

As it is.

Among the First Twelve, Elyndra serves as one of the greatest patrons of scholars, historians, sages, archivists, investigators, and seekers of knowledge.

She believes that truth possesses inherent value.

Even when painful.

Even when inconvenient.

Even when dangerous.

To Elyndra, ignorance is not merely a lack of knowledge.

It is a barrier between reality and understanding.

---

## Personality

Elyndra is thoughtful, analytical, and intensely curious.

Unlike many of her siblings, she is rarely motivated by emotion.

Instead, she seeks clarity.

Precision.

Understanding.

She does not pursue knowledge for power.

She pursues knowledge because truth deserves to be known.

Many mortals mistakenly assume Elyndra lacks compassion.

The opposite is often true.

She simply believes false comfort ultimately causes greater harm than difficult truths.

Among the First Twelve, she is widely respected for her honesty.

Even by those who disagree with her conclusions.

---

## The Nature Of Truth

Elyndra teaches that truth exists independently of belief.

Reality does not change because someone misunderstands it.

History does not change because it is forgotten.

Facts do not disappear because they are inconvenient.

Truth may be hidden.

Ignored.

Buried.

Forgotten.

Yet it remains.

This belief forms the foundation of her philosophy.

And eventually became the source of her greatest concern.

---

## Umbros

Elyndra's relationship with [[Umbros]] has long been characterized by philosophical disagreement.

Umbros accepts mystery.

Elyndra seeks understanding.

Umbros believes some questions may never be answered.

Elyndra believes every answer can eventually be discovered.

Their debates are legendary among theologians and celestial scholars.

Despite their disagreements, Elyndra has always respected Umbros.

Perhaps because he never lied.

Even when others preferred comforting answers.

---

## The Original Covenant

Elyndra supported the creation of the [[The Original Covenant|Original Covenant]].

The Covenant appeared logical.

Necessary.

Reasonable.

Reality required stability.

Civilization required time.

The agreement made sense.

For much of history, Elyndra viewed the Covenant as one of Heaven's greatest achievements.

She saw no reason to question it.

At first.

---

## The First Contradiction

No one knows precisely when Elyndra first became suspicious.

Most believe it began with a single inconsistency.

A missing record.

A damaged archive.

A historical event that appeared incomplete.

The discrepancy was small.

Insignificant.

Yet it troubled her.

The more she investigated, the more contradictions she discovered.

Records that disagreed with one another.

Ancient texts referencing unknown figures.

Events whose causes no longer existed.

Names that seemed to have vanished entirely.

At first she believed these were errors.

Eventually she realized something far more disturbing.

The historical record itself had been altered.

---

## The Investigation

For thousands of years, Elyndra has quietly pursued a truth she cannot fully see.

She knows something has been removed.

She knows memories have been changed.

She knows records have been altered.

What she does not know is why.

Or by whom.

This uncertainty has become one of the defining struggles of her existence.

For perhaps the first time in history, the Primordial of Truth finds herself confronting a mystery she cannot solve.

---

## Sol'Vara And Velora

Though their motivations differ, Elyndra increasingly finds herself aligned with both [[Sol'Vara]] and [[Velora]].

Sol'Vara feels that something has been forgotten.

Velora preserves possibilities others would erase.

Elyndra seeks the truth hidden beneath both.

None openly coordinate their efforts.

Yet all three have begun moving toward the same unanswered question.

What happened to the missing god?

---

## The Modern Age

Among the First Twelve, Elyndra may possess the clearest understanding that something is fundamentally wrong.

She knows the official history cannot be trusted.

She knows gaps exist within reality's memory.

She knows the Covenant's story is incomplete.

What she lacks are the final pieces.

For this reason, Elyndra has become one of the most dangerous beings in Heaven.

Not because she seeks power.

Because she seeks answers.

And answers have a way of changing everything.

---

## Worship

Elyndra is commonly revered by:

- Scholars
    
- Historians
    
- Scribes
    
- Judges
    
- Investigators
    
- Teachers
    
- Archivists
    

Her temples often function as libraries, universities, archives, and centers of learning.

Followers are encouraged to question assumptions and pursue understanding throughout their lives.

---

## Common Teachings

- Truth exists whether it is believed or not.
    
- Ignorance is the beginning of learning, not its end.
    
- Understanding requires humility.
    
- Questions are often more valuable than answers.
    
- Knowledge carries responsibility.
    

---

## Philosophy

> Truth exists whether it is welcomed or not.
