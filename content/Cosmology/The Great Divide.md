# The Great Divide

The Great Divide is the oldest and most significant philosophical conflict among the First Twelve.

Though often mistaken for a political disagreement, the Divide concerns a much deeper question:

> What should happen when a thing has fulfilled its purpose?

The answer to this question has shaped the history of Heaven, Dragons, and Mortals alike.

Entire civilizations have unknowingly inherited beliefs born from the Divide.

Many of the greatest events in history can be traced back to its influence.

---

## Origins

The Divide began during the Age Before Ages.

At the time, reality existed in a state of constant transformation.

Kingdoms rose and fell rapidly.

Magic changed unpredictably.

Civilizations struggled to endure.

The emergence of [[Xyros, The Final Turning]] forced the First Twelve to confront a difficult truth.

Nothing remained unchanged forever.

The response to this truth divided Heaven.

From that division emerged three philosophical alignments:

- [[The Concordant]]
    
- [[The Ascendant]]
    
- [[The Balance]]
    

Though the Covenant temporarily united the Twelve, the Divide never truly ended.

It merely became dormant.

---

## The Central Question

The Great Divide ultimately revolves around a single question:

> When the purpose of a thing has been fulfilled, should it remain or should it change?

The answer depends upon whom one asks.

---

## The Concordant

The Concordant believes that stability is civilization's greatest achievement.

A system that functions should be preserved.

A structure that protects should endure.

A tradition that works should not be discarded simply because it is old.

The Concordant fears unnecessary change.

Its members believe that the destruction of functioning institutions often creates suffering without guaranteeing improvement.

To the Concordant, permanence is not a flaw.

It is a virtue.

---

## The Ascendant

The Ascendant believes that all things must eventually evolve.

No system remains beneficial forever.

No institution remains perfect forever.

No age lasts forever.

The Ascendant fears stagnation more than uncertainty.

Its members believe that refusing change ultimately creates greater suffering than accepting it.

To the Ascendant, growth is impossible without transformation.

---

## The Balance

The Balance believes both sides possess part of the truth.

Some things deserve preservation.

Others deserve renewal.

Wisdom lies in understanding the difference.

The Balance seeks harmony between continuity and transformation.

Its members often act as mediators between the other two alignments.

However, the Covenant crisis has placed extraordinary strain upon this philosophy.

Many now find neutrality increasingly difficult.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

For thousands of years, the Divide remained largely philosophical.

The creation of the [[The Original Covenant|Original Covenant]] provided a temporary compromise.

Reality gained stability.

Civilization gained time.

The future was delayed.

Not denied.

At least, that was the intention.

---

Over time, however, the purpose of the Covenant became forgotten.

The compromise became tradition.

The tradition became doctrine.

The doctrine became certainty.

The original promise faded from memory.

The Divide re-emerged.

More dangerous than before.

---

## The Modern Divide

In the current age, the Great Divide is no longer theoretical.

The question facing Heaven is no longer:

> Was the Covenant necessary?

Most agree that it was.

The question now is:

> Has it been long enough?

The answer determines the future of the world.

---

### The Concordant Position

The Covenant should remain.

The stability it created is too valuable to risk.

The future remains uncertain.

The burden must continue.

---

### The Ascendant Position

The Covenant has fulfilled its purpose.

The promise should be honored.

The Turning should continue.

The future must be allowed to arrive.

---

### The Balance Position

The Covenant was justified.

The promise was real.

The burden was meaningful.

The challenge is determining whether the appointed time has finally come.

---

## Why It Matters

The Great Divide influences nearly every aspect of existence.

Theologies.

Kingdoms.

Dragon Regents.

Laws.

Traditions.

Wars.

Even those unaware of the Divide often inherit its beliefs.

The conflict between preservation and transformation echoes throughout all creation.

---

## The Future

The Great Divide remains unresolved.

Its answer may determine:

- The fate of [[Umbros]]
    
- The future of [[Xyros, The Final Turning]]
    
- The role of the [[The Celestial Court]]
    
- The authority of the Dragon Regents
    
- The destiny of mortal civilization
    

For the first time since the Age Before Ages, Heaven may soon be forced to answer the question it has delayed for millennia:

> Has it been long enough?
