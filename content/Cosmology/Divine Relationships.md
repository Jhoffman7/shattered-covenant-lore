# Divine Relationships

The First Twelve are not united.

Though mortals often imagine the Primordials as perfect and eternal beings, the reality is far more complicated.

The First Twelve possess friendships, rivalries, loyalties, disagreements, and ancient wounds that have endured since before the birth of civilization.

Entire religions, kingdoms, and wars have been shaped by their relationships.

Many theologians believe the political and philosophical tensions among the First Twelve are reflected throughout the mortal world.

---

## [[Sol'Vara|Sol'Vara, the First Light]] and [[Umbros|Umbros, the Hollow King]]

**Relationship:** Eternal Companions

The oldest known relationship among the First Twelve.

Ancient myths claim that before reality existed, there was only Sol'Vara and Umbros.

The First Light and the Hollow King.

Creation and Void.

Possibility and Consequence.

Though they embody opposing principles, countless cultures describe them as inseparable.

Some traditions teach that creation itself was born from their love.

Others claim reality is sustained by the tension between them.

Their relationship is often used as a symbol for balance.

Neither seeks to dominate the other.

Both understand that existence requires them equally.

Many theologians believe that if either were ever truly lost, reality itself would suffer catastrophic consequences.

---

## [[Tharos|Tharos, the Golden Law]] and [[Vaelor|Vaelor, the Open Hand]]

**Relationship:** Ancient Rivals

The oldest ideological conflict among the First Twelve.

Tharos believes civilization flourishes through structure, law, and authority.

Vaelor believes life has meaning only when individuals are free to choose their own paths.

Their disagreement has shaped countless mortal governments.

Kings often invoke Tharos.

Revolutionaries often invoke Vaelor.

Neither has ever fully prevailed over the other.

---

## [[Velora|Velora, Weaver of Paths]] and [[Vaelor|Vaelor, the Open Hand]]

**Relationship:** Philosophical Opponents

Velora governs possibility.

Vaelor governs choice.

Their followers have debated the nature of destiny for millennia.

Does every life follow one of countless possible paths?

Or does every individual create their own future entirely through choice?

Even the gods disagree.

This question remains unresolved.

---

## [[Ilyria|Ilyria, Heart of Life]] and [[Nethrys|Nethrys, the Quiet End]]

**Relationship:** Eternal Siblings

Life and Death.

Many mortals assume they are enemies.

The gods know otherwise.

Ilyria and Nethrys are among the closest of the First Twelve.

Both understand that neither can exist without the other.

Life without death would eventually consume itself.

Death without life would have no purpose.

Their followers often cooperate more frequently than outsiders expect.

---

## [[Elyndra|Elyndra, Keeper of Truths]] and [[Umbros|Umbros, the Hollow King]]

**Relationship:** Philosophical Opponents

Truth and Mystery.

Elyndra seeks understanding.

Umbros accepts that some things may never be understood.

Elyndra believes knowledge illuminates reality.

Umbros believes mystery gives reality meaning.

Their debates are legendary within the Celestial Court.

Neither has ever convinced the other.

---

## [[Kael'Thur|Kael'Thur, Lord of Strife]] and [[Tharos|Tharos, the Golden Law]]

**Relationship:** Frequent Adversaries

Kael'Thur believes growth comes through struggle.

Tharos believes prosperity comes through stability.

Their philosophies frequently clash.

Many of history's greatest wars have been interpreted as manifestations of their ongoing dispute.

Yet neither truly despises the other.

Both believe they are strengthening civilization.

They simply disagree on how.

---

## [[Morwyn|Morwyn, Lady of Dreams]] and [[Aion|Aion, Keeper of Ages]]

**Relationship:** Close Allies

Dreams and Time share a unique connection.

Morwyn sees countless possibilities.

Aion witnesses the paths that become reality.

Among the First Twelve, they are often viewed as patient observers.

Many prophecies involve the influence of both Primordials.

Some scholars believe Morwyn dreams the future while Aion records it.

---

## [[Thaloris|Thaloris, the Ever-Tide]] and [[Kael'Thur|Kael'Thur, Lord of Strife]]

**Relationship:** Respected Allies

Change and Conflict often work together.

Kael'Thur creates pressure.

Thaloris guides transformation.

Both believe stagnation is dangerous.

Their followers frequently overlap among explorers, conquerors, innovators, and reformers.

---

## [[Elyndra|Elyndra, Keeper of Truths]] and [[Aion|Aion, Keeper of Ages]]

**Relationship:** Trusted Allies

Truth and Time rarely disagree.

Aion remembers.

Elyndra reveals.

Many historians and archivists worship both Primordials together.

Ancient records often depict them as standing side by side during moments of great historical importance.

---

## [[Sol'Vara|Sol'Vara, the First Light]] and [[Velora|Velora, Weaver of Paths]]

**Relationship:** Quiet Allies

Creation and Possibility share a natural kinship.

Sol'Vara imagines what could be.

Velora preserves the paths that might lead there.

Though they rarely act in concert, their philosophies often align.

Both believe the future should remain open.

Both increasingly question whether the Covenant still serves its original purpose.

---

## [[Sol'Vara|Sol'Vara, the First Light]] and [[Ilyria|Ilyria, Heart of Life]]

**Relationship:** Kindred Spirits

Among the First Twelve, few care more deeply for creation than Sol'Vara and Ilyria.

Both invest themselves emotionally in the lives of others.

Both believe growth should be nurtured rather than controlled.

In times of uncertainty, they frequently find themselves in agreement.

---

## [[Elyndra|Elyndra, Keeper of Truths]] and [[Velora|Velora, Weaver of Paths]]

**Relationship:** Unlikely Partners

Truth and Possibility approach reality from different directions.

Elyndra seeks answers.

Velora preserves questions.

Yet in recent ages, both have found themselves pursuing the same mystery.

One follows evidence.

The other preserves opportunities.

Neither fully understands the truth they seek.

Both suspect it has been hidden.

---

## Unanswered Questions

The following mysteries remain subjects of debate among theologians and scholars:

- Did creation truly begin with Sol'Vara and Umbros?
    
- Which of the First Twelve is oldest?
    
- Have any Primordials ever gone to war with one another?
    
- Can a Primordial truly die?
    
- What would happen if one of the First Twelve disappeared?
    
- Are the Primordials bound by fate, or do they possess true freedom?
    
- What secret does Umbros refuse to reveal?
    
- What truth does Elyndra fear discovering?
