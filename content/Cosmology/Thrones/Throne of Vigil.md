# Throne of Vigil

**Function:** Defense, Protection, Security, Crisis Response, and Eternal Watchfulness

**Associated Alignment:** [[The Ascendant]]

**Common Titles:**

- The Eternal Watch
    
- Guardians of the Heavens
    
- Keepers of the Vigil
    
- The First Shield
    
- Wardens of the Boundaries
    

---

## Overview

The Throne of Vigil serves as the primary defensive institution of the [[The Celestial Court]].

Its purpose is not conquest.

Its purpose is protection.

The Throne safeguards the Heavens against internal and external threats, coordinates defensive operations, secures sacred locations, and responds to crises that threaten celestial stability.

Among the Twelve Thrones, Vigil bears a unique responsibility.

Others preserve.

Others govern.

Others judge.

Vigil stands watch.

Its members often describe themselves not as warriors, but as guardians entrusted with burdens others should never have to carry.

---

## Purpose

The Heavens exist within a vast and often unpredictable reality.

Ancient threats endure.

Planar disturbances occur.

Sacred sites require protection.

Institutions require security.

Someone must remain vigilant.

The Throne of Vigil exists to fulfill that role.

Its responsibilities include:

- Celestial defense
    
- Strategic threat assessment
    
- Protection of sacred sites
    
- Emergency response
    
- Security coordination
    
- Defensive planning
    
- Support of judicial enforcement
    
- Protection of major celestial institutions
    
- Maintenance of defensive infrastructure
    

The Throne seeks neither glory nor recognition.

Success often means nothing happens at all.

---

## Authority

Unlike many Thrones, Vigil possesses significant operational authority.

It commands defensive forces throughout the Heavens and maintains broad authority during emergencies.

When immediate action is required, Vigil is often granted temporary powers that would normally require extensive approval.

These powers are carefully regulated and rarely exercised lightly.

The Throne understands that authority granted during crisis can easily become dangerous if left unchecked.

---

## Relationship With Kael'Thur

The Throne maintains deep philosophical ties to [[Kael'Thur]].

Outsiders often associate Kael'Thur with warfare.

Members of Vigil rarely do.

Instead, they emphasize his teachings regarding sacrifice, endurance, duty, and protection.

To the Throne, strength exists for a purpose.

The strong are expected to bear burdens others cannot.

This belief shapes every aspect of Vigil's culture.

Many members regard sacrifice as the highest expression of service.

---

## The Eternal Watch

At the center of the Throne's operations stands a vast defensive network known as the Eternal Watch.

The Eternal Watch consists of:

- Watchposts
    
- Fortresses
    
- Defensive bastions
    
- Observation stations
    
- Strategic gateways
    
- Sacred defensive sites
    

These locations are distributed throughout the Heavens and maintained continuously.

Many have existed since the earliest ages of the Celestial Court.

Though most remain peaceful for long stretches of time, every site remains active.

The purpose of the Eternal Watch is simple:

Someone must always be watching.

---

## Relationship With The Dragon Regents

The Throne of Vigil maintains one of the strongest relationships within the Court with the [[Dragon Regents]].

Both institutions share a common purpose.

Protection.

However, their responsibilities remain distinct.

Vigil protects the Heavens.

The Dragon Regents protect the Mortal Realm.

Neither serves beneath the other.

Instead, they function as partners separated by jurisdiction.

Mutual respect between the two institutions is common.

---

## Relationship With The Divine Boundary

The [[The Divine Boundary]] represents one of Vigil's greatest frustrations.

The Throne frequently identifies threats emerging within the Mortal Realm.

Yet direct intervention remains costly and dangerous.

As a result, Vigil often relies upon:

- Dragon Regents
    
- Saints
    
- Mortal champions
    
- Authorized agents
    

to address problems beyond its direct reach.

Many members of the Throne view this limitation as necessary.

Others find it deeply frustrating.

---

## Internal Philosophies

Though united by purpose, Vigil contains several influential traditions.

### The Shields

The Shields believe protection is the highest duty.

Force should only be used when absolutely necessary.

Their focus remains on preservation rather than destruction.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Let others live without fear.

---

### The Spears

The Spears believe threats should be confronted before they become catastrophes.

Preparation and decisive action save lives.

Waiting too long often carries greater costs.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Tomorrow's danger begins today.

---

### The Wardens

The Wardens focus upon safeguarding boundaries, sacred locations, and critical infrastructure.

Patience, discipline, and vigilance define their philosophy.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Every gate deserves a guardian.

---

## Relationship With Judgment

The Throne frequently supports the decisions of [[Throne of Judgment]].

While Judgment determines responsibility and issues rulings, Vigil often assists with enforcement and protection.

Despite this relationship, Vigil remains cautious regarding judicial overreach.

Its members generally believe protection should never become oppression.

---

## Relationship With Revelation

The Throne of Vigil works closely with [[Throne of Revelation]].

Revelation identifies threats.

Vigil prepares responses.

This partnership has prevented numerous crises throughout Heaven's history.

The relationship occasionally becomes strained when investigators uncover dangers that cannot easily be addressed.

Nevertheless, both institutions depend heavily upon one another.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

Among the Twelve Thrones, Vigil may be uniquely troubled by developments surrounding the Covenant Crisis.

Historical contradictions continue emerging.

Prophetic reliability continues declining.

Investigations continue uncovering anomalies.

Many institutions view these developments as intellectual concerns.

Vigil views them as potential threats.

The Throne does not oppose truth.

It fears the consequences that truth may unleash.

If long-standing foundations begin to fracture, someone must protect those caught beneath them.

Many members quietly wonder whether Heaven is approaching such a moment.

---

## The Burden of Protection

The Throne teaches that true protection often requires sacrifice.

Those who stand watch rarely receive recognition.

Those who bear burdens often do so alone.

Yet the institution views this reality as honorable rather than tragic.

A common saying within Vigil states:

> The first burden is ours.

Few philosophies better summarize the Throne's identity.

---

## Reputation

Throughout Heaven, Vigil possesses a reputation for discipline, reliability, and quiet professionalism.

Most citizens rarely interact directly with its members.

Many prefer it that way.

The absence of Vigil's presence often means peace.

Its appearance usually means something has gone wrong.

A common saying throughout the Court states:

> If Vigil has arrived, pay attention.

---

## Philosophy

> Strength exists so others may not need it.
