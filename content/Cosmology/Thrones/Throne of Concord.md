# Throne of Concord

**Function:** Diplomacy, Reconciliation, Mediation, Unity, and Cooperative Harmony

**Associated Alignment:** [[The Concordant]]

**Associated Primordial:** [[Elyndra]]

**Common Titles:**

- The Bridging Throne
    
- Keepers of Harmony
    
- The Great Mediators
    
- Wardens of Accord
    
- The Hands of Reconciliation
    

---

## Overview

The Throne of Concord serves as the Celestial Court's steward of unity, diplomacy, and reconciliation.

Its purpose is not to eliminate disagreement.

Its purpose is to ensure disagreement does not become division.

Among the Twelve Thrones, Concord concerns itself with relationships.

Between individuals.

Between communities.

Between civilizations.

Between Thrones.

Its members believe that lasting peace is not achieved through the absence of conflict.

It is achieved through understanding.

The Throne therefore dedicates itself to helping creation remain united despite its differences.

---

## Purpose

Conflict is an inevitable part of existence.

Different perspectives create disagreement.

Different experiences create tension.

Different goals create friction.

The Throne of Concord exists to ensure that such differences do not become irreparable fractures.

Its responsibilities include:

- Diplomacy
    
- Mediation
    
- Reconciliation
    
- Treaty maintenance
    
- Inter-Throne cooperation
    
- Cultural exchange
    
- Conflict resolution
    
- Restoration of trust
    
- Facilitation of dialogue
    

The Throne does not seek uniformity.

It seeks harmony.

---

## Relationship With Elyndra

The Throne maintains deep philosophical ties to [[Elyndra]].

Among the First Twelve, Elyndra embodies compassion, empathy, understanding, and healing.

Members of Concord frequently teach that reconciliation begins with understanding.

One cannot heal what one refuses to understand.

For this reason, the Throne places extraordinary value upon listening.

Many of its greatest successes began not with solutions, but with conversations.

---

## Authority

The Throne possesses very little formal authority.

It commands no armies.

Issues no rulings.

Passes no laws.

Its influence stems almost entirely from trust.

When conflicts arise, parties often invite Concord to assist because they believe the Throne genuinely seeks mutual understanding.

This reputation has become one of its greatest strengths.

And one of its greatest vulnerabilities.

---

## The Grand Forum

At the heart of the Throne stands the Grand Forum.

The Forum serves as a neutral gathering place where disputes, negotiations, and diplomatic discussions may occur.

Representatives from every Throne are welcome within its halls.

The Forum is intentionally designed to encourage dialogue rather than confrontation.

Many of Heaven's most significant agreements were forged within its chambers.

---

## Relationship With Governance

The Throne of Concord works closely with [[Throne of Governance]].

Governance creates systems that allow cooperation.

Concord nurtures the relationships that make cooperation possible.

The two institutions frequently collaborate when addressing disputes involving multiple Thrones.

Though their methods differ, their goals often align.

---

## Relationship With Judgment

Concord and [[Throne of Judgment]] maintain a respectful but occasionally complicated relationship.

Judgment determines responsibility.

Concord seeks restoration.

Many conflicts require both.

Justice without reconciliation can leave wounds unresolved.

Reconciliation without accountability often proves temporary.

Together, the two institutions strive to achieve lasting resolution.

---

## Relationship With Faith

The Throne maintains close ties with [[Throne of Faith]].

Faith concerns itself with the relationships between mortals and the divine.

Concord concerns itself with relationships between people.

The overlap between these responsibilities frequently leads to collaboration.

Many members consider Faith one of Concord's closest allies.

---

## Internal Philosophies

Though united by purpose, Concord contains several influential traditions.

### The Bridges

The Bridges focus on connection.

They seek opportunities to bring individuals and communities together.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Every divide can be crossed.

---

### The Voices

The Voices emphasize communication and understanding.

They believe most conflicts persist because people stop listening.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Understanding begins with listening.

---

### The Hands

The Hands specialize in reconciliation.

Their work focuses on repairing relationships damaged by conflict, tragedy, or betrayal.

Their unofficial motto is:

> What is broken can be mended.

---

## The Philosophy of Harmony

The Throne teaches that harmony is not agreement.

Harmony is cooperation despite disagreement.

Members frequently caution against confusing unity with uniformity.

A healthy society contains many perspectives.

The goal is not to eliminate differences.

The goal is to ensure those differences strengthen rather than weaken the whole.

This philosophy forms the foundation of nearly every Concord initiative.

---

## Relationship With Saints

Many Saints of Concord become diplomats, mediators, advisors, counselors, and peacemakers.

Their work often places them at the center of disputes where no clear solution exists.

The Throne teaches that building trust is among the most difficult forms of service.

Many Saints spend entire lifetimes pursuing it.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

Among all Twelve Thrones, Concord may be uniquely concerned about the social consequences of the Covenant Crisis.

Historical contradictions.

Shifting beliefs.

Forgotten truths.

Returning mysteries.

Each possesses the potential to divide societies and institutions alike.

While other Thrones focus upon discovering the truth, Concord focuses upon what follows.

How do communities respond?

How do relationships survive?

How do people move forward together when their understanding of reality changes?

These questions increasingly occupy the Throne's attention.

---

## The Burden of Reconciliation

The Throne teaches that reconciliation is often more difficult than conflict.

Conflict demands conviction.

Reconciliation demands humility.

Many members consider this one of the hardest lessons within creation.

Yet they also believe it is among the most important.

A common saying within Concord states:

> The strongest bridge is built by both sides.

---

## Reputation

Throughout Heaven and the Mortal Realm, the Throne of Concord possesses a reputation for patience, compassion, and diplomacy.

Some view its members as idealists.

Others view them as realists who understand that cooperation remains necessary even when agreement is impossible.

The Throne accepts both descriptions.

---

## Philosophy

> Harmony is not the absence of difference. It is the willingness to move forward together.
