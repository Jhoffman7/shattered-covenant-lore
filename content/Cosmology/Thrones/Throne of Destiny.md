# Throne of Destiny

**Function:** Prophecy, Future Observation, Probability Analysis, and Strategic Foresight

**Associated Alignment:** Philosophically aligned with [[The Balance]]

**Common Titles:**

- Keepers of Tomorrow
    
- The Thousand Paths
    
- The Watchers Beyond the Veil
    
- The Unfinished Horizon
    
- The Eyes of Possibility
    

---

## Overview

The Throne of Destiny serves as the Celestial Court's foremost authority on prophecy, future observation, and predictive analysis.

Contrary to popular belief, the Throne does not predict a single future.

It studies possibility itself.

Its seers, scholars, prophets, and analysts devote their existence to understanding how present actions shape future outcomes.

The Throne's responsibility is not to determine what will happen.

Its responsibility is to understand what may happen.

This distinction forms the foundation of its philosophy.

Throughout Heaven, few institutions are more respected.

Few are more feared.

---

## Purpose

The future influences every decision made within Heaven.

Kingdoms rise.

Empires fall.

Wars begin.

Civilizations change.

The Celestial Court requires guidance regarding the likely consequences of its actions.

The Throne of Destiny exists to provide that guidance.

By studying patterns, prophecy, and possibility, the Throne attempts to identify emerging threats, opportunities, and turning points before they occur.

Its findings influence nearly every major institution within the Heavens.

---

## The Nature of Destiny

The Throne rejects the notion of a singular predetermined future.

Instead, it teaches that existence contains countless possible futures.

Each choice creates new possibilities.

Each possibility influences countless others.

Some futures remain highly probable.

Others become increasingly unlikely.

Yet all remain possible.

Because of this, the Throne does not attempt to predict certainty.

It seeks to understand likelihood.

Its members often compare reality to an ever-changing web of interconnected paths.

---

## Authority

Unlike many Thrones, Destiny possesses little direct authority.

It commands no armies.

Passes no laws.

Conducts no investigations.

Yet its influence reaches every corner of Heaven.

The reason is simple:

Everyone wants to know what comes next.

Governance relies upon its forecasts.

Civilization relies upon its warnings.

Vigil relies upon its threat assessments.

Faith relies upon its prophetic interpretations.

Even the other Thrones often consult Destiny before making major decisions.

As a result, the Throne possesses enormous political influence despite holding relatively little formal power.

---

## Relationship With Velora

Among the First Twelve, the Throne maintains its closest philosophical connection to [[Velora]].

Both are concerned with possibility.

Both study paths not yet taken.

Yet their perspectives differ significantly.

Velora values possibility itself.

The Throne values understanding possibility.

Velora embraces uncertainty.

The Throne attempts to measure it.

This subtle difference has shaped the institution for ages.

---

## Relationship With Morwyn

The Throne also maintains strong ties to [[Morwyn]].

Many prophetic visions originate within dreams.

As a result, Destiny and Morwyn's followers frequently collaborate.

Some of the Throne's greatest discoveries began as seemingly meaningless dreams.

Others began as nightmares.

The Throne treats both with equal seriousness.

---

## The Observatory of Futures

At the heart of the Throne stands the Observatory of Futures.

A vast celestial complex dedicated to studying possibility.

Within its halls, seers examine prophetic visions, probability models, historical patterns, and potential outcomes.

Many visitors describe the Observatory as overwhelming.

Some claim it contains countless representations of futures that may never exist.

Others believe the building itself changes as reality evolves.

The Throne neither confirms nor denies such claims.

---

## The Three Schools

Though united by purpose, Destiny contains several competing philosophies.

### The Weavers

The oldest and most influential school.

The Weavers believe every possible future possesses value.

Their goal is to understand the entire web of possibility rather than focusing upon any single outcome.

Most senior seers belong to this tradition.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Every path matters.

---

### The Calculators

The Calculators believe probability is the most important tool available to the Throne.

Though countless futures exist, some are significantly more likely than others.

Their efforts focus on identifying these dominant outcomes.

Many strategic forecasts originate from this school.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Likelihood guides wisdom.

---

### The Watchers

The Watchers believe prophecy should inform decisions but never direct them.

Their role is observation.

Not manipulation.

They oppose efforts to engineer specific futures and advocate restraint whenever possible.

Their unofficial motto is:

> To see is not to choose.

---

## The Problem of Prediction

For most of recorded history, the Throne's forecasts were remarkably reliable.

While never perfect, they consistently reflected reality with extraordinary accuracy.

In recent ages, this reliability has begun to deteriorate.

At first, the deviations were minor.

Small discrepancies.

Unexpected outcomes.

Unusual variances.

Today, the problem has become increasingly difficult to ignore.

Predicted events fail to occur.

Unlikely outcomes emerge unexpectedly.

Certain futures appear unstable.

No explanation has yet been discovered.

This development has quietly alarmed many of the Throne's most experienced seers.

---

## The Missing Future

Among Destiny's oldest unresolved mysteries is a recurring anomaly appearing throughout prophetic records.

Across thousands of visions, dreams, forecasts, and observations, investigators have repeatedly documented strange patterns.

A missing shape.

A broken wheel.

A path that should not exist.

A door opening where none should be present.

A shadow standing among twelve figures.

No explanation has ever been found.

The anomaly appears throughout history.

Yet never clearly enough to identify.

Many dismiss it as coincidence.

Others disagree.

The Throne has never reached an official conclusion.

---

## The Divine Boundary

Like all celestial institutions, the Throne remains subject to [[The Divine Boundary]].

Direct intervention within the Mortal Realm is rare and carefully controlled.

As a result, many of Destiny's forecasts must be acted upon through intermediaries.

These include:

- [[Dragon Regents]]
    
- Saints
    
- Authorized agents
    
- Religious institutions
    
- Mortal champions
    

This limitation frequently frustrates members of the Throne.

Seeing a threat and stopping a threat are very different responsibilities.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

The Throne of Destiny was among the first institutions to recognize that something unusual was happening.

Not because it understood the cause.

Because its predictions began failing.

Many seers now believe reality itself is approaching a major turning point.

A moment of transformation unlike anything seen in recorded history.

No one agrees on the cause.

No one agrees on the outcome.

Only one conclusion appears consistent:

Something is coming.

---

## Reputation

Throughout Heaven, Destiny possesses a unique reputation.

Most institutions fear being wrong.

Destiny fears being right.

For when its gravest warnings prove accurate, the consequences are often catastrophic.

Many of Heaven's greatest tragedies were foreseen.

Very few were prevented.

---

## Philosophy

> The future is not written. It is becoming.
