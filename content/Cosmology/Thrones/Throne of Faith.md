# Throne of Faith

**Function:** Faith, Spiritual Stewardship, Divine Relationships, Saint Oversight, and Religious Continuity

**Associated Alignment:** [[The Concordant]]

**Common Titles:**

- The Sacred Flame
    
- Keepers of Belief
    
- The Shepherd's Throne
    
- Custodians of Hope
    
- Wardens of the Covenant
    

---

## Overview

The Throne of Faith serves as the Celestial Court's steward of belief, devotion, and the relationship between mortals and the divine.

Contrary to common assumption, the Throne does not exist to spread religion.

Its purpose is to nurture, protect, and guide faith wherever it appears.

Throughout creation, belief shapes individuals, communities, cultures, and even gods themselves.

The Throne exists to ensure that this power remains a force for growth rather than manipulation.

Among all Twelve Thrones, Faith concerns itself most directly with people.

Not institutions.

Not laws.

Not records.

People.

---

## Purpose

Faith exists because belief possesses power.

Not merely symbolic power.

Real power.

Throughout creation, countless lesser gods draw strength from the faith of their followers.

The quality and quantity of belief influence the divine landscape itself.

The Throne therefore serves as the steward of relationships between:

- Mortals
    
- Saints
    
- Religious institutions
    
- Lesser gods
    
- The Heavens
    

Its purpose is not to control these relationships.

Its purpose is to preserve their health.

---

## The Nature of Faith

The Throne teaches that faith possesses two dimensions.

### Quantity

The number of individuals who believe.

---

### Depth

The strength of that belief.

The devotion, trust, conviction, and commitment possessed by those believers.

---

Both are important.

A faith with many followers possesses influence.

A faith with devoted followers possesses resilience.

The Throne teaches that neither should be ignored.

---

## Relationship With The First Twelve

Unlike many lesser gods, the [[The First Twelve]] do not depend upon faith for existence.

They existed before worship.

Before civilization.

Before religion.

They remain foundational aspects of creation itself.

However, Faith still studies and honors the First Twelve because they embody many of the ideals believers seek to follow.

Among them, the Throne maintains particularly strong ties to [[Sol'Vara]].

---

## Relationship With Sol'Vara

Though Sol'Vara requires no worship, she remains one of the most respected Primordials within the Throne.

Members frequently point to her as the embodiment of hope, meaning, and purpose.

The Throne teaches that faith is not certainty.

Faith is trust despite uncertainty.

This belief forms the cornerstone of many of its teachings.

---

## Saints

The Throne of Faith serves as the primary steward of all [[Saints]].

Its responsibilities include:

- Recognition of candidates
    
- Guidance of aspiring Saints
    
- Oversight of training
    
- Spiritual development
    
- Assignment coordination
    
- Preservation of Saint traditions
    

Faith does not own the Saints.

Rather, it helps them discover where they are meant to serve.

Many Saints consider the Throne their spiritual home regardless of their assigned Throne.

---

## The Path of Ascension

The Throne oversees the [[The Path of Ascension]], the institution responsible for identifying and preparing future Saints.

Through the Path, Faith seeks to discover individuals whose lives possess the potential to impact creation for the better.

Members of the Throne often describe the Path as one of Heaven's greatest responsibilities.

For through it, the future of the Heavens is continuously renewed.

---

## Relationship With Lesser Gods

The Throne maintains extensive relationships with the countless lesser gods that inhabit creation.

Because belief sustains many such beings, Faith often acts as mediator, advisor, and observer regarding matters affecting divine worship.

The Throne does not determine which gods deserve followers.

Nor does it regulate belief.

Instead, it seeks to ensure that faith remains genuine rather than coerced.

This principle remains one of its most sacred obligations.

---

## The Hall of Calling

At the center of the Throne's educational efforts stands the [[The Hall of Calling]].

Here, future Saints are educated and evaluated before beginning their service.

The Hall serves as one of the most active institutions maintained by Faith.

Many members consider it the physical embodiment of the Throne's mission.

---

## Internal Philosophies

Though united by purpose, the Throne contains several influential traditions.

### The Keepers

The Keepers focus upon preserving traditions, sacred teachings, and religious continuity.

They believe stability strengthens faith.

Their unofficial motto is:

> What is worth believing deserves remembering.

---

### The Shepherds

The Shepherds emphasize compassion, guidance, and service.

They believe faith exists primarily to help people navigate life's challenges.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Every soul deserves guidance.

---

### The Seekers

The Seekers embrace inquiry and reflection.

They argue that sincere questions often strengthen belief rather than weaken it.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Doubt is not the enemy of faith.

---

## Relationship With The Covenant Crisis

The Throne of Faith may ultimately become one of the institutions most affected by the Covenant Crisis.

Historical revelations do not merely alter knowledge.

They alter belief.

Belief influences cultures.

Belief influences civilizations.

Belief influences gods.

As contradictions continue emerging throughout creation, many within the Throne have begun asking difficult questions.

What happens when ancient certainties change?

What happens when forgotten truths return?

What happens when faith itself must adapt?

No institution understands the significance of these questions better than Faith.

---

## The Burden of Belief

The Throne teaches that faith is among the most powerful forces in existence.

For better or worse, what people choose to believe shapes the world around them.

Because of this, the Throne treats belief with extraordinary care.

Faith should inspire.

Faith should guide.

Faith should strengthen.

It should never be exploited.

---

## Reputation

Throughout creation, the Throne of Faith possesses a reputation for compassion, patience, and wisdom.

Many see it as one of the most approachable institutions within the Celestial Court.

Others view it as quietly influential.

For while other Thrones govern systems, Faith governs something far more difficult.

The hearts of people.

---

## Philosophy

> Faith is not the absence of doubt. It is the courage to continue despite it.
