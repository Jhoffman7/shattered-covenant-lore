# Throne of Revelation

**Function:** Investigation, Discovery, Inquiry, and Hidden Truths

**Associated Alignment:** Philosophically aligned with [[The Balance]]

**Common Titles:**

- Seekers of the Hidden
    
- The Lantern Throne
    
- The Unveiled Eye
    
- Wardens of Inquiry
    
- Keepers of Revelation
    

---

## Overview

The Throne of Revelation serves as the primary investigative institution of the [[The Celestial Court]].

Its purpose is simple:

To uncover that which is hidden.

Unlike the [[Throne of Memory]], which preserves knowledge, the Throne of Revelation is tasked with discovering it.

Its members investigate mysteries, anomalies, divine crimes, historical inconsistencies, unexplained phenomena, and matters of celestial concern.

Throughout Heaven, the Throne possesses a reputation for asking questions many would rather leave unanswered.

This reputation is well deserved.

---

## Purpose

The Throne of Revelation exists to ensure that truth is not merely preserved.

It must also be found.

Many of Heaven's greatest discoveries originated within its halls.

Ancient relics.

Forgotten histories.

Lost prophecies.

Corrupted institutions.

The Throne's investigators are often assigned to matters other organizations cannot explain.

When uncertainty emerges, Revelation is frequently the first institution called upon to investigate it.

---

## Core Responsibilities

The Throne of Revelation oversees:

- Celestial investigations
    
- Divine crimes
    
- Historical anomalies
    
- Corrupted artifacts
    
- Unauthorized magical activity
    
- Planar disturbances
    
- Lost relics
    
- Missing celestial personnel
    
- Unexplained prophecies
    
- Matters referred by other Thrones
    

Many investigations require cooperation with other institutions.

Particularly the [[Throne of Memory]], [[Throne of Destiny]], and [[Throne of Judgment]].

---

## Authority

The Throne possesses broad investigative authority throughout the Heavens.

It may request records.

Conduct interviews.

Examine evidence.

Inspect sacred sites.

Review official findings.

However, its authority is not unlimited.

The Throne remains subject to the [[The Doctrine of Jurisdiction]].

Many investigations require permissions from other offices before access can be granted.

This restriction has become a frequent source of tension.

Particularly when investigating matters that span multiple jurisdictions.

---

## Official and Unofficial Investigations

Most investigations conducted by Revelation are formally authorized by the Court.

These include criminal inquiries, artifact recovery operations, and institutional reviews.

However, many investigators become consumed by questions that were never officially assigned.

These unofficial investigations often begin as personal curiosities.

Unexplained contradictions.

Missing records.

Historical inconsistencies.

Mysteries that refuse to disappear.

Among Revelation's members, such pursuits are often referred to as:

> "Lingering Questions."

Many of the Throne's greatest discoveries began this way.

---

## Relationship With the Throne of Memory

The Throne of Revelation maintains one of the closest relationships within the Court with the [[Throne of Memory]].

The two institutions depend heavily upon one another.

Memory preserves evidence.

Revelation investigates it.

Memory identifies contradictions.

Revelation seeks explanations.

Historically this relationship has been highly productive.

Recently, however, the growing number of unexplained inconsistencies has strained both institutions.

As questions surrounding ancient records continue to grow, cooperation between the Thrones has become increasingly important.

---

## Relationship With Elyndra

Among the First Twelve, few exert greater influence upon Revelation than [[Elyndra]].

Her pursuit of truth and willingness to confront uncomfortable realities are widely admired.

Many investigators regard Elyndra as an ideal embodiment of their purpose.

This admiration has occasionally placed the Throne at odds with institutions that prioritize stability over inquiry.

Revelation generally views difficult questions not as threats.

But as responsibilities.

---

## The Problem of the Mortal Realm

The Throne possesses no permanent investigative presence within the Mortal Realm.

This limitation exists because of [[The Divine Boundary]].

Though celestial beings may descend into the Mortal Realm, doing so carries significant risks.

As a result, Revelation typically conducts mortal investigations through intermediaries.

These include:

- [[Dragon Regents]]
    
- Saints
    
- Authorized agents
    
- Religious institutions
    
- Mortal operatives
    

Consequently, many investigations proceed far more slowly than investigators would prefer.

A common saying within the Throne states:

> "We can see the thread. We cannot touch it."

---

## Internal Factions

Though united by purpose, Revelation contains several philosophical schools.

### The Seekers

The largest faction.

Seekers believe truth should be pursued regardless of where it leads.

Many of the Throne's most celebrated investigators belong to this tradition.

Their unofficial motto is:

> "Questions exist to be answered."

---

### The Guardians

The Guardians believe certain truths are inherently dangerous.

While truth remains important, they argue revelation must be balanced with responsibility.

Some mysteries should be handled carefully.

Others should remain sealed.

Many senior investigators belong to this faction.

---

### The Lanterns

The Lanterns advocate for broad transparency.

They believe knowledge should be shared whenever possible.

Many are critical of excessive secrecy throughout the Court.

Their views frequently place them at odds with supporters of strict jurisdictional control.

---

## The Witness Connection

Officially, the Throne maintains no relationship with the secretive archivists known as the Witnesses.

Officially.

Rumors persist that certain investigators receive information originating from within the deepest archives of the [[Throne of Memory]].

No evidence has ever proven such claims.

Nevertheless, some of Revelation's most troubling investigations appear to begin with information that should not exist.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

In recent ages, the Throne has become increasingly concerned by a growing pattern of unexplained anomalies.

Missing records.

Contradictory histories.

References to unknown events.

Prophecies lacking causes.

Evidence that appears incomplete.

Individually, these mysteries seem unrelated.

Collectively, they suggest a deeper problem.

Many investigators have quietly begun tracing these contradictions toward a common point of origin.

A mystery centered around the [[The First Record]].

No official conclusion has yet been reached.

Yet concern continues to grow.

---

## Reputation

Throughout Heaven, Revelation possesses a simple reputation:

> If Revelation arrives, someone is about to become uncomfortable.

This reputation has made the Throne both respected and feared.

Investigators rarely arrive where everything is normal.

They arrive where questions exist.

And they seldom leave until answers are found.

---

## Philosophy

> Every mystery deserves an answer.
