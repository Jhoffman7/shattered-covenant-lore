# Throne of Passage

**Function:** Transition, Guidance, Thresholds, Soul Stewardship, and the Great Cycle

**Associated Alignment:** [[The Ascendant]]

**Associated Primordial:** [[Nethrys]]

**Common Titles:**

- The Lantern Throne
    
- Keepers of the Crossing
    
- Guides of the Great Cycle
    
- Wardens of Thresholds
    
- The Gentle Passage
    

---

## Overview

The Throne of Passage serves as the Celestial Court's steward of transitions.

Its purpose is not to govern death.

Its purpose is to guide change.

Among the Twelve Thrones, Passage concerns itself with moments when one state of existence gives way to another.

Birth.

Coming of age.

Marriage.

Pilgrimage.

Coronation.

Retirement.

Death.

The end of an era.

The beginning of another.

The Throne teaches that all existence is movement.

Nothing remains unchanged forever.

The role of Passage is to ensure that such transitions occur with dignity, understanding, and purpose.

---

## Purpose

Every life contains thresholds.

Every civilization experiences change.

Every soul eventually reaches a crossroads.

The Throne exists to help guide these moments.

Its responsibilities include:

- Soul guidance
    
- Funerary traditions
    
- Major life transitions
    
- Pilgrimages
    
- Rites of passage
    
- End-of-era ceremonies
    
- Stewardship of the Great Cycle
    
- Guidance during periods of change
    
- Preservation of transitional traditions
    

The Throne does not determine destinations.

It helps others navigate the journey.

---

## Relationship With Nethrys

The Throne maintains deep philosophical ties to [[Nethrys]].

Many mortals mistakenly associate Nethrys with death.

Members of Passage reject this characterization.

To them, Nethrys is not the end.

She is the guide who stands beside travelers when one chapter closes and another begins.

The Throne teaches that endings possess value because they make beginnings possible.

Without transition, growth becomes impossible.

Without release, renewal cannot occur.

---

## The Great Cycle

The Throne serves as the primary steward of [[The Great Cycle]].

According to its teachings, souls do not simply cease to exist upon death.

Instead, they enter a vast cosmic process that continues beyond mortal understanding.

Most souls eventually experience one of several known paths:

### Return

The soul eventually returns to the Mortal Realm.

---

### Rest

The soul enters an afterlife aligned with its nature and experiences a period of reflection and peace.

---

### Beyond

The soul continues somewhere beyond the known reach of Heaven.

Even the Celestial Court does not fully understand what lies Beyond.

The Throne considers this one of creation's greatest mysteries.

---

## Authority

The Throne possesses very little direct authority.

It commands no armies.

Passes no laws.

Issues no judgments.

Its influence derives from trust, wisdom, and experience.

Many seek the guidance of Passage during moments of uncertainty because its members understand transitions better than any other institution.

---

## Relationship With Faith

The Throne of Passage maintains a close relationship with [[Throne of Faith]].

Faith helps people understand what they believe.

Passage helps people navigate what happens when those beliefs are tested by change.

The two institutions frequently work together during major life events and spiritual transitions.

---

## Relationship With Stewardship

Passage and [[Throne of Stewardship]] share a uniquely complementary philosophy.

Stewardship teaches the importance of preservation.

Passage teaches the importance of release.

Together they embody a fundamental truth:

Some things must be protected.

Some things must be allowed to change.

Wisdom lies in knowing the difference.

---

## Relationship With Destiny

The Throne often collaborates with [[Throne of Destiny]].

Destiny studies possible futures.

Passage studies the journeys that lead toward them.

Many members view the two institutions as natural partners.

One examines what may come.

The other helps others arrive there.

---

## Internal Philosophies

Though united by purpose, Passage contains several influential traditions.

### The Guides

The Guides help individuals navigate major transitions.

Whether assisting a soul entering the Great Cycle or a civilization entering a new era, their role is to provide direction and support.

Their unofficial motto is:

> No traveler walks alone.

---

### The Witnesses

The Witnesses observe and preserve the meaning of important transitions.

They record endings and beginnings alike.

Their unofficial motto is:

> Every passage deserves remembrance.

---

### The Lanterns

The Lanterns provide comfort during uncertain transitions.

They are often found where fear, grief, uncertainty, or change are greatest.

Their unofficial motto is:

> We carry the light between chapters.

---

## Relationship With Saints

Saints of Passage often serve as counselors, pilgrims, funeral guides, historians of transition, and companions during life's most significant moments.

Their work rarely attracts attention.

Yet few Saints are remembered more fondly by those whose lives they touched.

The Throne teaches that helping someone cross a difficult threshold may be among the greatest acts of service.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

Among the Twelve Thrones, Passage may be one of the first to recognize that something fundamental is changing.

Its members spend their existence studying transitions.

They understand the rhythms of change better than most.

Recent centuries have revealed unusual disruptions within patterns long considered stable.

Ancient certainties appear increasingly uncertain.

Established pathways no longer behave exactly as expected.

Many within the Throne believe creation itself may be entering a period of transition unlike any previously recorded.

The implications remain unknown.

---

## The Philosophy of Thresholds

The Throne teaches that every ending contains the possibility of a beginning.

Likewise, every beginning eventually becomes an ending.

Neither should be feared.

Both should be respected.

Members often remind others that change is not the enemy of stability.

Change is part of existence itself.

The challenge is learning how to walk through it.

---

## Reputation

Throughout Heaven and the Mortal Realm, the Throne of Passage is respected for its wisdom, compassion, and serenity.

Its members are often sought during life's most difficult moments.

Many describe them as calm presences amid uncertainty.

A common saying among the Throne states:

> We do not choose the road. We carry the lantern.

---

## Philosophy

> Every ending is a doorway. Every doorway leads somewhere.
