# The Doctrine of Jurisdiction

**Classification:** Foundational Celestial Law

**Established By:** The Early Celestial Court

**Status:** Active

---

## Overview

The Doctrine of Jurisdiction is one of the oldest administrative laws within [[The Celestial Court]].

The doctrine governs how authority, responsibility, and information are distributed among the [[The Twelve Thrones|Twelve Thrones]].

Its central principle is simple:

> Authority is entrusted. Knowledge is assigned.

No Throne is intended to oversee every aspect of creation.

Each office is entrusted with specific responsibilities and granted access to information necessary to fulfill those responsibilities.

The doctrine exists to prevent excessive concentrations of authority within any single institution.

For much of Heaven's history, it has been considered essential to maintaining stability.

---

## Origins

The doctrine emerged during the earliest ages of the Celestial Court.

As Heaven expanded, it became increasingly difficult for any individual office to manage every aspect of governance.

Responsibilities were divided among the Thrones.

With those responsibilities came access to specialized knowledge.

The architects of the doctrine believed that no single institution should possess complete authority over Heaven.

Likewise, no institution should possess unrestricted access to every truth.

This separation was intended to create balance.

Each Throne would depend upon the others.

Each Throne would remain accountable to the Court as a whole.

---

## Jurisdictional Authority

Under the doctrine, each Throne possesses authority over specific domains of responsibility.

Examples include:

- [[Throne of Memory]] — Historical records and archives
    
- [[Throne of Revelation]] — Investigation and discovery
    
- [[Throne of Destiny]] — Prophecy and future observation
    
- [[Throne of Judgment]] — Legal interpretation
    
- [[Throne of Vigil]] — Defense and security
    

Information obtained through one Throne's jurisdiction is not automatically available to another.

Access generally requires formal authorization, cooperation agreements, or direct Court approval.

---

## Jurisdictional Breach

Unauthorized sharing of protected information is classified as a Jurisdictional Breach.

Such violations are taken seriously throughout Heaven.

The severity of punishment depends upon the nature of the information involved.

Minor breaches may result in:

- Formal reprimands
    
- Loss of privileges
    
- Restricted archive access
    
- Temporary suspension of duties
    

Severe breaches may result in:

- Removal from office
    
- Permanent loss of authority
    
- Exile from a Throne's service
    
- Designation as an Untrusted Witness
    

---

## The Untrusted Witness

Among the most serious consequences recognized by the doctrine is designation as an Untrusted Witness.

An individual receiving this designation is considered unreliable in official proceedings.

Their testimony, findings, and records become subject to heightened scrutiny.

For investigators, archivists, judges, and scholars, this punishment can effectively end a career.

Though rarely invoked, its existence serves as a powerful deterrent.

---

## Modern Criticism

While the doctrine was originally intended to preserve balance, critics argue that it has become increasingly rigid.

Many claim the system now prioritizes procedure over truth.

Supporters maintain that the doctrine prevents dangerous concentrations of power.

Opponents argue that it often obstructs cooperation between institutions that should be working together.

This debate has become particularly significant as investigations into historical inconsistencies continue to expand.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

The Doctrine of Jurisdiction has become increasingly controversial during the modern era.

Many of the mysteries surrounding the Covenant require information held by multiple Thrones.

Historical records.

Prophetic observations.

Investigative findings.

Ancient treaties.

Each falls under separate jurisdictions.

As a result, individuals attempting to uncover the truth frequently encounter institutional barriers.

Some argue these barriers protect Heaven.

Others fear they prevent Heaven from understanding itself.

---

## Secret Violations

Though officially respected throughout the Court, the doctrine is quietly violated more often than most realize.

Certain members of the [[Throne of Memory]] and [[Throne of Revelation]] have allegedly exchanged information without authorization.

Rumors persist of unofficial networks operating between sympathetic officials.

Most reports remain unproven.

Officially, no such networks exist.

Officially.
