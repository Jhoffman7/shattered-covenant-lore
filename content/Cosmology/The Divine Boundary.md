# The Divine Boundary

**Classification:** Foundational Cosmological Principle

**Established By:** The First Twelve

**Status:** Immutable

---

## Overview

The Divine Boundary is one of the oldest truths governing creation.

It defines the separation between the Heavens and the Mortal Realm.

Though often mistaken for a law, decree, or celestial policy, the Divine Boundary is not a rule created by the gods.

It is a fundamental reality of existence itself.

The Primordials did not establish the Boundary.

They discovered it.

Over countless ages, the First Twelve came to understand a simple truth:

> Gods are not meant to walk among men.

Not because they are forbidden.

Because they are dangerous.

---

## The Nature of Divinity

Divine beings do not merely govern concepts.

They embody them.

A celestial associated with judgment carries judgment within its very nature.

A celestial associated with dreams carries dreams.

A celestial associated with death carries death.

Where divine beings walk, their domains follow.

This influence is often subtle at first.

Yet over time, reality begins to bend around their presence.

Laws become more rigid.

Dreams become more vivid.

Life flourishes unnaturally.

Death lingers longer than it should.

The stronger the divine being, the greater the distortion.

For this reason, prolonged divine presence within the Mortal Realm is considered extraordinarily dangerous.

---

## The Erosion of Divinity

The Boundary does not merely protect mortals.

It protects the divine.

When a celestial remains within the Mortal Realm for extended periods, reality begins resisting their presence.

This phenomenon is known as Divine Erosion.

The process is gradual.

At first, the effects are barely noticeable.

Over time, however, the celestial begins losing pieces of their divine nature.

Their connection to their domain weakens.

Their power diminishes.

Their immortality fades.

Eventually, if the process continues long enough, the divine being may lose their godhead entirely.

Becoming mortal.

---

## Intent Matters

The Boundary does not respond equally to every descent.

The purpose behind a celestial's actions appears to influence the severity of Divine Erosion.

Acts performed in service of creation often produce lesser effects.

Acts performed for personal gain produce far greater consequences.

Many theologians believe reality itself recognizes the difference between sacrifice and self-interest.

Though no one fully understands why, centuries of observation support this conclusion.

---

## The Fall of the Self-Crowned

Ancient records tell of celestial beings who descended into the Mortal Realm seeking worship, authority, kingdoms, and influence.

Many attempted to rule.

Some sought to establish dynasties.

Others desired devotion.

Without exception, each eventually suffered catastrophic Divine Erosion.

These cautionary tales are frequently cited throughout Heaven as proof that the Boundary cannot be ignored indefinitely.

---

## Why the Dragon Regents Exist

The Dragon Regents were established as intermediaries between Heaven and the Mortal Realm.

They allow divine interests to be represented without requiring direct celestial intervention.

When matters within the Mortal Realm require action, the Heavens generally act through:

- Dragon Regents
    
- Saints
    
- Mortal champions
    
- Authorized agents
    
- Religious institutions
    

This arrangement preserves the Boundary while still allowing limited interaction between realms.

For most of history, it has proven remarkably effective.

---

## Rare Exceptions

Direct intervention by members of the Heavens is not impossible.

It is merely costly.

In moments of extraordinary crisis, celestial beings occasionally choose to descend despite the risks.

Such events are exceedingly rare.

The appearance of a true celestial within the Mortal Realm is often viewed as a sign that something has gone terribly wrong.

Many kingdoms possess no recorded instance of such an event occurring within their entire history.

---

## The Boundary and the Covenant

Some scholars quietly speculate that the Divine Boundary may be connected to the original structure of creation itself.

Others believe it emerged alongside the Covenant.

No evidence has ever conclusively supported either theory.

The First Twelve have never publicly addressed the matter.

As a result, the true origins of the Boundary remain unknown.

---

## Modern Interpretation

Most members of the Celestial Court accept the Divine Boundary as both practical necessity and cosmic truth.

Some view it as protection.

Others view it as sacrifice.

A few view it as a prison.

Regardless of interpretation, few dispute its existence.

The consequences of ignoring it have been demonstrated too many times throughout history.

---

## Philosophy

> The divine may guide creation, but it must not live in its place.
