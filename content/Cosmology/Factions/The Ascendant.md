# The Ascendant

The Ascendant is one of the three great philosophical alignments among the First Twelve.

Its members believe that growth, transformation, and possibility are essential to the health of reality.

The Ascendant teaches that nothing was created to remain unchanged forever.

Civilizations must evolve.

Ideas must be challenged.

The future must remain open.

Among the First Twelve, the Ascendant has historically championed exploration, innovation, struggle, and self-determination.

---

## Core Belief

The Ascendant believes that growth requires movement.

A world protected from all risk eventually ceases to grow.

A civilization protected from all hardship eventually weakens.

A future that cannot change is not truly a future.

The Ascendant does not oppose stability.

It opposes permanence.

---

## View Of The Covenant

Many members of the Ascendant opposed the Covenant from its creation.

Those who accepted it did so only because it was intended to be temporary.

The continued existence of the Covenant is viewed by many Ascendant members as a denial of reality's natural progression.

The future cannot be postponed forever.

The Turning cannot be delayed indefinitely.

---

## Modern Position

In the current age, many members of the Ascendant believe the world has remained within the Covenant far longer than originally intended.

Some argue that the greatest threat facing civilization is no longer instability.

It is stagnation.

The Ascendant generally supports allowing the Turning to continue and believes the world must eventually confront the future represented by [[Xyros, The Final Turning]].
