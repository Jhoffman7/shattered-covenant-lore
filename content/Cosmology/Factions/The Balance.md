# The Balance

The Balance is one of the three great philosophical alignments among the First Twelve.

Its members seek harmony between stability and transformation.

The Balance teaches that neither permanence nor constant change can sustain reality alone.

Order without growth becomes stagnation.

Change without structure becomes chaos.

Both are necessary.

Neither is sufficient.

---

## Core Belief

The Balance believes that wisdom lies not in choosing one extreme over another, but in understanding when each is necessary.

Every season has a purpose.

Every age has a place.

Every ending gives rise to a beginning.

The challenge is knowing when one must give way to the other.

---

## View Of The Covenant

The Balance supported the Covenant because it served a purpose.

Reality required stability.

Civilization required time.

The Covenant provided both.

However, many within the Balance believe the Covenant was never intended to endure forever.

The question is not whether the Covenant was justified.

The question is whether its purpose has now been fulfilled.

---

## Modern Position

No philosophical alignment is more divided than the Balance.

Some members believe honoring the original promise is necessary.

Others fear the consequences of ending the Covenant.

Many struggle between compassion for [[Umbros]] and concern for the world that has grown beneath the Covenant.

The Balance finds itself caught between the certainty of the Concordant and the conviction of the Ascendant.

For many within the Balance, the central question is simple:

> Has it been long enough?
