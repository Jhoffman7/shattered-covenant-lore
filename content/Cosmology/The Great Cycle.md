# The Great Cycle

**Classification:** Foundational Cosmic Process

**Primary Steward:** [[Throne of Passage]]

**Associated Primordial:** [[Nethrys]]

---

## Overview

The Great Cycle is the cosmic process through which souls continue their journey beyond mortal life.

Among the oldest known truths of creation, the Cycle governs the movement of souls through existence, ensuring that death is not an ending but a transition.

Though countless philosophies exist regarding the nature of the Cycle, one principle remains universally accepted:

> Nothing truly ends.
> 
> It only changes.

The Great Cycle serves as one of the fundamental mechanisms through which creation maintains balance across ages beyond counting.

---

## The Nature of the Cycle

When a mortal life concludes, the soul enters the Great Cycle.

Contrary to popular belief, the Cycle does not possess a single destination.

Instead, it represents a process of transition through which souls continue their journey in ways that remain only partially understood.

The Cycle is not governed by reward or punishment.

It is governed by purpose.

---

## Known Destinations

While much remains mysterious, three broad outcomes are recognized by the [[Throne of Passage]].

### Return

Some souls eventually return to the Mortal Realm.

The circumstances surrounding this return remain poorly understood.

Many scholars believe remnants of previous experiences occasionally influence future lives through intuition, talents, affinities, or unexplained familiarity.

---

### Rest

Some souls enter realms of peace, reflection, and fulfillment.

These afterlives vary greatly in nature.

Many souls remain here for long periods before continuing their journey.

Others never appear to leave.

Whether this represents completion or simply another stage remains unknown.

---

### Beyond

The rarest and most mysterious path.

Some souls continue beyond all known destinations.

Past Heaven.

Past the afterlives.

Past recorded understanding.

Even the Celestial Court possesses little knowledge regarding what lies Beyond.

The subject remains one of creation's greatest mysteries.

---

## Relationship With Nethrys

The Great Cycle is closely associated with [[Nethrys]].

However, Nethrys is not believed to control the Cycle.

Instead, she serves as its steward and guide.

Members of the Throne of Passage often describe her role using a simple analogy:

> She does not choose the road.
> 
> She keeps the lantern.

---

## The Cycle and Free Will

The Great Cycle remains deeply intertwined with free will.

No known institution possesses authority to determine a soul's destination.

Many scholars believe the choices made during life continue influencing the soul's journey long after death.

Others argue the Cycle itself possesses a wisdom beyond mortal understanding.

No consensus exists.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

Recent observations suggest subtle irregularities within patterns once believed stable.

Most remain minor.

Yet members of the [[Throne of Passage]] have begun documenting unusual occurrences involving soul transitions and long-standing cyclical behavior.

No official conclusions have been reached.

Nevertheless, concern continues to grow.

Some quietly wonder whether the Covenant Crisis may be affecting more than history.

It may be affecting the Cycle itself.

---

## Reputation

Throughout creation, the Great Cycle is viewed with equal measures of comfort and mystery.

For many, it offers hope that existence continues beyond death.

For others, it serves as a reminder that not all questions possess answers.

---

## Philosophy

> The journey does not end.
> 
> It continues beyond the horizon.
