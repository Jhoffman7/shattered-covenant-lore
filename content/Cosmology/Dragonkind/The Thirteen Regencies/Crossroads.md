# Crossroads

## Overview

Crossroads is the Regency of choice, possibility, consequence, and the countless paths that emerge from a single decision.

Where Forgefire teaches that creation is shaped through effort, Crossroads teaches that creation is shaped through choice.

Every future begins as a decision.

Every destiny begins as a path not yet taken.

A traveler chooses a road.

A ruler chooses a course.

A Dragon chooses what kind of being they will become.

Among Dragonkind, Crossroads is regarded as the embodiment of possibility itself—the understanding that creation is not fixed, but continually shaped by the choices made within it.

No path is inevitable.

Every path begins at a crossroads.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Crossroads embodies:

- Choice
- Possibility
- Consequence
- Fate and Free Will
- Divergence
- Opportunity
- Decision

Its influence can be found wherever multiple futures remain possible.

A ruler deciding between peace and war.

A hatchling choosing their first path.

A Dragon accepting or rejecting a mantle.

A traveler standing before an unknown road.

All are reflections of Crossroads.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Crossroads emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Crossroads as thoughtful, perceptive, and unusually patient.

While many of the First Regents focused upon what existed, Crossroads often contemplated what might exist.

Legends claim the First Regent possessed an uncanny ability to recognize moments that would shape entire ages.

Not because they could see the future.

Because they understood how profoundly a single choice could alter it.

Many stories portray Crossroads as a guide rather than a leader, helping others recognize the significance of decisions they might otherwise overlook.

To Crossroads, the future was never a destination.

It was a collection of possibilities waiting to be chosen.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Crossroads seeks Dragons who possess wisdom, discernment, and an appreciation for consequence.

Those chosen are often advisors, diplomats, judges, and visionaries.

Many Regents of Crossroads become known for their ability to navigate difficult decisions where no option is perfect.

The mantle values judgment above certainty.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "The weight of a choice is measured by the futures it leaves behind."

---

## Philosophy

Crossroads teaches that freedom and responsibility cannot be separated.

Every choice creates consequences.

Every path closes others.

Every decision shapes the future in ways both seen and unseen.

Followers of Crossroads believe that wisdom is not the ability to predict every outcome.

Wisdom is understanding that choices matter.

To Crossroads, indecision is itself a choice.

Refusing to choose does not halt the future.

It merely allows another path to unfold.

The purpose of choice is not perfection.

The purpose of choice is growth.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Crossroads have often served as counselors and mediators within Dragon society.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Crossroads frequently encourages careful deliberation before significant actions are taken.

Its Regents are known for examining multiple perspectives and considering the long-term consequences of decisions.

Many Dragons seek the counsel of Crossroads during moments of uncertainty, believing that its Regents possess an exceptional understanding of how present actions shape future outcomes.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Crossroads views creation as a web of interconnected possibilities.

Every life influences countless others.

Every action creates ripples.

Every choice alters the shape of what comes next.

For this reason, Crossroads teaches humility.

No individual can fully predict the consequences of their decisions.

Yet every individual remains responsible for making them.

Crossroads shares strong philosophical ties with Journey, Horizons, Dawn, and Growth.

Together, they represent the forces through which creation continually moves toward new futures.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Crossroads include:

- Forked Roads
- Intersecting Paths
- Open Gates
- Branching Rivers
- Four-Way Crossings
- Compass Roses

Among Dragons, a river dividing into many channels is often considered the purest symbol of Crossroads.

Not because it chooses a single destination.

Because it creates many.
