# Growth

## Overview

Growth is the Regency of evolution, adaptation, transformation, and becoming.

Where Dawn teaches that every ending creates the possibility of a new beginning, Growth teaches what must happen afterward.

Potential alone is not enough.

A seed may contain the promise of a forest.

A hatchling may contain the promise of greatness.

A civilization may contain the promise of a golden age.

Yet promises mean little unless they are allowed to grow.

Among Dragonkind, Growth is regarded as one of the purest expressions of the Crucible of Creation.

Nothing remains unchanged forever.

Everything is becoming something.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Growth embodies:

- Evolution
- Adaptation
- Transformation
- Maturity
- Learning
- Becoming
- Flourishing

Its influence can be found wherever life develops beyond what it once was.

A hatchling becoming an elder.

A forest reclaiming barren land.

A people learning from past mistakes.

A soul transformed by experience.

All are reflections of Growth.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Growth emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Growth as endlessly fascinated by possibility.

While other Regents focused upon what already existed, Growth was captivated by what creation might become.

Many legends claim that Growth spent ages observing the world's earliest forms of life, marveling as they adapted, evolved, and flourished.

Some stories even suggest that Growth viewed change itself as one of creation's greatest gifts.

Where others saw instability, Growth saw opportunity.

Where others saw imperfection, Growth saw potential.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Growth seeks Dragons who embrace change rather than fear it.

Those chosen often possess open minds, adaptable spirits, and a willingness to challenge long-held assumptions.

Many Regents of Growth become teachers, innovators, explorers, and visionaries who encourage Dragonkind to evolve alongside the world around them.

The mantle values wisdom gained through experience over certainty inherited through tradition.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "What grows survives.
>
> What refuses to grow is left behind."

---

## Philosophy

Growth teaches that change is not the enemy of stability.

It is one of the conditions required for life itself.

Followers of Growth believe that stagnation is among the greatest threats creation can face.

A tree that ceases to grow begins to die.

A people that cease to learn begin to decline.

A civilization that refuses to adapt becomes vulnerable to the future.

Growth teaches that becoming is a lifelong process.

No creature ever truly finishes growing.

It merely grows in different ways.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Growth have often served as advocates for innovation, adaptation, and progress.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Growth frequently challenges Dragonkind to reconsider traditions that no longer serve their purpose.

While Foundation preserves what is worth keeping, Growth asks whether those traditions still allow Dragonkind to flourish.

This occasionally places Growth at odds with more conservative voices.

Yet many Dragons acknowledge that without Growth, even the strongest foundation can become a prison.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Growth views creation as a living process of continual transformation.

Nothing remains exactly as it was.

Not mountains.

Not forests.

Not civilizations.

Not even Dragons.

For this reason, Growth shares strong philosophical ties with Dawn, Journey, Horizons, and Wilds.

Together, these Regencies represent the forces that propel creation forward.

Growth teaches that change is neither good nor bad.

It is inevitable.

Wisdom lies in learning how to grow with it.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Growth include:

- Young Trees
- Expanding Roots
- Flowering Vines
- Emerging Wings
- Molting Scales
- Spirals

Among Dragons, a tree growing from a crack in ancient stone is often considered the purest symbol of Growth.

Not because it is powerful.

Because it became something more than what existed before.

---

## Common Saying

> "Creation is not what is.
>
> It is what becomes."
