# The Thirteen Regencies

## Overview

The Thirteen Regencies are among the oldest institutions in existence.

Where the Primordials embody the Cosmic Forces that define reality, and the Original Thirteen Broods serve as the Reality Anchors that stabilize creation, the Regencies exist as sacred mantles entrusted with their stewardship.

Each Regency represents a responsibility carried by Dragonkind since the dawn of creation.

They are not positions of political authority, nor are they simple titles.

A Regency is a living mantle.

Throughout history, countless Dragons have carried these mantles, yet the Regencies themselves have endured unchanged.

When a Regent dies, their Regency returns to the [[Cradle of Scales]], where it awaits a worthy successor.

The [[Conclave of Scales]] gathers to determine which Dragon most fully embodies the nature of the vacant Regency. If accepted by the mantle, that Dragon becomes the next Regent.

In this way, individual Regents come and go, but the Regencies themselves endure through every age.

To Dragonkind, the Regencies are not merely offices.

They are sacred mantles entrusted with the stewardship of reality's foundational systems.

---

## The Nature of a Regency

Every Regency carries within it the accumulated wisdom of those who held the mantle before.

The [[Echoes of Scales]] of former Regents become intertwined with the Regency itself, creating an unbroken lineage of memory stretching back to the First Regents.

A newly ascended Regent does not lose their individuality.

Rather, they become part of something greater.

The Echoes of former bearers may offer guidance, warnings, and perspective during moments of great significance.

These voices do not command.

They advise.

Because of this, every Regency possesses a continuity of purpose that transcends any individual Dragon.

## The Thirteen Regencies

The Regencies are sacred mantles entrusted with safeguarding the Reality Anchors of creation.

Each Regency reflects a responsibility inherited from the First Regents and carried forward through every generation of Dragonkind.

Though the Broods themselves serve as the living Reality Anchors, the Regencies exist to ensure those responsibilities are remembered, preserved, and upheld.

## The First Regents

At the dawn of creation, each Regency was first carried by one of the First Thirteen Regents.

These primordial Dragons became the original stewards of their respective mantles and laid the foundations of Dragon civilization.

The First Regents were also entrusted with knowledge concerning the true nature of the Reality Anchors and the role Dragonkind serves within creation.

This responsibility would later become one of the most carefully guarded traditions preserved through the Echoes of Scale.

Though the First Regents have long since passed into the Echoes of Scale, their influence remains present within every Regency.

Their wisdom continues to guide those who inherit the mantles they once carried.

---

## The Selection of Regents

No Regency belongs to any particular Brood.

When a Regency becomes vacant, Dragons from every Brood may present candidates before the [[Conclave of Scales]].

The Conclave may deliberate, advise, and evaluate potential successors, but the final decision rests with the mantle itself.

Only when the Regency accepts a candidate does ascension occur.

Historically, the Regencies tend toward balance, and most eras see all thirteen major Broods represented among the Regents.

However, this is tradition rather than law.

There have been rare periods in Dragon history where multiple Regents emerged from the same Brood, while others held no Regency at all.

Such occurrences are often viewed as signs that creation itself is responding to unusual circumstances.

---

## The Regencies and Dragon Society

The Regencies are not governments.

They do not rule Dragonkind.

Instead, they serve as exemplars of the principles they embody.

A Regent's authority comes not from political power, but from their role as the living bearer of a Regency.

For this reason, Regents are among the most respected figures within Dragon society.

Their words carry weight not because they command obedience, but because they speak with the wisdom of countless generations.

---

## The Regencies and the Conclave

The current bearers of the Thirteen Regencies hold permanent seats within the [[Conclave of Scales]].

Together with the eldest Dragons of the major Broods, they help preserve the continuity of Dragonkind across the ages.

Though disagreements are common, all Regents share a responsibility greater than themselves:

To preserve the integrity of creation and safeguard the future of Dragonkind.

In this duty, the Regencies remain among the most sacred and enduring institutions in existence.
