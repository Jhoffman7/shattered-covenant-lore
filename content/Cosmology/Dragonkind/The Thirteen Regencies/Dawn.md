# Dawn

## Overview

Dawn is the Regency of renewal, hope, beginnings, and the promise of what comes next.

Where Journey teaches that all things move, Dawn teaches that every ending gives rise to another beginning.

Night yields to morning.

Winter yields to spring.

Failure yields to wisdom.

Death yields to another turning of the Great Cycle.

Among Dragonkind, Dawn is regarded as one of the most beloved Regencies, for it embodies the truth that no darkness lasts forever.

No matter how long the night, dawn always comes.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Dawn embodies:

- Renewal
- Hope
- Beginnings
- Rebirth
- Opportunity
- Aspiration
- New Cycles

Its influence can be found wherever something begins anew.

A hatchling emerging from its egg.

A forest recovering after fire.

A kingdom rebuilt after tragedy.

A soul beginning another chapter of its existence.

All are reflections of Dawn.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Dawn emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation itself.

Ancient traditions describe Dawn as possessing an unshakable optimism that bordered on impossible.

Where others saw endings, Dawn saw opportunities.

Where others saw failure, Dawn saw lessons.

Where others saw darkness, Dawn looked toward the coming light.

Many legends claim that during the earliest days of the world, Dawn served as a source of encouragement to the other First Regents whenever creation faced uncertainty or instability.

To Dawn, every challenge carried within it the possibility of renewal.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Dawn seeks Dragons who inspire others to continue moving forward.

Those chosen often possess remarkable resilience, optimism, and faith in the future.

Many Regents of Dawn become mentors, visionaries, healers, and leaders who help others find purpose after loss or hardship.

The mantle values perseverance not because it ignores suffering, but because it believes suffering is never the end of the story.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "The sun does not ask permission to rise."

---

## Philosophy

Dawn teaches that creation is never truly finished.

Every ending creates space for something new.

Every failure contains a lesson.

Every loss carries the possibility of growth.

Followers of Dawn believe that hope is not blind optimism.

Hope is the choice to continue despite uncertainty.

To Dawn, the future remains unwritten.

That is what makes it worth pursuing.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Dawn have often served as sources of inspiration during periods of uncertainty.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Dawn frequently advocates for faith in future generations and confidence in Dragonkind's ability to overcome adversity.

When other Regencies become focused on past failures or present difficulties, Dawn often reminds them of what may still be achieved.

Many Dragons consider Dawn the Regency most closely associated with the spirit of renewal that has allowed Dragonkind to endure across countless ages.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Dawn views creation as an endless cycle of endings and beginnings.

Nothing lasts forever.

Neither does anything end forever.

Creation continuously renews itself.

For this reason, Dawn shares strong philosophical ties with Growth, Horizons, Harvest, and the Great Cycle itself.

Together, these forces represent creation's capacity to continually become something new.

Dawn teaches that renewal is not a denial of the past.

It is the bridge that carries the past into the future.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Dawn include:

- Rising Suns
- Morning Light
- Spring Blossoms
- New Leaves
- Hatching Eggs
- First Light Upon Mountains

Among Dragons, the first ray of sunlight touching a mountain peak is often considered the purest symbol of Dawn.

Not because it banishes darkness.

Because it announces the arrival of a new day.
