# Endurance

## Overview

Endurance is the Regency of perseverance, resilience, survival, and unwavering resolve.

Where Growth teaches that all things must change, Endurance teaches that some things must remain.

Storms may test creation.

Time may erode it.

Loss may burden it.

Yet some things endure.

Among Dragonkind, Endurance is regarded as the embodiment of persistence against all odds. It is not concerned with victory, glory, or achievement.

It concerns itself with continuing.

When all else falters, Endurance remains.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Endurance embodies:

- Resilience
- Perseverance
- Survival
- Patience
- Resolve
- Fortitude
- Continuity

Its influence can be found wherever something refuses to yield.

A mountain weathering countless storms.

A people surviving catastrophe.

A Dragon honoring an ancient oath.

A flame that continues to burn through the longest night.

All are reflections of Endurance.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Endurance emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Endurance as quiet, steadfast, and impossible to break.

While other Regents sought to shape the newborn world, Endurance often stood watch over what had already been built.

Legends claim that during creation's earliest instability, Endurance remained unmoving while reality itself trembled around them.

Many stories portray the First Regent not as the strongest of the Thirteen, but as the one most willing to bear burdens that others could not.

To Endurance, strength was never measured by power.

It was measured by persistence.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Endurance seeks Dragons who continue despite hardship.

Those chosen are often patient, disciplined, and remarkably steadfast.

Many Regents of Endurance become guardians, oathkeepers, defenders, and protectors of sacred places.

The mantle values reliability over brilliance and commitment over talent.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "Anyone can stand when the skies are clear.
>
> Endurance is revealed when the storm remains."

---

## Philosophy

Endurance teaches that true strength is not found in avoiding hardship.

It is found in continuing despite it.

Followers of Endurance believe that adversity reveals character, but perseverance defines it.

Anyone may begin a difficult path.

Few possess the resolve to finish it.

To Endurance, resilience is not stubbornness.

It is the wisdom to continue carrying what matters when the weight becomes difficult.

The purpose of strength is not domination.

The purpose of strength is to endure.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Endurance have served as guardians of continuity within Dragon society.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Endurance frequently advocates preserving commitments, honoring ancient responsibilities, and remembering the sacrifices of previous generations.

When Dragonkind faces uncertainty or hardship, many look to Endurance for reassurance that difficult times will pass.

Its Regents are often among the last to abandon hope and the last to retreat from responsibility.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Endurance views creation as a process that unfolds across vast spans of time.

Not every challenge can be solved quickly.

Not every wound heals immediately.

Not every burden can be avoided.

For this reason, Endurance teaches patience.

Creation survives because countless things choose to continue despite hardship.

Endurance shares strong philosophical ties with Foundation, Storms, and Hearth.

Together, they represent the forces that allow creation to withstand the trials of existence.

Endurance teaches that survival is not weakness.

It is one of creation's greatest triumphs.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Endurance include:

- Mountains
- Ancient Trees
- Eternal Flames
- Standing Stones
- Unbroken Chains
- Weathered Cliffs

Among Dragons, an ancient tree that has survived countless seasons is often considered the purest symbol of Endurance.

Not because it never suffered.

Because it continued to grow despite suffering.

---

## Common Saying

> "To endure is to prevail against time itself."
