# Journey

## Overview

Journey is the Regency of movement, passage, exploration, migration, and the paths that connect all things.

Where Depths asks what lies beyond understanding, Journey compels one to seek the answer.

No creature remains truly still.

Rivers flow.

Seasons turn.

Stars travel across the heavens.

Souls pass through the Great Cycle.

Even mountains are slowly shaped by the passage of ages.

Among Dragonkind, Journey is regarded as the embodiment of motion itself—not merely physical travel, but the understanding that existence is defined by movement from one state to another.

Life is not a destination.

It is a journey.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Journey embodies:

- Travel
- Migration
- Passage
- Exploration
- Pilgrimage
- Discovery
- Transformation Through Experience

Its influence can be found wherever movement creates growth.

A Dragon leaving its Brood for the first time.

A pilgrim seeking wisdom.

A river carving its course through stone.

A soul passing from one life to the next.

All are reflections of Journey.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Journey emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Journey as restless, curious, and impossible to confine.

While many of the First Regents established themselves within the newborn world, Journey sought to see every corner of it.

Legends claim the First Regent was the first Dragon to circumnavigate the world, the first to cross unknown seas, and the first to venture beyond the lands claimed by Dragonkind.

Many stories portray Journey not as a wanderer without purpose, but as one who understood that wisdom cannot be found by standing still.

To Journey, every path held a lesson.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Journey seeks Dragons who embrace movement and growth.

Those chosen are often explorers, diplomats, adventurers, navigators, and seekers of distant horizons.

Many Regents of Journey spend significant portions of their lives beyond the Broodlands, learning from distant lands and unfamiliar peoples.

The mantle values experience above certainty.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "The path teaches what the destination never could."

---

## Philosophy

Journey teaches that growth requires movement.

A creature unwilling to leave the familiar cannot discover what lies beyond it.

Every experience changes the traveler.

Every road offers lessons.

Every departure creates the possibility of transformation.

Followers of Journey believe that fear of change often prevents individuals from becoming what they were meant to be.

To Journey, the greatest failure is not choosing the wrong path.

It is refusing to walk one at all.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Journey have often served as emissaries, explorers, and bridges between distant peoples.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Journey frequently advocates openness to new ideas and perspectives.

Many Dragons view Journey as the Regency most concerned with the future, not because it predicts what will come, but because it understands that the future is reached one step at a time.

Its Regents are often among the first to investigate newly discovered lands, unusual phenomena, or emerging threats.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Journey views creation as a series of interconnected paths.

Nothing remains unchanged.

Everything moves.

Everything transitions from one state to another.

For this reason, Journey shares strong philosophical ties with Crossroads, Horizons, Growth, and Passage itself.

Together, they represent the continual movement through which creation evolves.

Journey teaches that even the Great Cycle is, at its heart, another road.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Journey include:

- Roads
- Rivers
- Wings in Flight
- Footprints
- Open Gates
- Distant Trails

Among Dragons, a winding river is often considered the purest symbol of Journey.

Not because it travels far.

Because it never truly stops moving.
