# Storms

## Overview

Storms is the Regency of adversity, challenge, transformation through trial, and strength earned through hardship.

Where Harvest teaches that life flourishes through stewardship, Storms reminds creation that growth without challenge breeds fragility.

No mountain is shaped by calm weather alone.

No tree develops deep roots without enduring strong winds.

No Dragon discovers the limits of their strength without facing hardship.

Among Dragonkind, Storms is regarded as one of the most respected and misunderstood Regencies.

It does not seek suffering.

It recognizes suffering as one of creation's greatest teachers.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Storms embodies:

- Adversity
- Challenge
- Conflict
- Trial
- Resilience
- Adaptation
- Earned Strength

Its influence can be found wherever hardship transforms weakness into strength.

A hatchling surviving its first winter.

A kingdom rebuilding after disaster.

A Dragon overcoming failure.

All are reflections of Storms.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Storms emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient draconic traditions describe Storms as fierce, uncompromising, and impossible to intimidate.

Many legends claim that when the young world was still unstable, Storms willingly entered its most dangerous regions to test the limits of both reality and themselves.

These tales often portray Storms not as a destroyer, but as a teacher whose lessons were rarely gentle.

The First Regent believed that creation could not endure unless it learned how to withstand hardship.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Storms seeks Dragons who confront adversity rather than avoid it.

Those chosen are often courageous, determined, and unwilling to surrender when faced with overwhelming odds.

Many Regents of Storms become legendary warriors, explorers, or leaders who have survived trials that would have broken lesser beings.

Yet physical strength alone is never enough.

The mantle values perseverance far more than power.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "The storm does not ask whether you are ready.
>
> It asks what remains when it has passed."

---

## Philosophy

Storms teaches that hardship is not the opposite of growth.

It is one of its necessary conditions.

Followers of Storms believe that challenge reveals truth.

Adversity exposes weakness.

Failure creates opportunity for improvement.

Without trial, strength remains unproven.

Without struggle, resilience remains undeveloped.

To Storms, the purpose of hardship is not punishment.

The purpose is transformation.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Storms have often served as catalysts for action within the Conclave of Scales.

Where Foundation urges patience and Hearth seeks unity, Storms frequently challenges Dragonkind to confront difficult realities.

They are often among the first to advocate decisive action during times of crisis.

Though their counsel may be uncomfortable, many Dragons acknowledge that Storms often speaks truths others would rather ignore.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Storms views creation as a living process that becomes stronger through adversity.

Nothing meaningful remains unchanged forever.

Every living thing is tested.

Every structure faces pressure.

Every civilization encounters hardship.

Storms teaches that these challenges are not flaws within creation.

They are part of creation itself.

For this reason, Storms often shares philosophical ties with Growth, Endurance, and Wilds.

Together, they embody the forces that shape life through struggle and adaptation.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Storms include:

- Lightning
- Thunderclouds
- Tempest Winds
- Crashing Waves
- Storm-Touched Peaks
- Rainfall After Drought

Among Dragons, a mountain scarred by countless storms yet still standing is often considered the purest symbol of Storms.

Not because it endured unchallenged.

Because it endured at all.

---

> "Only the tested know their true strength."
