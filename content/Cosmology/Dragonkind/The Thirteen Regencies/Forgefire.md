# Forgefire

## Overview

Forgefire is the Regency of creation, craftsmanship, innovation, and transformation through deliberate effort.

Where Endurance teaches the value of preserving what matters, Forgefire teaches that creation must also be shaped.

Nothing of worth emerges fully formed.

A blade begins as ore.

A fortress begins as stone.

A civilization begins as an idea.

Among Dragonkind, Forgefire is regarded as the embodiment of purposeful creation—the force that transforms possibility into reality through vision, skill, and determination.

Creation provides the materials.

Forgefire teaches what can be built from them.

---

## Domain

The Regency of Forgefire embodies:

- Creation
- Craftsmanship
- Innovation
- Artistry
- Industry
- Invention
- Transformation Through Effort

Its influence can be found wherever raw potential becomes something greater.

A smith shaping metal.

A sculptor revealing beauty within stone.

A Dragon designing a new hatchery.

A people building a future with their own hands.

All are reflections of Forgefire.

---

## The First Regent

The first bearer of Forgefire emerged alongside the First Thirteen Regents during the dawn of creation.

Ancient traditions describe Forgefire as endlessly fascinated by possibility.

While many of the First Regents sought to understand the world as it was, Forgefire sought to imagine what it could become.

Legends claim the First Regent crafted some of the earliest wonders of the world, shaping structures and creations that survived for ages beyond counting.

Many stories portray Forgefire as a visionary who saw creation not as a finished work, but as an invitation.

To Forgefire, the world was not merely something to inhabit.

It was something to build upon.

---

## The Mantle

The Mantle of Forgefire seeks Dragons driven by imagination, skill, and purpose.

Those chosen are often artisans, inventors, builders, architects, and creators of every kind.

Many Regents of Forgefire become renowned for their ability to bring ambitious ideas into existence.

The mantle values action over intention.

Ideas possess value.

Completed works possess legacy.

Among Dragonkind it is said:

> "A dream remains smoke until given form."

---

## Philosophy

Forgefire teaches that creation is an ongoing act.

The world is not static.

It is continually shaped by those who inhabit it.

Followers of Forgefire believe that every individual possesses the ability to leave a mark upon creation.

Whether through art, craftsmanship, leadership, innovation, or service, all beings contribute to the world they leave behind.

To Forgefire, creation is not limited to grand achievements.

Every meaningful act of improvement is a form of creation.

The purpose of knowledge is application.

The purpose of vision is realization.

---

## Role Within Dragonkind

Throughout history, Regents of Forgefire have served as builders of Dragon civilization.

Within the Conclave of Scales, Forgefire frequently advocates for innovation, adaptation, and the pursuit of new possibilities.

Its Regents often encourage Dragonkind to embrace invention rather than relying solely upon ancient traditions.

While Foundation preserves what has proven its worth, Forgefire seeks to discover what might be possible tomorrow.

The two Regencies are often viewed as complementary forces within Dragon society.

---

## Relationship to Creation

Forgefire views creation as unfinished.

Not flawed.

Not incomplete.

Simply ongoing.

Every generation inherits a world shaped by those who came before and contributes to the world that follows.

For this reason, Forgefire teaches responsibility alongside creativity.

Creation grants the power to shape.

Wisdom determines how that power should be used.

Forgefire shares strong philosophical ties with Growth, Harvest, and Horizons.

Together, they represent the forces through which creation continually advances.

---

## Symbolism

Common symbols associated with Forgefire include:

- Forges
- Anvils
- Molten Metal
- Sculptor's Tools
- Crafted Wonders
- Flames Shaping Steel

Among Dragons, a blade fresh from the forge is often considered the purest symbol of Forgefire.

Not because it is powerful.

Because it became something greater than the material from which it was made.
