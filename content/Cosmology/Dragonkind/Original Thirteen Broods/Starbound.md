# Starbound

## The Brood of Endless Horizons

### Common Name

**The Wayfinders**

---

## Overview

The Starbound Brood is among the most adventurous and inquisitive lineages within [[Dragonkind]].

Masters of the ever-changing region known as the [[Wandering Skies]], the Starbound embody curiosity, discovery, and the pursuit of possibility.

Where many Dragons seek wisdom, power, stability, or understanding, the Starbound seek what lies beyond.

Beyond the horizon.

Beyond the map.

Beyond the known world.

To a Starbound, every mystery is an invitation.

Every unanswered question is a path waiting to be walked.

Every frontier exists to be explored.

Throughout history, Starbound Dragons have served as explorers, cartographers, pathfinders, scouts, and pioneers whose discoveries expanded the understanding of creation itself.

Many of the greatest discoveries in history began with a simple Starbound question:

> "I wonder what's over there?"

---

## Homeland

### The Wandering Skies

The ancestral territory of the Starbound Brood is a vast and ever-changing region known as the Wandering Skies.

Unlike the territories of most Broods, the Wandering Skies refuse to remain constant.

The region is characterized by:

- Floating islands
- Ancient skyborne ruins
- Migrating landmasses
- Shifting pathways
- Celestial currents
- Reality fractures
- Dimensional crossings

No map remains accurate forever.

Landmarks move.

Pathways change.

Entire islands may disappear for years before returning.

Some regions appear to drift between different realms entirely.

Even among the Starbound, the Wandering Skies remain only partially explored.

This is exactly how they prefer it.

---

## Sacred Landmark

### The Last Horizon

No place is more sacred to the Starbound than the Last Horizon.

Unlike the sacred sites of other Broods, the Last Horizon possesses no fixed location.

It appears unpredictably throughout creation.

Different realms.

Different worlds.

Different ages.

Every recorded sighting places it somewhere new.

No two descriptions agree completely.

Yet every Starbound knows it exists.

For many, finding the Last Horizon becomes a lifelong pursuit.

To the Starbound, it represents the ultimate truth of existence:

There is always something beyond what is known.

---

## Appearance

Starbound Dragons possess some of the most distinctive and unusual features within Dragonkind.

Built for exploration rather than warfare, their bodies are uniquely adapted for navigating unstable environments, shifting skies, and even the boundaries between realms themselves.

Common traits include:

- Long aerodynamic bodies
- Broad endurance-focused wings
- Metallic silver, bronze, copper, and ivory scales
- Windswept horn formations
- Bright inquisitive eyes
- Lean athletic builds
- Weathered appearances shaped by centuries of travel

Unlike many Broods, Starbound rarely appear pristine.

Most elder Starbound bear visible evidence of the countless journeys they have undertaken throughout creation.

To other Dragons, they often resemble living journals written across scales and bone.

### The Sextant Horns

The horns of a Starbound are among their most recognizable features.

Rather than growing as fixed structures, their horns develop as layered segments resembling ancient astrolabes, sextants, and navigational instruments.

When focusing upon a destination, celestial pathway, or dimensional crossing, these horn segments subtly shift and realign.

Many Starbound claim their horns help them sense:

- Directional currents
- Celestial alignments
- Dimensional pathways
- Reality fractures
- Ley line convergences

Whether this ability is magical, instinctual, or biological remains a topic of debate among Dragonkind.

### Prism Eyes

Starbound eyes possess unusual prism-like irises composed of countless crystalline facets.

Light passing through these structures refracts in strange and often beautiful ways.

This adaptation allows Starbound to perceive phenomena invisible to most other Dragons, including:

- Magical currents
- Planar instabilities
- Hidden pathways
- Dimensional crossings
- Spatial distortions

Many younger Dragons describe the experience of speaking with a Starbound as unsettling.

Their eyes often appear focused on something just beyond ordinary sight.

### Navigation Veins

Specialized scales along the neck, wings, and spine form luminous patterns known among the Brood as Navigation Veins.

When traveling through unfamiliar regions, these scales often emit faint lines of light that spread across the wing membranes.

Rather than resembling constellations, the patterns resemble:

- Routes
- Currents
- Pathways
- Intersections
- Cartographic markings

The patterns constantly shift and change as the Starbound interprets its surroundings.

No two Dragons display identical Navigation Veins.

### Variable Geometry Wings

The wings of a Starbound are among the most sophisticated biological structures found within Dragonkind.

Along the edges of the wings grow specialized scale-feathers capable of independent movement.

These structures continuously adjust during flight, allowing Starbound to:

- Ride microscopic air currents
- Navigate turbulent skies
- Exploit dimensional updrafts
- Perform impossible aerial maneuvers

Many observers describe Starbound flight as graceful, precise, and almost unnatural.

### Tail Rudder

Rather than possessing heavy tails designed for combat, Starbound tails terminate in elongated fan-like structures that function as living rudders.

These adaptations allow them to make sudden directional changes while navigating difficult terrain, floating islands, and unstable magical environments.

This contributes significantly to their reputation as exceptional navigators.

### Horizon Marks

Perhaps the most fascinating feature of elder Starbound Dragons is the presence of Horizon Marks.

Repeated exposure to strange realms, reality fractures, celestial phenomena, and planar anomalies often leaves permanent alterations upon their bodies.

Examples include:

- Crystalline scale formations
- Semi-translucent scales
- Iridescent oil-slick coloration
- Distorted reflections
- Areas frozen in unusual states of transformation
- Metallic surfaces displaying impossible colors

Among the Starbound, these marks are viewed as symbols of experience rather than injury.

Each mark tells a story.

Each story represents a journey.

### Living Collections

Many Starbound permanently incorporate relics from their travels into their appearance.

Ancient artifacts, foreign charms, navigation tokens, and cultural keepsakes are often woven into scales, attached to horns, or fused into armor-like portions of their bodies.

A centuries-old Starbound may carry:

- Relics from forgotten civilizations
- Fragments of distant realms
- Ancient maps
- Celestial artifacts
- Tokens gifted by foreign peoples

No two elder Starbound appear alike.

Each becomes a living collection of the places they have visited and the stories they have gathered.

### Visual Identity

A Starbound should appear less like a noble Dragon ruling a territory and more like an explorer who has spent centuries wandering the edges of creation.

They are the Dragons most likely to arrive carrying impossible stories, unfamiliar relics, and knowledge from places few others have ever seen.

To encounter an ancient Starbound is to meet a living testament to the vastness of creation itself.

---

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Starbound are renowned for discovering what others overlook.

They are often the first to uncover:

- Lost pathways
- Forgotten civilizations
- Hidden realms
- Ancient ruins
- Unexplored frontiers

A common saying among Dragonkind states:

> "If it exists, a Starbound will eventually find it."

Many regard them as restless.

Most Starbound consider this a compliment.

---

## Culture

Starbound culture values:

- Curiosity
- Discovery
- Courage
- Independence
- Adaptability
- Wonder

Young Starbound are encouraged to explore from an early age.

Experience is valued more highly than comfort.

Questions are valued more highly than certainty.

Many Starbound spend significant portions of their lives wandering beyond their homeland.

Remaining in one place for too long is often viewed as a form of stagnation.

Among the Starbound, exploration is not merely a profession.

It is a calling.

---

## Temperament

Starbound are naturally inquisitive and adventurous.

The unfamiliar excites them.

The mysterious attracts them.

The unknown inspires them.

When confronted with something strange, their first instinct is rarely fear.

It is curiosity.

Their response to adversity is often summarized as:

### Pressure → Curiosity

The more unusual a situation becomes, the more interested they become in understanding it.

This tendency has led to many incredible discoveries.

It has also led to more than a few disasters.

---

## What They Are Known For

Throughout Dragonkind, Starbound are renowned as:

### Explorers

Those who travel where others will not.

---

### Pathfinders

Discoverers of forgotten roads and hidden routes.

---

### Cartographers

Mappers of the known and unknown alike.

---

### The Seekers of Creation

The Dragons most dedicated to exploring the limitless possibilities of existence.

---

## First Scale Selection

### The Unmapped Path

When a Starbound First Scale passes, candidates undertake an ancient succession ritual known as the Unmapped Path.

Each candidate is tasked with venturing beyond the known frontiers of Dragonkind.

No destination is assigned.

No route is prescribed.

No guidance is provided.

The purpose of the trial is discovery.

Candidates are expected to return with knowledge, understanding, or discoveries previously unknown to their people.

Upon their return, they are judged by Starbound elders and the [[Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon whose journey contributes most significantly to Dragonkind becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Beyond the Realms

Unlike most Dragons, Starbound frequently travel beyond the Mortal Realm, the Heavens, and even the [[Wyrmreach]] itself.

Many dedicate their lives to exploring the countless realms that exist throughout creation.

Some become experts on forgotten worlds.

Others chart planar pathways between realities.

A few disappear entirely while pursuing mysteries beyond known existence.

Among the Starbound, a Dragon who has crossed realms is respected.

A Dragon who has discovered a previously unknown realm becomes legendary.

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Starbound have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Horizons]]
- [[Journey]]
- [[Crossroads]]
- [[Stars]]

However, Starbound possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Starbound have expanded the boundaries of known creation.

Their discoveries have revealed lost worlds.

Their courage has opened forgotten paths.

Their curiosity has reshaped how Dragonkind understands reality itself.

While other Broods preserve, protect, guide, or connect, the Starbound continue searching for what lies beyond.

For they believe no map is ever truly complete.

And no horizon is ever truly final.
