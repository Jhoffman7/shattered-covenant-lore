# Moonscale

## The Brood of Moonveil

### Common Name

**The Silver Eyes**

---

## Overview

The Moonscale Brood is among the most respected and beloved lineages within [[Dragonkind]].

Masters of the mystical territory known as the [[Astral Glades]], the Moonscales are renowned for their wisdom, compassion, and extraordinary understanding of both Dragons and the world around them.

Where many Broods seek strength through power, action, or endurance, the Moonscales seek understanding.

They are often mentors, healers, advisors, and guides whose influence extends far beyond their own territory.

Though generally peaceful, Moonscales are not weak.

Their patience is immense, but not limitless.

When driven to anger, they can become surprisingly fierce defenders of those under their protection.

Throughout history, Moonscales have served as Regents, teachers, scholars, and spiritual leaders whose wisdom helped shape the course of Dragonkind.

---

## Homeland

### The Astral Glades

The ancestral territory of the Moonscale Brood lies within a vast region of ancient forests, crystal-clear lakes, sacred groves, and celestial wonders known as the Astral Glades.

Unlike many regions of the [[Wyrmreach]], the Astral Glades possess a profound connection to the heavens.

The land itself appears responsive to celestial events.

The phases of the moon influence the region in subtle and sometimes dramatic ways.

The Astral Glades are characterized by:

- Ancient silver-barked forests
- Crystal-clear lakes
- Sacred moonlit groves
- Celestial stone circles
- Luminous flora
- Vast open glades
- Unusually brilliant night skies

Many Moonscales believe the Astral Glades preserve a unique connection to creation's celestial rhythms.

Whether this belief is literal truth or ancient tradition remains uncertain.

---

## The Lunar Cycle

The Astral Glades are deeply influenced by the phases of the moon.

### New Moon

The forests grow unusually quiet.

Many luminous plants dim.

The region becomes a place of introspection and self-reflection.

---

### Crescent Moon

Growth accelerates throughout the Glades.

New blossoms appear.

Many mentoring ceremonies and educational traditions occur during this phase.

---

### Half Moon

Considered the phase of balance.

Many gatherings and communal celebrations are held during this time.

---

### Full Moon

The Astral Glades become radiant.

Luminous plants glow brightly.

Ancient standing stones shimmer with faint light.

The lakes reflect the heavens with breathtaking clarity.

This phase is regarded as especially sacred among the Moonscales.

---

### Celestial Events

Lunar eclipses, meteor showers, celestial conjunctions, and other rare astronomical phenomena often produce unusual magical effects throughout the Glades.

Ancient pathways may reveal themselves.

Dreams become more vivid.

Forgotten memories surface.

Many of the Brood's most sacred traditions occur during these rare events.

---

## Appearance

Moonscale Dragons possess some of the most ethereal and beautiful forms within Dragonkind.

They do not resemble creatures touched by moonlight.

They resemble pieces of the night sky given physical form.

Graceful, silent, and seemingly weightless, Moonscales move through the world with an elegance that often feels supernatural.

Where many Dragons dominate their surroundings through power or presence, Moonscales alter the world through tranquility.

Their mere existence brings stillness.

Common traits include:

- Silver-white scales
- Lunar horn formations
- Constellation markings beneath the skin
- Liquid-silver eyes
- Silent flight structures
- Mist-like atmospheric trails
- Prismatic lunar shadows
- Celestial bioluminescence

Many observers describe Moonscales as appearing less like Dragons and more like dreams wandering beneath the stars.

### Lunar Crescent Horns

The horns of a Moonscale are among their most distinctive features.

Formed from materials resembling polished pearl, nacre, and moonstone, these structures naturally develop shapes reminiscent of lunar phases.

Some possess sweeping crescent formations.

Others develop inward-curving rings resembling full moons.

No two Moonscales possess identical horn structures.

Many Dragons view these formations as visible reflections of the individual's spiritual nature.

Among the Brood, the shape of one's horns is considered deeply personal and often becomes part of their identity.

### Liquid-Mirror Scales

The scales of a Moonscale possess an extraordinary texture unlike that of any other Dragon.

Neither metallic nor organic in appearance, they resemble liquid silver, polished hematite, or mother-of-pearl.

During daylight, these scales appear soft and satin-like.

At night, they become highly reflective, capturing and redistributing moonlight throughout their surroundings.

The effect often causes Moonscales to appear illuminated from within even when standing in complete darkness.

### Silken Flight Structures

The wings of a Moonscale possess remarkably delicate appearances.

Their membranes resemble flowing silk, celestial fabric, or the fins of exotic deepwater creatures.

Despite their fragile appearance, these structures are incredibly durable.

Most remarkably, Moonscale flight is completely silent.

They glide through forests, mountains, and skies without disturbing leaves, branches, or even the air itself.

Many creatures never realize a Moonscale has passed overhead.

### Living Constellations

Beneath the outer layers of a Moonscale's scales lies a network of bioluminescent celestial markings.

These formations resemble stars connected by delicate lines of pale indigo, silver, and lavender light.

Each Dragon's constellation pattern is entirely unique.

Many scholars believe these markings reflect aspects of the Dragon's spirit, destiny, or connection to the [[Great Cycle]].

Among Dragonkind, these celestial patterns are often referred to as Living Constellations.

### Lunar Pulse

The light within a Moonscale's constellation markings changes according to the phases of the moon.

During the New Moon, the lights dim until they are nearly invisible.

The Dragon appears as a ghostly silver silhouette against the darkness.

During the Full Moon, the constellation patterns blaze with brilliant celestial radiance.

The light spills outward across the surrounding environment, casting intricate patterns of stars upon the ground, trees, and water.

Many sacred ceremonies within the Astral Glades are performed beneath the Full Moon for this reason.

### Eyes of the Cosmos

Moonscale eyes contain no visible pupils.

Instead, they resemble perfectly smooth pools of liquid silver or polished star-sapphires.

Looking into the eyes of a Moonscale often feels less like making eye contact and more like gazing into a still body of water reflecting an endless night sky.

Many describe the experience as profoundly calming.

Others find it unsettling.

As though the Dragon is quietly observing truths beyond mortal understanding.

### The Mist-Weaver Shroud

Moonscales naturally generate a thin veil of cool silver mist around themselves.

This phenomenon is known as the Mist-Weaver Shroud.

The mist carries the scents of damp earth, night-blooming flowers, moonlit forests, and fresh rain.

As it drifts across the landscape, nearby plants often unfurl their leaves and blossoms.

The effect contributes significantly to the calming presence for which the Brood is renowned.

### Astronomical Shadows

Light passing through the wings of a Moonscale refracts into intricate celestial patterns.

The resulting shadows resemble miniature eclipses, lunar halos, and shifting constellations.

As a Moonscale walks through a forest, these patterns move across the ground beneath them.

Many witnesses describe the effect as though the Dragon carries its own private night sky.

### The Blood-Moon Shift

Though Moonscales are among the most patient and understanding of Dragons, their wrath is legendary.

When pushed beyond their limits, the celestial light beneath their scales undergoes a dramatic transformation.

The soft silver and blue radiance shifts into deep crimson.

Their Living Constellations burn with the color of a total lunar eclipse.

The Blood-Moon Shift serves as a warning recognized throughout Dragonkind.

If a Moonscale has reached this state, every opportunity for peace has already been ignored.

### Void Exhale

The breath of an enraged Moonscale is among the most unsettling manifestations of draconic power.

Rather than releasing fire, lightning, or frost, they exhale a silent wave of cosmic darkness.

This phenomenon appears as a moving absence of starlight.

A region where color, sound, and warmth seem to disappear.

Those caught within its path often describe the sensation as standing upon the dark side of the moon itself.

Cold.

Silent.

Empty.

The aftermath is marked by eerie stillness and frost-kissed shadows.

### Visual Identity

A Moonscale should appear less like a Dragon illuminated by moonlight and more like a living fragment of the night sky.

Beautiful.

Serene.

Ancient.

And quietly vast beyond comprehension.

They embody the belief that true understanding does not come from dominating the world.

It comes from reflecting upon it.

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Moonscales are known for their compassion and wisdom.

Many Dragons seek their counsel during times of uncertainty.

Others travel great distances to learn from them.

Their reputation is built not upon authority, but trust.

A common saying among Dragonkind states:

> "Every Dragon remembers a Moonscale who helped them."

---

## Culture

Moonscale culture values:

- Compassion
- Understanding
- Patience
- Perspective
- Guidance
- Wisdom

Mentorship occupies a central role within their society.

Knowledge is viewed as something meant to be shared rather than hoarded.

Many young Dragons spend time among the Moonscales regardless of their Brood of origin.

To the Moonscales, helping another Dragon grow is among the highest callings.

---

## Temperament

Moonscales are generally calm, thoughtful, and understanding.

They strive to see situations from multiple perspectives before forming conclusions.

Violence is rarely their first choice.

Nor their second.

Nor often their third.

However, their patience should not be mistaken for weakness.

When a Moonscale finally chooses to fight, it is usually because every peaceful alternative has been exhausted.

This can make them unexpectedly dangerous opponents.

Among Dragonkind it is often said:

> "If a Moonscale is angry, you have already ignored every warning."

---

## What They Are Known For

Throughout Dragonkind, Moonscales are renowned as:

### Mentors

Teachers and guides who help younger Dragons find their path.

---

### Healers

Caretakers of both body and spirit.

---

### The Eyes of Dragonkind

Those who perceive truths others overlook.

---

### Seers

Dragons capable of interpreting dreams, celestial omens, and hidden meanings.

---

## First Scale Selection

### The Silent Reflection

When a Moonscale First Scale passes, candidates undergo an ancient succession ritual known as the Silent Reflection.

The trial requires each candidate to spend an extended period in complete solitude within the Astral Glades.

No advisors.

No companions.

No guidance.

Only themselves.

The purpose of the Reflection is not survival.

It is self-understanding.

Candidates are expected to confront their fears, ambitions, regrets, and desires.

Upon their return, they are judged by Moonscale elders and the [[Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon who demonstrates the greatest wisdom, perspective, and understanding becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Moonscales have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Dawn]]
- [[Growth]]
- [[Crossroads]]
- [[Journey]]

However, Moonscales possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Moonscales have served as guides, teachers, and caretakers for Dragonkind.

Their wisdom has ended conflicts.

Their compassion has healed wounds.

Their guidance has shaped countless lives.

Though they rarely seek recognition, their influence can be found throughout the Wyrmreach.

Like the moon itself, they illuminate paths others struggle to see.
