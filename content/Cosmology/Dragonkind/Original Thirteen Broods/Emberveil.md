# Emberveil

## The Brood of Veiled Truth

### Common Name

**The Veilkeepers**

---

## Overview

The Emberveil Brood is among the most contemplative and scholarly lineages within [[Dragonkind]].

Masters of the ancient realm known as the [[Cinder Veil]], the Emberveils embody memory, truth, preservation, and the belief that no story should ever be truly lost.

Unlike many Dragons who concern themselves with power, duty, discovery, or wisdom, the Emberveils dedicate themselves to remembrance.

To an Emberveil, every civilization leaves behind lessons.

Every life leaves behind a story.

Every age leaves behind truths worth preserving.

Where others seek the future, Emberveils safeguard the past.

For countless generations, they have served as archivists, historians, chroniclers, and keepers of knowledge whose efforts ensure that even forgotten ages continue to echo through history.

Among Dragonkind it is often said:

> "If the world forgets, the Emberveil remembers."

---

## Homeland

### The Cinder Veil

The ancestral territory of the Emberveil Brood is a mysterious region known as the Cinder Veil.

The realm appears perpetually caught between fire and ash.

Ancient forests of blackened trees stretch across the landscape.

Ash drifts endlessly through the air.

Rivers of glowing embers wind through valleys of dark stone.

The region is characterized by:

- Ashen forests
- Ember-lit valleys
- Ancient archives
- Forgotten monuments
- Ruined civilizations preserved by time
- Memory shrines
- Endless drifting cinders

Despite its appearance, the Cinder Veil is not a land of destruction.

It is a land of preservation.

To the Emberveils, ash is not a symbol of endings.

It is evidence that something once existed.

---

## Sacred Landmark

### The Last Ember

No place is more sacred to the Emberveil Brood than the Last Ember.

Hidden within the deepest chamber of the Cinder Veil burns a single eternal ember.

Small.

Silent.

Unchanging.

According to Dragon tradition, every forgotten story leaves behind an ember.

Every lost kingdom.

Every fallen hero.

Every forgotten age.

The Last Ember is said to contain the memory of all things that would otherwise be lost.

Whether this belief is literal truth or spiritual symbolism remains uncertain.

To the Emberveils, the distinction is unimportant.

The Last Ember serves as a reminder that memory itself is sacred.

---

## Appearance

Emberveil Dragons possess some of the most haunting and contemplative forms within Dragonkind.

They do not resemble creatures that wield fire.

They resemble memories that refuse to fade.

Lean, quiet, and perpetually smoldering, Emberveils embody remembrance, preservation, and reflection.

Where many Dragons command attention through force or majesty, Emberveils seem to draw the world inward toward silence.

Their presence evokes forgotten libraries, abandoned temples, and stories preserved across the ages.

Common traits include:

- Carbonized scale structures
- Petrified charcoal horns
- Script-like glowing veins
- Ember-red eyes
- Ash-mantled wings
- Acoustic dampening fields
- Ink-like smoke trails
- Ancient archival aesthetics

Many observers describe Emberveils as appearing less like living creatures and more like stories given physical form.

### Fragmented Carbon Scales

The scales of an Emberveil resemble layers of compressed charcoal, carbonized parchment, and burnt birch bark.

Rather than appearing solid and armored, the scales possess paper-thin edges that lift slightly from the body.

When moving, these scales brush together with a faint whispering sound.

Many compare it to pages turning within a quiet library.

Despite their delicate appearance, the scales are remarkably durable and resistant to decay.

### Petrified Trunk Horns

The horns of an Emberveil resemble ancient sections of burned and petrified wood.

Deep fractures run throughout their structures.

Within those cracks pulses a faint crimson glow.

The light rises and falls with the Dragon's breathing, creating the impression of embers hidden beneath layers of ash.

The oldest Emberveils often possess horn structures resembling entire charred tree trunks frozen in time.

### Ash-Mantled Wings

The wings of an Emberveil possess membranes resembling ancient parchment blackened by centuries of smoke.

Their edges never appear entirely solid.

Instead, the boundaries dissolve into drifting veils of soft gray ash.

During flight, this ash trails behind the Dragon like fading ink upon paper.

Observers often describe the sight as a memory slowly disappearing into the wind.

### The Smoldering Ledger

Beneath the scales of every Emberveil runs a network of glowing channels known as the Smoldering Ledger.

Unlike the chaotic magma veins of Ashwings or the flowing currents of Tideborn, these pathways form precise geometric patterns.

Many resemble script.

Others resemble maps.

Some appear similar to ancient runes or celestial diagrams.

The glow intensifies whenever the Dragon is engaged in study, contemplation, chronicling, or remembrance.

Scholars believe these patterns physically record portions of the Dragon's accumulated memories.

### Eyes of Living Embers

The eyes of an Emberveil resemble deep coals buried beneath layers of ash.

No visible pupils exist.

Most of the eye appears dim and dormant.

However, when focusing upon a person, object, or memory, the ash-like surface parts slightly to reveal a brilliant crimson ring beneath.

Many individuals report the sensation that an Emberveil is not merely looking at them.

It is preserving them.

Recording them.

Ensuring they will not be forgotten.

### Ink-Smoke Trails

A thin stream of dark smoke drifts constantly from an Emberveil's nostrils, horn fractures, and scale seams.

Unlike ordinary smoke, this vapor behaves unnaturally.

It hangs low to the ground.

Pools around their feet.

Flows across surfaces like liquid ink.

Eventually it disperses into soft gray ash.

The phenomenon creates the impression that the Dragon carries a living shadow composed of memory and soot.

### The Vacuum of Noise

The presence of an Emberveil subtly alters the acoustics of the surrounding environment.

Echoes diminish.

Voices soften.

Footsteps become muted.

The world grows quieter.

This phenomenon is known among the Brood as the Vacuum of Noise.

Many archives, libraries, and memorial sites deliberately seek the presence of Emberveils because of this effect.

Conversations held near them often feel intimate and deeply personal.

### The Scent of the Past

Emberveils carry a unique atmospheric signature.

Their bodies radiate a dry, gentle warmth accompanied by scents of:

- Old paper
- Cedar wood
- Worn leather
- Campfire soot
- Ancient libraries

The environment surrounding them becomes naturally resistant to moisture and decay.

Books survive longer.

Records remain preserved.

Artifacts resist deterioration.

Among the Brood, this is viewed as a sacred extension of their purpose.

### The Colors of Memory

Emberveil coloration reflects the final stages of a dying fire.

Common primary tones include:

- Charcoal black
- Burnt umber
- Soot gray
- Ash white

Their internal energy displays only muted colors:

- Smoldering crimson
- Ochre amber
- Ember orange
- Chalk white

The resulting palette feels ancient and restrained.

Nothing burns brightly.

Everything endures.

### The Crystallized Record

As Emberveils age, their scales gradually cease shedding ash.

The carbonized layers become increasingly rigid.

The soft textures of charcoal transform into glossy obsidian and fused cinder.

The Dragon slowly transitions from living creature to permanent historical artifact.

Many view this process as the physical manifestation of memory becoming history.

### The Living Monument

The oldest Emberveils eventually choose permanent residence within the archives of the [[Cinder Veil]].

There they settle into final repose.

Over centuries, their bodies transform into immense statues of obsidian, charcoal stone, and fused ash.

Though motion ceases, the glowing script of the Smoldering Ledger continues pulsing forever.

These ancient Dragons become living records.

Entire generations visit them to study forgotten histories, lost truths, and memories preserved across the ages.

Among the Brood, this final transformation is considered the highest honor possible.

### Visual Identity

An Emberveil should appear less like a Dragon preserving knowledge and more like memory itself given physical form.

Quiet.

Ancient.

Unforgettable.

Every aspect of their existence reflects their central belief:

Every ember carries a story.

## Reputation

Among Dragonkind, Emberveils are renowned for their dedication to preserving knowledge.

When records are lost.

When histories are forgotten.

When truths disappear beneath centuries of neglect.

Emberveils recover them.

Many of the oldest surviving records in existence were preserved through Emberveil efforts.

Common sayings include:

> "Ask an Emberveil if you wish to know what was forgotten."

> "Ash remembers what fire once knew."

---

## Culture

Emberveil culture values:

- Memory
- Truth
- History
- Reflection
- Patience
- Preservation

They believe knowledge carries responsibility.

Not every truth should be revealed immediately.

Not every secret should remain hidden forever.

Wisdom lies in understanding the difference.

Many Emberveils dedicate centuries to studying a single subject.

Others wander the world gathering stories before they can be lost.

Among the Emberveils, remembering is considered an act of respect.

---

## Temperament

Emberveils are thoughtful, patient, and deeply introspective.

They rarely rush to judgment.

They rarely act without understanding.

When confronted with uncertainty, their response is often summarized as:

### Pressure → Reflection

The greater the confusion becomes, the more carefully they examine it.

Many Emberveils believe the answers people seek often already exist.

One simply needs to remember where to look.

---

## What They Are Known For

Throughout Dragonkind, Emberveils are renowned as:

### Historians

Preservers of the past.

### Chroniclers

Recorders of important events.

### Archivists

Protectors of knowledge.

### The Keepers of Hidden Truth

The Dragons entrusted with preserving what must never be lost.

---

## First Scale Selection

### The Forgotten Path

When an Emberveil First Scale passes, candidates undertake an ancient succession ritual known as the Forgotten Path.

Candidates are tasked with recovering a truth, story, artifact, or piece of history believed lost.

Finding the truth alone is not sufficient.

They must understand it.

Preserve it.

And determine whether it should be shared with the world.

Upon returning, candidates present their findings before Emberveil elders and the [[Echoes of Scales]].

The Dragon who demonstrates the greatest wisdom regarding memory and truth becomes the next [[First Scale]].

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

Throughout history, Emberveils have produced Regents from many different lineages of the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]].

They are most commonly associated with:

- [[Crossroads]]
- [[Depths]]
- [[Horizons]]
- [[Forgefire]]

However, Emberveils possess no special claim to these Regencies.

Like all Dragons, their ancestry does not determine the mantle they may inherit.

---

## Legacy

Across countless generations, the Emberveils have preserved stories that would otherwise have vanished forever.

They have protected ancient records.

Recovered forgotten truths.

Maintained histories others abandoned.

Their legacy is not measured through power.

It is measured through remembrance.

As long as the Emberveils endure, Dragonkind believes no story is ever truly lost.
