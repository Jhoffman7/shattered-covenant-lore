# The Wyrmreach

## Overview

The Wyrmreach is the ancestral homeland of Dragonkind.

Known among Dragons as the Elder Wilds, it exists as a twin realm to the Mortal Realm, born from the same primordial forces that shaped reality itself.

Ancient beyond measure, the Wyrmreach serves as the heart of draconic civilization and the place where the oldest traditions of Dragonkind continue to endure.

It is within the Wyrmreach that the Cradle of Scales resides.

It is within the Wyrmreach that the Conclave of Scales gathers.

And it is within the Wyrmreach that the great Broods maintain their ancestral territories.

To Dragons, the Wyrmreach is more than a homeland.

It is a living testament to creation itself.

---

## The Elder Wilds

According to Dragon tradition, the Wyrmreach emerged alongside the earliest days of reality.

While the Mortal Realm gradually became shaped by civilizations, kingdoms, and the ambitions of mortals, the Wyrmreach remained closer to creation's original nature.

For this reason, Dragons often refer to their homeland as the Elder Wilds.

Many believe the realm preserves echoes of reality as it existed in its infancy.

Whether this belief reflects literal truth or ancient cultural memory remains uncertain.

What is certain is that the Wyrmreach is unlike any other place in existence.

---
## The First Sanctuary

Among the oldest traditions preserved by Dragonkind is the belief that the Wyrmreach was the first region of reality to fully stabilize after creation came into being.

According to these traditions, while much of the newborn cosmos remained turbulent and unfinished, the Wyrmreach became the first true sanctuary of creation.

It was here that the Cradle of Scales formed.

It was here that the First Thirteen emerged.

And it was here that the Reality Anchors first established themselves within the world.

For this reason, many Dragons believe the Wyrmreach is more than their ancestral homeland.

It is the place where creation first learned how to endure.

Whether this belief represents literal history or sacred symbolism remains a matter of debate.

Among Dragons, however, the distinction is often considered unimportant.

The meaning remains the same.

---

## A Twin Realm

The Wyrmreach is not a kingdom.

Nor is it merely a distant land.

It is a realm unto itself.

Though intrinsically connected to the Mortal Realm, the Wyrmreach exists alongside it rather than within it.

Dragon scholars often describe the two realms as twin reflections of creation.

The Mortal Realm became the domain of civilizations, nations, and history.

The Wyrmreach remained the domain of ancient lineages, primordial landscapes, and the enduring memory of creation.

Travel between the two realms is possible, but neither was ever intended to replace the other.

Together they represent different expressions of the same reality.

---

## The Great Broods

Dragon society is organized around ancient lineages known as Broods.

Each Brood traces its history through countless generations and maintains its own customs, traditions, and methods of selecting a First Scale.

Some Broods value age.

Others value wisdom.

Some value strength.

Others seek consensus.

Though the Broods often differ in philosophy and temperament, all recognize one another as part of the greater family of Dragonkind.

The Broods are independent.

They are not isolated.

Though often described as lineages, the Original Thirteen Broods hold a significance beyond ancestry alone.

According to the oldest traditions, they serve as the living Reality Anchors through which creation maintains many of its foundational systems.

For this reason, the health of the Broods has long been regarded as a matter of concern not only for Dragonkind, but for reality itself.

---

## The Sacred Heart

Deep within the Wyrmreach lies the Cradle of Scales.

No place is more sacred.

No responsibility is more important.

Every Brood recognizes the Cradle as belonging to all Dragons rather than any individual lineage.

For this reason, conflicts that might otherwise divide the Broods are often set aside when the safety of the Cradle is concerned.

Its protection is considered a shared responsibility.

One that transcends lineage, territory, and personal ambition.

---

## The Hall of Scales

The Hall of Scales stands within The Wyrmreach as the meeting place of the Conclave of Scales.

There the Regents and First Scales gather to deliberate matters affecting all Dragonkind.

The Hall is among the oldest surviving structures in existence and is regarded as neutral ground by every Brood.

Within its chambers, ancient rivalries are expected to yield to collective responsibility.

Though disagreements remain common, violence within the Hall is considered unthinkable.

---

## Sanctuary

The Wyrmreach are considered sacred territory.

Although outsiders occasionally visit, access is heavily restricted.

Few mortals have ever set foot within the Wyrmreach.

Fewer still have been invited.

To violate the sanctity of The Wyrmreach is to invite the unified response of Dragonkind itself.

Historically, even hostile Broods have set aside their differences when confronting threats to their homeland.

The protection of The Wyrmreach is among the few causes capable of uniting all Dragons.

---

## The Wild Regions

Large portions of The Wyrmreach remain unsettled even by Dragons.

These ancient regions are often referred to simply as the Wild Regions.

Many contain creatures older than recorded history.

Others house ruins predating modern civilizations.

Some are avoided entirely.

Not because Dragons fear them.

Because they respect them.

The Wild Regions serve as a reminder that creation still contains mysteries beyond even draconic understanding.

Some regions are believed to predate Dragon civilization itself.

The oldest maps of the Wyrmreach mark certain territories not with names, but with warnings.

Even the Conclave possesses little reliable knowledge concerning these places.

Many Dragons believe remnants of creation's earliest ages still linger there.

---

## Relationship to the Crucible of Creation

The Wyrmreach are often viewed as the physical manifestation of the Crucible of Creation.

Unlike mortal kingdoms, the region was never shaped according to a singular vision.

It grew naturally.

Broods emerged.

Territories developed.

Traditions evolved.

No central authority imposed order upon the land.

Instead, The Wyrmreach became what creation allowed them to become.

For many Dragons, this makes the homeland a living example of the Crucible's teachings.

---

## Relationship to the Mortal Realm

The Mortal Realm and the Wyrmreach are often described by Dragons as twin reflections of creation.

Though born from the same primordial forces, each followed a different path.

The Mortal Realm became a place of kingdoms, civilizations, and fleeting generations.

The Wyrmreach remained a realm of ancient lineages, enduring traditions, and landscapes shaped by the oldest powers of creation.

Most Dragons spend the majority of their lives within the Wyrmreach.

While Dragons are capable of traversing the Mortal Realm, relatively few choose to dwell there permanently.

As a result, true Dragons remain rare and often mythic figures within mortal history.

Many mortals live and die without ever witnessing a Dragon firsthand.

Among Dragons, it is often said that every journey eventually leads back to the Elder Wilds.

---
## The Unmapped Wilds

Although the territories of the Broods are well known, they comprise only a portion of the Wyrmreach.

Vast regions remain unexplored even by Dragon standards.

These lands are collectively known as the Elder Wilds.

Some contain landscapes untouched since the dawn of creation.

Others house ruins, creatures, and mysteries older than Dragon civilization itself.

Many Dragons choose to spend portions of their lives exploring these regions.

Some never return.

Those who do rarely return unchanged.

Among Dragonkind, the existence of the Elder Wilds serves as a reminder that creation still contains mysteries beyond even draconic understanding.

---

## Legacy

Across countless ages, The Wyrmreach have endured.

Empires have risen and fallen.

Nations have appeared and vanished.

Borders have shifted.

Yet the homeland of Dragonkind remains.

As long as The Wyrmreach endure, Dragons believe that the oldest memories of creation will endure alongside them.

---
