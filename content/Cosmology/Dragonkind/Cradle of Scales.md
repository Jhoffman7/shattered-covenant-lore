# The Cradle of Scales

## Overview

The Cradle of Scales is the most sacred place in all of Dragonkind.

Older than kingdoms.

Older than empires.

Older than many of the institutions that govern the world.

The Cradle has existed since the earliest moments of creation itself.

It is here that Dragonkind believes the first eggs were laid after reality emerged from the chaos beyond.

It is here that new generations continue to be born.

And it is here that the mantles of the Regencies return when their bearers pass from the world.

To Dragons, the Cradle is more than a location.

It is the beating heart of their civilization.

---

## The First Nest

According to ancient tradition, the Cradle was formed during the dawn of creation.

When reality stabilized and the first Dragons emerged, a portion of creation itself responded to their existence.

Deep within the newborn world, a place of immense primal power took shape.

The Dragons named this place the Cradle of Scales.

Many legends claim that traces of the First Thirteen still linger there.

Others believe the Cradle remains connected to the forces that first gave rise to Dragonkind.

No Dragon knows the full truth.

Most believe the mystery itself is part of the Cradle's nature.

---
## The First Emergence

Among the oldest traditions preserved within Dragonkind is the belief that the First Thirteen Regents emerged from the Cradle itself.

Not as hatchlings.

Not as ordinary Dragons.

But as fully formed manifestations of creation's response to the newborn reality.

According to these traditions, when the Primordials completed the act of creation, reality answered in kind.

The Cradle formed.

The First Thirteen emerged.

And through them, the Reality Anchors became established within the world.

Whether this account is literal history or symbolic truth remains a matter of debate.

Yet among Dragons, few doubt the connection between the Cradle and the origins of the Original Thirteen Broods.

For this reason, many consider the Cradle to be the birthplace not only of Dragonkind, but of the Reality Anchors themselves.

---

## The Sacred Hatcheries

The Cradle contains countless dragon eggs.

Some rest openly within vast nesting grounds.

Others lie hidden within chambers untouched for ages.

Some are expected to hatch within years.

Others may remain dormant for centuries.

No Dragon fully understands the process.

Attempts to count the eggs have repeatedly failed.

Some swear the number changes.

Others claim new eggs occasionally appear where none existed before.

Regardless of the explanation, one truth remains constant:

The Cradle never stands empty.

For this reason, many Dragons believe that Dragonkind can never truly disappear while the Cradle endures.

Many Dragons believe the Cradle itself participates in the continuation of Dragonkind.

No Dragon has ever witnessed an egg being created.

Yet no Dragon can fully explain how the Cradle continues to replenish itself across the ages.

Among Dragonkind, it is considered one of the oldest and most sacred mysteries in existence.

---

## Creation's Blessing

Some scholars believe the Cradle serves as a point where creation itself remains closer to the world than anywhere else.

Others argue that the Cradle is the physical scar left behind when reality first stabilized.

No consensus exists.

Yet all agree that the power present within the Cradle is unlike anything found elsewhere in creation.

This power predates the Regencies.

Predates the Conclave.

Some even believe it predates the Covenant itself.

The magic of the Cradle protects the eggs housed within it and sustains them across immense spans of time.

No force has successfully destroyed a dragon egg within the Cradle.

Whether this protection is a blessing, a natural law, or something older remains unknown.

The Dragons have never sought to unravel the mystery completely.

Some truths are considered sacred.

---

## The Regency Mantles

When a Regent dies, their Regency does not immediately pass to a successor.

Instead, the mantle returns to the Cradle.

There it remains until the Conclave of Scales gathers to seek a worthy heir.

During this period, the mantle is said to rest among the oldest chambers of the Cradle.

Many Dragons believe the Echoes of previous Regents commune there, helping guide the mantle toward its next bearer.

The exact process remains known only to those directly involved in Regency succession.

Even among Dragons, it is regarded with profound reverence.

---
## The Anchors and the Cradle

Among the deepest secrets preserved by Dragonkind is the belief that the Cradle maintains a unique relationship with the Reality Anchors.

The exact nature of this connection remains unknown.

Some believe the Anchors draw strength from the Cradle.

Others believe the Cradle merely reflects their condition.

Several ancient accounts suggest that disturbances within the Cradle have historically coincided with periods of instability among the Broods.

Because of this possibility, the Conclave closely monitors unusual changes within the Cradle.

Few Dragons are permitted to study such matters directly.

Fewer still are trusted with the conclusions.

---

## Guardians of the Cradle

Protecting the Cradle is considered one of the highest responsibilities within Dragon society.

No single Brood controls it.

No single Regency claims authority over it.

Instead, guardians from many lineages maintain a perpetual watch.

These Dragons answer only to the Conclave itself.

Their purpose is simple:

Protect the Cradle.

Protect the eggs.

Protect the future.

To fail in this duty is considered one of the gravest dishonors imaginable.

---

## The War of Dominion

The Cradle played a significant role during the War of Dominion.

Although the conflict never reached the sacred grounds themselves, many Dragons feared that continued escalation could eventually threaten the future of Dragonkind.

This concern became one of the strongest arguments for ending the conflict before it spiraled beyond control.

To Dragons, the loss of a kingdom would be tragic.

The loss of a Brood would be devastating.

The loss of the Cradle would be unthinkable.

Its protection remains one of the few causes capable of uniting every Brood and Regency without exception.

---

## Relationship to the Conclave

The Conclave of Scales serves as the ultimate steward of the Cradle.

Yet even the Conclave does not claim ownership over it.

This distinction is important.

According to the principles of the Crucible of Creation, the Cradle belongs to creation itself.

The Conclave merely safeguards it.

Many Dragons view this relationship as the perfect expression of stewardship without dominion.

---

## Relationship to the Crucible of Creation

The Cradle is often regarded as the purest physical expression of the Crucible.

It embodies continuity.

Growth.

Renewal.

Possibility.

Every generation of Dragons begins there.

Every Regency eventually returns there.

Through the Cradle, Dragonkind is continually renewed.

For this reason, many Dragons describe it as the place where creation remembers itself.

---

## Legacy

Across countless ages, the Cradle has remained unchanged.

Civilizations have risen and fallen.

Wars have been fought.

Empires have vanished.

Yet the eggs endure.

The Regencies endure.

The Cradle endures.

As long as it remains, Dragonkind believes there will always be another generation waiting to emerge into the world.

---

## Common Saying

> "As long as the Cradle endures, so too shall Dragonkind."
