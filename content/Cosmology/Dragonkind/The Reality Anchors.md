# The Reality Anchors

## Overview

The Reality Anchors are among the oldest and most closely guarded truths preserved by Dragonkind.

According to the oldest traditions, when the First Primordials united to bring reality into existence, creation itself responded.

From that response emerged the First Thirteen.

These beings would become the founders of the Original Thirteen Broods and the first bearers of the Regencies.

More importantly, they became the first Reality Anchors.

Unlike the Primordials, who embody the cosmic truths that define existence, the Reality Anchors embody the physical systems through which reality functions.

The Primordials established reality.

The Anchors stabilized it.

Together they form one of the oldest balancing structures in creation.

Most Dragons know only fragments of this truth.

The full understanding of the Reality Anchors is preserved through the Echoes of Scale and safeguarded by the Conclave of Scales.

---

## The Nature of an Anchor

A Reality Anchor is not an object.

Nor is it a title.

It is a living manifestation of a foundational physical force within creation.

Each of the Original Thirteen Broods serves as an Anchor.

As long as a Brood endures, the force it embodies remains stabilized within reality.

Should an Anchor weaken, reality itself may begin to exhibit signs of instability.

Should an Anchor collapse entirely, the consequences would be catastrophic.

This possibility is known as [[The Cataclysm]].

For this reason, the preservation of the Original Thirteen Broods has always been regarded as one of Dragonkind's most sacred responsibilities.

---
According to the oldest teachings of Dragonkind, the Anchors were not created because they represented the greatest forces within reality.

They were created because they represented the forces reality could not survive without.

Many Dragon scholars describe the Anchors as creation's first act of self-preservation.

Others speak of them as the stitches that prevented the newborn world from unraveling.

Though interpretations differ, all agree on one truth:

Without the Anchors, reality would not endure.

---

## The Original Thirteen Anchors

The Original Thirteen Broods each serve as custodians of a foundational physical system within reality.

### Stoneheart

**Anchor of Foundation**

Stonehearts stabilize the geological framework of reality itself.

Mountains, tectonic stability, bedrock, and the enduring structures upon which the world is built all fall within their sphere.

---

### Tideborn

**Anchor of Flow**

The Tideborn maintain the great cycles of movement throughout creation.

Currents, circulation, exchange, and the pathways through which things travel are reflected through their Anchor.

---

### Stormclaw

**Anchor of Atmosphere**

Stormclaws embody atmospheric pressure, electrical discharge, and the dynamic forces of weather.

They anchor the restless energies that move through the skies.

---

### Winterborn

**Anchor of Preservation**

Winterborn govern dormancy, conservation, equilibrium, and the natural pauses that allow systems to recover and endure.

---

### Wildroot

**Anchor of Adaptation**

Wildroots embody growth through adaptation.

They anchor ecological resilience, evolution, and nature's ability to respond to changing conditions.

---

### Ashwing

**Anchor of Transformation**

Ashwings represent change through destruction and renewal.

Volcanism, metamorphosis, refinement, and rebirth all fall within their domain.

---

### Dawnfire

**Anchor of Radiance**

Dawnfires stabilize illumination, life-giving energy, and the forces that sustain growth and vitality throughout creation.

---

### Emberveil

**Anchor of Continuity**

Emberveils embody memory, preservation of knowledge, and the continuity that allows wisdom to survive across generations.

---

### Ironcrest

**Anchor of Structure**

Ironcrests anchor integrity, reinforcement, order, and the systems that prevent collapse under pressure.

---

### Moonscale

**Anchor of Cycles**

Moonscales stabilize recurring patterns, natural rhythms, celestial cycles, and the repetition upon which many systems depend.

---

### Skyfang

**Anchor of Gravity**

Skyfangs embody gravity, ascent, descent, and the invisible forces that govern elevation, momentum, and balance.

---

### Deepscale

**Anchor of Shadow**

Deepscales anchor darkness, concealment, boundaries, secrets, and the unseen spaces that exist alongside the known world.

---

### Starbound

**Anchor of Horizons**

Starbounds embody exploration, expansion, discovery, and the drive that pushes creation toward what lies beyond the known.

---

## The Balance of Creation

No Anchor exists in isolation.

Each relies upon the others.

Foundation without Flow becomes stagnant.

Flow without Structure becomes chaos.

Transformation without Preservation becomes destruction.

Preservation without Transformation becomes stagnation.

The Anchors form an interconnected system through which reality maintains equilibrium.

Because of this, Dragonkind has long believed that no Brood is more important than another.

Each serves a purpose that cannot be fully replaced.

---

## The First Regents

The First Regents were more than rulers.

They were the first living expressions of the Reality Anchors.

Through them, creation established the systems necessary for reality to endure beyond its earliest moments.

Though the First Regents have long since passed into the Echoes of Scale, their legacy remains present within every Brood.

The Anchors they established continue to stabilize creation to this day.

---

## The Hidden Stewardship

Knowledge of the Reality Anchors is not widely known.

Most Dragons understand only the traditions of their Brood.

Few comprehend the full significance of the Original Thirteen.

The deepest truths are preserved through the Echoes of Scale and entrusted to the Conclave of Scales.

New Regents may spend decades or centuries gradually learning the full responsibilities associated with the Anchors.

This secrecy exists not to create hierarchy, but to ensure that wisdom accompanies knowledge.

Among Dragonkind, stewardship is considered a greater responsibility than power.

---

## Relationship to the Cataclysm

The Reality Anchors and the Cataclysm are inseparable concepts.

The Cataclysm is the name given to the collapse of an Anchor.

Every known Near-Cataclysm in Dragon history has involved the weakening of one or more Anchors.

For this reason, the health of the Original Thirteen Broods remains a matter of constant concern to the Conclave.

To safeguard the Anchors is to safeguard creation itself.

---

## Legacy

The Reality Anchors are among the oldest systems established within creation.

Long before kingdoms.

Long before civilizations.

Long before history itself.

They endured.

As long as the Original Thirteen Broods survive, Dragonkind believes the Anchors will continue to fulfill the purpose for which they were created:

To ensure that reality remains stable enough for creation to continue becoming.

---

> 
> The Anchors taught it to endure."
