# The Broods

## Overview

The Broods are the great lineages of [[Dragonkind]].

Ancient beyond mortal comprehension, each Brood traces its ancestry back through countless generations to the earliest ages of creation.

To Dragons, a Brood is more than a family.

It is heritage.

Identity.

Tradition.

Every Dragon belongs to a Brood, and the customs, appearance, and culture of that Brood often shape them long before they assume positions of leadership or influence.

Though each Brood possesses its own traditions and ancestral territories, all ultimately recognize themselves as part of the greater family of Dragonkind.

---

## Origins

According to Dragon tradition, the first Broods emerged during the earliest days of creation.

These ancient lineages became known as the Original Thirteen and are regarded as the foundational bloodlines of Dragonkind.

Over countless ages, additional Broods emerged through branching lineages, unique ancestral developments, and the passage of time.

Today, Dragonkind recognizes approximately twenty to twenty-five active Broods.

Though younger Broods command respect, the Original Thirteen remain among the most influential and revered lineages within the [[Wyrmreach]].

---
## The Original Anchors

While Dragonkind recognizes many Broods in the modern age, only thirteen are considered Original Broods.

These lineages trace their ancestry directly to the First Thirteen who emerged during the earliest moments of creation.

According to the oldest traditions preserved by Dragonkind, the Original Thirteen serve as the living Reality Anchors through which creation stabilizes many of its foundational physical systems.

For this reason, the Original Thirteen occupy a unique place within Dragon society.

Their significance extends beyond ancestry, age, or influence.

They are regarded as essential components of reality itself.

Though younger Broods are respected and valued, no additional Reality Anchors have ever emerged.

To this day, the Original Thirteen remain singular within creation.

---

## Bloodline and Identity

A Brood is first and foremost a bloodline.

Dragons inherit their Brood through ancestry rather than personal choice.

Members of a Brood often share physical traits, instincts, and cultural traditions that distinguish them from other lineages.

However, a Dragon is not defined solely by their Brood.

While ancestry shapes identity, it does not determine destiny.

Among Dragons it is often said:

> "A scale remembers its origin, but not its path."

---

## Territories

Most Broods maintain ancestral territories within the [[Wyrmreach]].

These territories range from volcanic regions and mountain ranges to ancient forests, hidden valleys, and coastal expanses.

A Brood's homeland often influences its culture, traditions, and physical characteristics.

Despite these territorial claims, the lands of the Wyrmreach are not nations in the mortal sense.

Borders remain fluid, and Broods frequently travel beyond their ancestral domains.

The homeland of a Brood is considered a place of heritage rather than confinement.

---

## Culture and Tradition

Each Brood possesses its own customs.

Some value patience and wisdom.

Others prize courage, exploration, adaptability, or strength.

Methods of leadership, rites of passage, courtship traditions, and ancestral ceremonies often differ significantly between lineages.

These differences are viewed as a source of strength rather than division.

Many Dragons believe the diversity of the Broods reflects the diversity of creation itself.

---

## First Scales

Each Brood selects a leader known as a First Scale.

The method of selection varies between lineages.

Some choose through trials.

Others through consensus.

Some through ancestral rites observed by the [[Echoes of Scales]].

Regardless of the method, the First Scale serves as the representative and steward of their Brood.

Every First Scale also holds a permanent seat within the [[Conclave of Scales]].

---

## The Original Thirteen

The oldest and most revered lineages are collectively known as the [[Cosmology/Dragonkind/Original Thirteen Broods/The Original Thirteen Broods|Original Thirteen]].

These Broods trace their histories directly to the earliest generations of Dragonkind and have shaped draconic civilization since the dawn of creation.

While not inherently superior to younger lineages, their age and influence grant them considerable respect throughout Dragon society.

The histories of the Original Thirteen are deeply intertwined with the development of the [[Wyrmreach]], the [[Conclave of Scales]], and the traditions that define Dragonkind today.

Unlike younger lineages, the Original Thirteen are believed to possess a direct connection to the earliest moments of creation itself.

Each serves as a Reality Anchor, stabilizing a fundamental physical system necessary for reality's continued existence.

Because of this responsibility, the health of the Original Thirteen has long been monitored by the Conclave of Scales and safeguarded through traditions older than recorded history.

The weakening of an Original Brood is considered a matter of concern not only for Dragonkind, but for creation itself.

---

## Relationship to the Regencies

The Broods and the [[The Thirteen Regencies|Regencies]] are separate institutions.

A Dragon's Brood reflects their ancestry.

A Dragon's Regency reflects the mantle they inherit.

No Brood possesses ownership of a Regency.

Likewise, no Regency belongs exclusively to a single Brood.

Throughout history, Dragons from many different lineages have risen to hold the same Regency.

This distinction is considered one of the greatest strengths of Dragonkind, ensuring that the perspectives of the Regents evolve across generations rather than becoming tied to a single bloodline.

---

## Loyalty

Most Dragons recognize a traditional hierarchy of loyalty:

Creation
↓
Dragonkind
↓
Reality Anchor
↓
Brood
↓
Self

This hierarchy reflects the teachings of the [[Crucible of Creation]] and reinforces the belief that no individual lineage stands above Dragonkind as a whole.

Though rivalries and disagreements exist between Broods, true civil war among Dragons remains exceptionally rare.

---

## Legacy

The Broods serve as the living memory of Dragonkind.

Through them, traditions survive.

Histories endure.

Knowledge passes from one generation to the next.

Though Dragons may wander the Mortal Realm, serve as Regents, or travel beyond the boundaries of the Wyrmreach, they remain forever connected to the lineage from which they came.

For many Dragons, the story of their Brood is inseparable from the story of Dragonkind itself.
