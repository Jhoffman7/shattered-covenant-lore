# The Path of Ascension

**Classification:** Celestial Institution

**Administered By:** [[Throne of Faith]]

**Purpose:** Identification, Education, Evaluation, and Assignment of Saints

---

## Overview

The Path of Ascension is the primary institution through which exceptional mortals are recognized, educated, and prepared for service within the Heavens.

Though commonly associated with the creation of Saints, the Path serves a far broader purpose.

It exists to identify individuals whose actions, character, convictions, and potential mark them as extraordinary.

These individuals are not chosen because they are powerful.

They are chosen because they possess the capacity to serve something greater than themselves.

The Path seeks to determine where that service is best placed.

---

## Origins

The Path of Ascension was established during the early ages of the [[The Celestial Court]].

As the relationship between Heaven and the Mortal Realm matured, the Court recognized a recurring truth.

Exceptional individuals appeared throughout history.

Leaders.

Scholars.

Protectors.

Visionaries.

Martyrs.

Explorers.

These individuals often embodied the ideals represented by the Twelve Thrones.

Rather than allowing such potential to emerge without guidance, the Court created a formal process through which worthy candidates could be recognized and cultivated.

Thus, the Path of Ascension was born.

---

## The Philosophy of the Path

The Path operates according to a simple principle:

> Every soul possesses purpose. Few discover it alone.

Unlike many institutions within Heaven, the Path does not seek obedience.

It seeks alignment.

Its purpose is not to transform individuals into something they are not.

Its purpose is to reveal what they were always capable of becoming.

---

## Selection

Candidates for the Path are identified through numerous means.

Some are recommended by Saints.

Some are discovered by Dragon Regents.

Some are recognized through extraordinary acts of service, sacrifice, leadership, wisdom, faith, or courage.

Others attract the attention of Heaven through circumstances no one fully understands.

No universal standard exists.

The Path is less concerned with accomplishment than potential.

Many candidates arrive possessing little influence or prestige.

---

## The Hall of Calling

At the center of the Path stands the Hall of Calling.

The Hall serves as both academy and place of evaluation.

Here, candidates receive instruction from Saints, scholars, mentors, and representatives of the Twelve Thrones.

The Hall is often described as one of the few places within Heaven where all Twelve Thrones regularly interact without concern for jurisdiction or rivalry.

Its purpose is preparation.

Not recruitment.

---

## The Aptitude Trials

Every candidate eventually undergoes a series of evaluations known collectively as the Aptitude Trials.

The trials are not examinations of strength, intelligence, or magical ability.

They are examinations of character.

Each trial reflects values associated with one of the Twelve Thrones.

Examples include:

### Memory

Can the candidate preserve truth even when truth is painful?

### Revelation

Can the candidate pursue answers without fear?

### Destiny

Can the candidate act despite uncertainty?

### Governance

Can the candidate accept responsibility without recognition?

### Judgment

Can the candidate carry difficult decisions fairly?

### Vigil

Will the candidate protect others at personal cost?

No candidate is expected to excel equally in every trial.

The purpose is discovery.

Not perfection.

---

## Saint Assignment

Upon completion of the Aptitude Trials, candidates undergo a process known as Alignment.

During Alignment, mentors from the Twelve Thrones evaluate where the candidate's talents, virtues, and disposition are most naturally suited.

The resulting assignment determines which Throne the Saint will primarily serve alongside.

The assignment is not a reward.

Nor is it a ranking.

It is a recognition of purpose.

Many candidates find themselves assigned to Thrones they never expected.

Some spend years learning why.

---

## Saints

Successful candidates are formally recognized as Saints.

Contrary to popular belief, Saints do not become members of the Thrones themselves.

Instead, they serve as bridges between Heaven and the Mortal Realm.

Saints remain uniquely positioned to cooperate across institutional boundaries.

This role allows them to operate where the more rigid structures of Heaven often cannot.

For this reason, Saints are highly valued throughout the Celestial Court.

---

## The Role of the Throne of Faith

The [[Throne of Faith]] serves as steward of the Path.

Its responsibility is not to control the process.

Rather, it ensures that candidates are evaluated fairly and guided appropriately.

Faith oversees the development of Saints because it specializes in understanding people.

Other Thrones understand duties.

Faith understands purpose.

This distinction makes it uniquely suited to guiding future Saints toward their calling.

---

## Relationship With The Divine Boundary

Because of [[The Divine Boundary]], the Heavens cannot directly oversee every aspect of mortal civilization.

Saints help bridge this gap.

Operating within the Mortal Realm, they often serve as advisors, protectors, investigators, teachers, diplomats, and champions.

Their existence allows Heaven to influence creation without violating the principles established by the First Twelve.

---

## Relationship With The Dragon Regents

The Path maintains close ties with the [[Dragon Regents]].

Many candidates are first identified through Regent networks.

Likewise, Saints frequently work alongside Regents throughout their service.

While the Thrones shape a Saint's purpose, the Regents often provide opportunities to fulfill it.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

In recent ages, several mentors associated with the Path have expressed concern regarding unusual patterns among new candidates.

Certain individuals have demonstrated unexpected aptitude across multiple Thrones.

Others have displayed affinities that existing frameworks struggle to explain.

Though no official conclusions have been reached, some observers quietly wonder whether the changing nature of reality itself is beginning to influence the Path.

Such discussions remain unofficial.

For now.

---

## Reputation

Throughout both Heaven and the Mortal Realm, the Path of Ascension is regarded with a mixture of reverence and mystery.

To some, it represents opportunity.

To others, responsibility.

To many Saints, it represents the moment their lives changed forever.

---

## Philosophy

> Purpose is not assigned. It is discovered.
