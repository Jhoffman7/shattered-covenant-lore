# Saints

**Classification:** Celestial Servants

**Administered By:** [[The Path of Ascension]]

**Primary Affiliation:** Assigned Throne

---

## Overview

Saints are exceptional mortals recognized by the Heavens for their extraordinary potential, character, and service.

Though often mistaken for priests, prophets, or divine champions, Saints occupy a unique role within creation.

They serve as bridges between the Mortal Realm and the Heavens.

Unlike members of the [[The Celestial Court]], Saints possess no godhead.

Unlike ordinary mortals, Saints have been formally recognized and entrusted with responsibilities that extend beyond the concerns of a single life.

Each Saint serves alongside one of the [[The Twelve Thrones]], acting as an extension of that Throne's ideals within the Mortal Realm.

---

## The Purpose of Saints

The existence of Saints is closely tied to [[The Divine Boundary]].

The First Twelve understood that the Heavens could not directly guide every aspect of mortal civilization without risking harm to both realms.

Yet complete separation was never the goal.

Creation still required guidance.

Wisdom.

Protection.

Stewardship.

Saints exist to fulfill that need.

Through them, Heaven influences the Mortal Realm without violating the balance established by the Primordials.

---

## Becoming a Saint

Saints are not born.

They are recognized.

Potential candidates are identified through the [[The Path of Ascension]].

Many are discovered because of:

- Exceptional service
    
- Extraordinary sacrifice
    
- Unusual wisdom
    
- Remarkable courage
    
- Profound faith
    
- Leadership during crisis
    
- Dedication to others
    

No universal formula exists.

Some Saints begin as kings.

Others begin as farmers.

The Path seeks potential rather than status.

---

## The Path of Ascension

Before receiving recognition as a Saint, every candidate undergoes the Path of Ascension.

During this process, candidates are evaluated, educated, and guided through a series of Aptitude Trials.

The purpose of these trials is not to determine worth.

It is to determine calling.

Upon completion, candidates are assigned to the Throne whose values and responsibilities best align with their character.

## Saints and the Thrones

Saints serve alongside the Thrones.

They do not belong to them.

This distinction is important.

A member of a Throne serves the institution.

A Saint serves creation.

Because of this, Saints often work across institutional boundaries.

They frequently cooperate with multiple Thrones and maintain relationships throughout the Court.

Many institutions value Saints precisely because they can operate where bureaucracy struggles.

---

## Saints and Faith

The [[Throne of Faith]] serves as the primary steward of all Saints.

Faith oversees their education, development, recognition, and guidance.

This responsibility exists because Faith specializes in understanding people.

Where other Thrones understand duties, Faith understands purpose.

The Throne's role is not to control Saints.

It is to help them discover who they are meant to become.

---

## Saints and Lesser Gods

Saints maintain a unique relationship with the gods beneath the Primordials.

Because faith sustains many lesser deities, Saints often act as intermediaries between divine beings and mortal communities.

Their actions can strengthen faith, restore trust, and guide entire populations through periods of uncertainty.

As a result, many lesser gods regard Saints with tremendous respect.

Others regard them with caution.

A Saint's influence can shape the course of entire faiths.

---

## Living Saints

Most Saints spend the majority of their lives within the Mortal Realm.

They serve as:

- Teachers
    
- Advisors
    
- Protectors
    
- Investigators
    
- Diplomats
    
- Leaders
    
- Pilgrims
    
- Champions
    

Some become famous.

Many remain unknown.

Recognition has never been the purpose of sainthood.

---

## Ascended Saints

Upon death, some Saints continue their service within the Heavens.

These individuals are known as Ascended Saints.

Ascended Saints often become:

- Mentors within the Path of Ascension
    
- Advisors to the Thrones
    
- Custodians of sacred knowledge
    
- Guides for future generations
    

Not every Saint ascends.

The process remains poorly understood even among the Celestial Court.

---

## The Covenant Crisis

Recent generations have produced unusual developments among Saint candidates.

Some demonstrate strong affinities for multiple Thrones.

Others exhibit talents that do not fit established patterns.

Several mentors within the Path of Ascension have privately expressed concern that traditional methods of assignment are becoming less reliable.

No official explanation has been found.

Yet the trend continues.

Many quietly suspect that broader changes within creation may be influencing the emergence of new Saints.

---

## Reputation

Throughout the Mortal Realm, Saints are regarded with a mixture of admiration, reverence, and curiosity.

Some become legends.

Some become leaders.

Some become martyrs.

Most become examples.

Regardless of their path, Saints are remembered not for the power they possessed.

But for the lives they changed.
