# Knowledge Classification

## Purpose

This document tracks the intended knowledge level of articles within The Shattered Covenant.

The purpose of this system is not to hide lore.

The purpose is to determine:

- What an average person knows.
    
- What educated individuals know.
    
- What specialists know.
    
- What secrets must be discovered through play.
    
- What information remains exclusively for the Game Master.
    

As the campaign progresses, articles may move between categories as players uncover new truths.

---

# Knowledge Tiers

## Common Knowledge

Information known by most educated people throughout the world.

Examples include:

- Major nations
    
- Dragonkind
    
- The Celestial Court
    
- Dragonborn
    
- The Original Thirteen Broods
    
- Major historical events
    

Players should begin with access to these articles.

---

## Educated Knowledge

Information known by scholars, clergy, nobility, experienced adventurers, and specialists.

Examples include:

- The Thirteen Regencies
    
- The Conclave of Scales
    
- Wyrmreach
    
- The Divine Boundary
    
- The Great Cycle
    

Players may gain access through study, travel, faction membership, or interaction with knowledgeable NPCs.

---

## Restricted Knowledge

Information known only by specific organizations, religious authorities, Regents, Saints, senior scholars, or similar individuals.

Examples include:

- The Echoes of Scales
    
- The Cradle of Scales
    
- Certain Primordial doctrines
    
- Internal Conclave matters
    

Players should generally discover these through significant investigation or direct involvement.

---

## Secret Knowledge

Information intentionally hidden from the wider world.

Examples include:

- The Reality Anchors
    
- The Cataclysm
    
- Certain truths regarding the First Regents
    
- Certain secrets of the Primordials
    
- Hidden history surrounding the Great Divide
    

Players should only gain access through major revelations and campaign milestones.

---

## GM Knowledge

Information not intended for player access.

Examples include:

- Future campaign developments
    
- Hidden antagonist plans
    
- Unrevealed cosmological truths
    
- Campaign-specific mysteries
    

These articles remain permanently hidden unless intentionally revealed by the GM.

# Notes

Knowledge classifications are guidelines rather than absolute rules.

Different cultures, organizations, and individuals may possess knowledge outside their normal classification.

The classification reflects what a typical person within the setting would reasonably know.
