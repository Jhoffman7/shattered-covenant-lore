# The Chronicle of Near-Cataclysms

## Overview

The Chronicle of Near-Cataclysms serves as Dragonkind's master record concerning all known reality-threatening incidents that approached Cataclysmic conditions.

Maintained by the Conclave of Scales and entrusted to select Regents and custodians, the Chronicle exists to preserve institutional memory regarding threats capable of destabilizing the Reality Anchors.

Only five Near-Cataclysms have been confirmed throughout recorded history.

The fact that creation survived all five is considered one of the greatest successes in Dragon history.

# Observed Pattern

Analysis of the recorded Near-Cataclysms suggests that each event threatened a fundamental principle necessary for the continued stability of creation.

Known examples include:

| Near-Cataclysm | Threatened Principle |
|---------------|---------------|
| The Sundering Dawn | Stewardship / Stability |
| The Scourge of Ash | Renewal |
| The Third Recorded Near-Cataclysm | Unknown |
| The Silence Below | Memory |
| The Fifth Recorded Near-Cataclysm | Unknown |

Whether this pattern reflects the influence of the Reality Anchors or a deeper structure within creation remains a matter of debate among Dragon scholars.

# First Recorded Near-Cataclysm

## Classification

Near-Cataclysm I

## Known Name

The Sundering Dawn

## Era

Age of the First Regents

## Record Status

Documented

## Summary

The first confirmed Near-Cataclysm and the oldest reality-threatening event preserved within Dragon records.

Unlike later Near-Cataclysms, the Sundering Dawn was not caused by an external force, foreign entity, or Reality Anchor anomaly.

It was caused by the First Regents themselves.

Shortly after their emergence from the Cradle of Scales, the First Regents entered into a period of escalating conflict driven by differing understandings of their purpose within creation.

As their struggles intensified, reality itself began destabilizing beneath the weight of their power.

The crisis ultimately ended when the First Regents recognized that they were not separate from creation, but intrinsically connected to it.

Many Dragon scholars regard the Sundering Dawn as the event that gave rise to the stewardship philosophy that would later define Dragon civilization.

## Known Facts

- Occurred during the Age of the First Regents.
- Predates the Elder Broods as formal institutions.
- Predates the Conclave of Scales.
- Represents the First Recorded Near-Cataclysm.
- Was caused by conflict between the First Regents.
- Nearly destabilized multiple foundational systems of reality.
- Led directly to the earliest concepts of stewardship.
- Successfully resolved.

## Unknowns

- The precise sequence of events.
- The full extent of the damage inflicted upon newborn creation.
- Whether any Reality Anchors suffered permanent consequences.
- The exact circumstances that ended the conflict.

# Known Patterns

Analysis of all recorded Near-Cataclysms reveals several recurring characteristics.

## Reality Anchor Involvement

Every confirmed Near-Cataclysm involved the destabilization or potential destabilization of at least one Reality Anchor.

## Escalation

Each event began as a localized anomaly before expanding into a broader threat.

## Institutional Response

Containment required cooperation beyond a single Brood, Regency, or generation.

## Information Suppression

Following containment, significant portions of each incident were classified or sealed to prevent misuse of the knowledge involved.

# Conclave Assessment

Current assessment indicates that the probability of future Near-Cataclysms cannot be reduced to zero.

Continued vigilance remains necessary.

The prevention of a Sixth Recorded Near-Cataclysm remains one of the highest priorities of the Conclave of Scales.
