# Unresolved Mysteries

This document contains the major unanswered questions of the setting.

Some mysteries are publicly debated by scholars, theologians, and rulers.

Others are hidden truths known only to a handful of individuals.

Many of these questions may never be fully answered.

Others may become central to future campaigns.

The defining mystery of the current age.

At some point in history, one of the First Twelve disappeared.

The identity of the missing Primordial has been obscured.

Records have been altered.

Memories conflict.

Even the gods refuse to speak openly about the event.
