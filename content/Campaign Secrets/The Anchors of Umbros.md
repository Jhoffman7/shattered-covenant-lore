# The Anchors of Umbros

**Status:** Secret Lore

The Anchors of Umbros are among the oldest relics in existence.

Created during the forging of the [[The Original Covenant|Original Covenant]], each Anchor contains a fragment of [[Umbros]] that was separated from him in order to sustain the burden of the Covenant.

Most scholars know nothing of their true nature.

Many believe them to be divine relics.

Others believe they are lost artifacts from the Age Before Ages.

Only a handful of beings understand the truth.

The Anchors are not merely relics.

They are pieces of Umbros himself.

---

## The Purpose Of The Anchors

When Umbros agreed to become the anchor of the Covenant, the burden proved too great for a single being to contain.

To preserve both the Covenant and his own existence, portions of his essence were separated and transformed into sacred vessels.

Each vessel contained an aspect of what made Umbros who he was.

His memories.

His hopes.

His doubts.

His promises.

His identity.

His love.

Together, these fragments became known as the Anchors.

---

## The Scattering

Following the signing of the Covenant, the Anchors were hidden throughout creation.

Some were entrusted to divine guardians.

Others were concealed within forgotten places.

Several vanished from history entirely.

Over time their origins were forgotten.

Entire civilizations rose and fell around them without understanding what they truly were.

---

## The Nature Of The Anchors

The Anchors are not inert objects.

Each contains a fragment of Umbros' consciousness.

A memory.

An emotion.

A belief.

A piece of identity preserved across the ages.

Those who encounter an Anchor often experience:

- Visions
    
- Dreams
    
- Memories that are not their own
    
- Fragments of ancient conversations
    
- Echoes of forgotten history
    

The closer the Anchors are brought together, the stronger these manifestations become.

---

## The Twelve Anchors

Ancient records suggest twelve Anchors were created.

When Umbros accepted the burden of the Covenant, portions of his essence were entrusted to each of the First Twelve.

Each Anchor preserved a different aspect of what made Umbros who he was.

Together, these fragments ensured that no single being could ever possess the entirety of Umbros.

Nor could any single aspect of him be completely forgotten.

The Twelve Anchors were never intended to remain separated forever.

They were entrusted to the First Twelve so that Umbros could someday be restored when the Covenant fulfilled its purpose.

Over time, the Anchors became scattered throughout creation.

Their custodians changed.

Their purpose faded from memory.

The Anchors endured.

---

### The Anchor of Love

**Original Custodian:** [[Sol'Vara]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' capacity for love, devotion, and emotional connection.

Many believe this Anchor contains memories older than creation itself.

---

### The Anchor of Identity

**Original Custodian:** [[Xyros, The Final Turning]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' sense of self.

His name.

His purpose.

His understanding of who he truly is.

---

### The Anchor of Memory

**Original Custodian:** [[Aion]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' memories.

This Anchor remembers the world before the Covenant.

---

### The Anchor of Promise

**Original Custodian:** [[Velora]]

The fragment preserving the original promise upon which the Covenant was founded.

Many believe it contains the clearest record of what was originally agreed.

---

### The Anchor of Duty

**Original Custodian:** [[Tharos]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' willingness to bear responsibility for the sake of others.

This Anchor embodies sacrifice through obligation.

---

### The Anchor of Truth

**Original Custodian:** [[Elyndra]]

The fragment preserving truths that cannot be altered by time, memory, or belief.

---

### The Anchor of Choice

**Original Custodian:** [[Vaelor]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' belief that every being deserves the freedom to choose its own path.

---

### The Anchor of Hope

**Original Custodian:** [[Morwyn]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' belief that tomorrow can become better than today.

Many are surprised such a fragment exists.

---

### The Anchor of Resolve

**Original Custodian:** [[Kael'Thur]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' determination to endure suffering without surrendering to it.

---

### The Anchor of Change

**Original Custodian:** [[Thaloris]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' acceptance that all things must eventually transform.

---

### The Anchor of Compassion

**Original Custodian:** [[Ilyria]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' capacity for mercy, empathy, and understanding.

---

### The Anchor of Acceptance

**Original Custodian:** [[Nethrys]]

The fragment preserving Umbros' acceptance of endings and the necessity of letting go.
---

## Reforging Umbros

The Anchors were never intended to remain separated forever.

Originally they would have been restored when the Covenant fulfilled its purpose.

As the promise faded from memory, this restoration never occurred.

As a result, Umbros remained fragmented.

Incomplete.

Enduring.

Waiting.

---

## The Modern Age

Few beings understand what the Anchors truly are.

Several are believed lost.

Some may be hidden beneath kingdoms.

Others may rest within the vaults of Heaven.

A handful may have become legendary relics worshipped for reasons entirely unrelated to their original purpose.

Yet as the Covenant weakens, the Anchors have begun to stir.

Dreams spread.

Visions intensify.

Ancient secrets resurface.

The fragments of Umbros are beginning to call to one another.

---

## The Final Purpose

The Anchors were never created to imprison Umbros.

They were created to preserve him.

When reunited, they will not grant power.

They will restore wholeness.

The purpose of the Anchors is not to free Umbros.

The purpose of the Anchors is to allow Umbros to become himself again.

---
## Known Fragments

Though the exact nature and appearance of the Anchors remains unknown, surviving records suggest each preserves a different aspect of Umbros.

The most commonly referenced fragments are:

- Memory
    
- Identity
    
- Hope
    
- Doubt
    
- Promise
    
- Love
    
- Sacrifice
    

Whether these names are literal, symbolic, or incomplete remains a matter of debate.

Many scholars believe the true nature of each Anchor has been lost to time.
