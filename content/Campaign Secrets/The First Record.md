# The First Record

## Overview

The First Record is the oldest document in existence.

Preserved within the deepest vaults of the [[Throne of Memory]], it predates every known archive system, historical classification method, and celestial catalog.

No one knows who originally created it.

No one knows its true purpose.

No one agrees on what it contains.

Throughout history, countless scholars, archivists, prophets, judges, saints, and celestial officials have studied the document.

No two descriptions have ever perfectly matched.

For most of Heaven's history, the First Record has been regarded as a sacred mystery.

An ancient relic whose meaning was lost long ago.

In reality, the First Record is the original and unaltered text of [[The Original Covenant]].

It was never destroyed.

It was never hidden.

It was never forgotten.

Its true nature was simply obscured.

---

## The Great Safeguard

The First Record possesses a unique property unlike any other artifact in existence.

The document reveals a different interpretation of itself to every reader.

Each observer perceives something slightly different.

A judge may see law.

A scholar may see philosophy.

A prophet may see prophecy.

An archivist may see history.

A ruler may see governance.

Every interpretation appears authentic.

Every interpretation is internally consistent.

Yet no two readings perfectly agree.

This effect is not an illusion.

Nor is it a deception.

The document reveals only the truth the reader is capable of understanding.

---

## Purpose

The First Record was intentionally designed to prevent premature discovery of the Covenant's ultimate purpose.

Reality was not meant to remain beneath the Covenant forever.

Neither was it meant to abandon it too soon.

To safeguard this balance, the Covenant was bound to a living truth.

The document itself.

Rather than concealing information through force, the Record conceals understanding through perspective.

Until creation reaches the point it was intended to reach, the complete truth remains inaccessible.

The Record cannot be deciphered.

Only outgrown.

---

## The Missing Name

One of the oldest mysteries surrounding the First Record concerns its signatures.

Throughout history, scholars have reported wildly conflicting observations.

Some describe twelve signatures.

Others describe seven.

Some claim no signatures exist at all.

Several reports reference unknown symbols that cannot be translated.

Many historians dismiss these accounts as errors caused by the document's unusual nature.

They are mistaken.

Every observer sees only a fragment of the truth.

No observer has ever seen the complete list.

Not even the Throne of Memory.

---

## The Contradictions

Over countless ages, archivists began noticing strange patterns surrounding the First Record.

References appearing in one analysis but absent from another.

Descriptions contradicting one another.

Conclusions that could not simultaneously be true.

The deeper scholars investigated, the more questions emerged.

Eventually a disturbing realization formed.

Nearly every major contradiction within Heaven's historical record could be traced back to the First Record.

This discovery gave rise to the secretive faction within the Throne of Memory known as the Witnesses.

Though they possess no definitive answers, they believe the Record sits at the center of a mystery far older than the Celestial Court itself.

---

## The Truth

The First Record is the final surviving witness to the original agreement forged between the First Thirteen.

It remembers what Heaven forgot.

It remembers what history erased.

It remembers [[Xyros, The Final Turning]].

The document has never forgotten his existence.

Reality has.

---

## The Endgame Revelation

Near the conclusion of the campaign, the conditions protecting the First Record begin to fail.

The Anchors have been gathered.

Umbros has been restored.

The Forgotten Promise has been uncovered.

The truth hidden throughout history has begun returning.

For the first time since the Covenant's creation:

The First Record stops changing.

The text stabilizes.

Every observer sees the same words.

Every contradiction disappears.

The mystery ends.

The truth remains.

For the first time in history, Heaven reads the Covenant as it was originally written.

And discovers a name that should not exist.

A thirteenth signature.

A forgotten Primordial.

A promise that was never fulfilled.

---

## Why The Record Exists

The First Record is not a lock.

It is not a prison.

It is not a test.

It is a safeguard.

A final act of wisdom created to ensure that the truth would reveal itself only when creation was ready to understand it.

The Record never hid the truth.

The truth simply waited.
