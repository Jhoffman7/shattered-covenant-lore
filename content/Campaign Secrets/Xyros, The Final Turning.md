# Xyros, The Final Turning

**Status:** Secret Lore

**Title:** The Final Turning

**Domain:** Becoming, Transformation, Possibility, Renewal

Xyros is the Thirteenth Primordial.

Though nearly all memory of him has vanished from the modern world, he was once counted among the First Thirteen who shaped reality during the Age Before Ages.

His existence is the foundation upon which the Covenant was built.

His absence is the reason the world has remained unchanged for so long.

---

## The Nature Of Xyros

Many who learn of Xyros assume he represents destruction.

This is incorrect.

Xyros does not embody ruin.

He does not embody chaos.

He does not embody death.

Xyros embodies Becoming.

The natural progression of all things into something new.

The transformation of kingdoms.

The evolution of civilizations.

The growth of individuals.

The passing of one age into another.

Nothing remains unchanged beneath his influence.

Not because he destroys.

Because he allows things to become.

---

## The Final Turning

Among the First Thirteen, Xyros represented a truth that many found difficult to accept.

Nothing lasts forever.

Not kingdoms.

Not dragons.

Not gods.

Not even the systems created to preserve them.

Every structure eventually reaches the end of its purpose.

Every age eventually gives way to another.

Every story eventually becomes the foundation of a new story.

This process became known as the Final Turning.

---

## Why The Others Feared Him

The fear surrounding Xyros was never fear of malice.

It was fear of uncertainty.

The influence of Xyros ensured that no institution remained permanent.

Civilizations changed.

Traditions evolved.

Power shifted.

The future refused to remain predictable.

Many among the First Twelve viewed this as dangerous.

Others viewed it as necessary.

The debate surrounding Xyros ultimately led to the creation of the Covenant.

---

## The Covenant

Contrary to later belief, Xyros did not oppose the Covenant.

He did not resist it.

He did not fight against it.

He agreed to it.

Xyros understood the concerns of his siblings.

Reality was young.

Civilization was fragile.

History had not yet established roots.

He accepted the Covenant because he believed its purpose was temporary.

Like Umbros, he trusted the promise.

When the appointed time arrived, the Covenant would end.

The Turning would continue.

The future would move forward.

---

## The Long Silence

As millennia passed, Xyros remained beyond the Covenant.

History forgot him.

The world forgot him.

The promise forgotten by Heaven was never forgotten by Xyros.

He continued to wait.

Not because he desired freedom.

Not because he sought revenge.

Because he had given his word.

The agreement would end when its purpose had been fulfilled.

He trusted that the others would remember.

---

## Misunderstood By History

Modern religions that preserve fragments of Xyros often portray him incorrectly.

Some describe him as a destroyer.

Others as a devourer of civilizations.

Others as a force of apocalypse.

These beliefs emerged long after the original purpose of the Covenant had been forgotten.

They reflect fear.

Not truth.

---

## What Xyros Wants

Perhaps the greatest misunderstanding concerns Xyros' desires.

Many assume he seeks release.

Many assume he seeks justice.

Many assume he seeks vengeance.

None are true.

Xyros seeks nothing.

He demands nothing.

He claims nothing.

His purpose has never been to overthrow the world.

His purpose has never been to punish those who forgot him.

His purpose is simply to allow reality to continue becoming what it was always meant to become.

The future does not seek permission to arrive.

The future arrives regardless.

---

## The Age Beyond The Covenant

The future seen by Aion was not a future destroyed by Xyros.

It was a future shaped by him.

A future in which:

- Mortals increasingly govern themselves.
    
- Dragon Regents lose their unquestioned authority.
    
- New forms of magic emerge.
    
- New Saints arise.
    
- The influence of Heaven gradually changes.
    
- The world enters an age unlike any that came before.
    

This future frightened many.

Others viewed it as the next natural chapter of existence.

---

## The First Question

When the Covenant finally ends and Xyros is restored to reality, many expect anger.

Many expect judgment.

Many expect war.

They misunderstand him.

For thousands of years, one question remained unanswered.

One promise remained unfulfilled.

One burden remained unlifted.

The first concern of Xyros is not the Covenant.

It is not the world.

It is not Heaven.

It is Umbros.

---

## The Truth

Xyros is not the villain of the story.

Nor is he its hero.

He is the future.

Patient.

Unyielding.

Inevitable.

He is the Turning that every age must eventually face.

He is the reminder that no system, no kingdom, and no certainty lasts forever.

And he is the promise that after every ending, something new may begin.
