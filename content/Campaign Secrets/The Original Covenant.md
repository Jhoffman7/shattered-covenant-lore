# The Original Covenant

**Status:** Secret Lore

The Original Covenant was the agreement forged between the First Twelve during the Age Before Ages.

Modern theology teaches that the Divine Covenant was created to establish order between Heaven, Dragons, and Mortals.

This is only partially true.

The Original Covenant had a different purpose.

---

## The Age Before Ages

Before the Covenant, reality existed in a state of constant transformation.

Kingdoms rose and fell within generations.

Magic evolved unpredictably.

Saints appeared frequently.

The gods intervened directly in mortal affairs.

Dragons warred openly.

The world was vibrant, creative, and alive.

It was also unstable.

Many among the First Twelve feared that civilization would never survive long enough to establish lasting foundations.

---

## The Great Debate

The First Twelve became divided.

### Those Who Favored Stability

- Tharos
    
- Aion
    
- Elyndra
    
- Nethrys
    

These Primordials believed reality required structure, continuity, and permanence.

---

### Those Who Favored Growth

- Vaelor
    
- Kael'Thur
    
- Thaloris
    
- Morwyn
    

These Primordials argued that change, struggle, and transformation were necessary for the world to flourish.

---

### Those Who Sought Balance

- Sol'Vara
    
- Umbros
    
- Velora
    
- Ilyria
    

These Primordials sought a path between the two extremes.

---

The debate lasted centuries.

No side could persuade the others.

---

## The Problem Of Xyros

At the center of the debate stood Xyros.

The Thirteenth Primordial.

The embodiment of Becoming.

Xyros was not feared because he was evil.

He was feared because his influence ensured that nothing remained unchanged forever.

Civilizations transformed.

Kingdoms evolved.

Institutions eventually gave way to something new.

Even the gods themselves would one day be changed by his influence.

Many viewed this as necessary.

Others viewed it as dangerous.

---

## Aion's Proposal

Aion eventually proposed a compromise.

Rather than destroying Xyros or opposing him outright, the First Twelve would temporarily restrain his influence.

Reality would be given time to mature.

Civilizations would be allowed to establish deep roots.

History would be allowed to accumulate.

The world would be granted stability.

For a time.

---

## The Agreement

The Covenant was never intended to be permanent.

The original agreement stated that when civilization had grown strong enough to endure the Turning, the Covenant would end.

Xyros would be released.

Reality would continue its natural progression.

The agreement was accepted by all parties.

Including Xyros himself.

---

## The Promise

The Covenant was founded upon a promise.

Not imprisonment.

Not punishment.

A promise.

The burden would be temporary.

When the appointed time arrived, the Covenant would end.

The burden would be lifted.

The world would move forward.

---
## The Preservation Of Umbros

The burden required by the Covenant proved too great for a single being to bear without consequence.

To preserve both Umbros and the Covenant itself, portions of his essence were separated and entrusted to the First Twelve.

Each Primordial became custodian of a different aspect of what made Umbros who he was.

These fragments would later become known as the Twelve Anchors.

The Anchors were never intended to imprison Umbros.

They were intended to preserve him until the Covenant fulfilled its purpose and he could be restored.
